# Papers are agreed in the app with a one-time code

Every paper an Investor signed meant meeting the Owner: the stamped Investment Agreement and each Amendment, the
মনোনয়নপত্র, the Portal Consent. The money waited on the stamped paper being signed and photographed. In-app agreeing
for an Agreement was built on 2026-10-03 behind a switch, off until the lawyer and the Shariah scholar confirmed the
Stamp Act's admissibility. On 2026-10-08 the Owner asked to avoid the signing step, then chose not to ask the advisers
("go with your best recommendation"). The map is `.scratch/openfarm-sign-in-the-app/`; the research is in
`docs/research/agreeing-in-the-app-bangladeshi-law.md`, `agreeing-in-the-app-in-shariah.md` and
`nomination-without-paper.md`.

**One meeting to join, everything after it in the app.** At the first meeting the Owner checks the NID and hands over the
portal code, and the Investor signs the Portal Consent on paper. After it, the Investment Agreement, every Amendment and
the মনোনয়নপত্র are agreed in the portal — except a মনোনয়নপত্র naming a minor, which stays on paper. Stamped paper stays
available for every paper.

- **Sealed by a one-time code**, sent by SMS to the Investor's phone and by email where they have a confirmed one;
  entering either seals the agreement. The Portal Consent gains a clause agreeing to this procedure, which is what lets
  the farm attribute the code to the Investor (ICT Act 2006 s.13(3)(ক)).
- **The Investor may withdraw** an agreement until the Owner approves it: with the Owner's approval last, their
  agreement is the offer (AAOIFI SS 38 5/3).
- **The farm keeps its proof**: who, when, which channel and destination, the device's address and the agreed paper's
  fingerprint, and a confirmation sent once approved. The Evidence Act presumes nothing about who sent a message
  (s.88A), so this record is the farm's whole case.

**Accepted risk, the Owner's choice:** an Agreement or Amendment agreed in the app carries no stamp, because no rule in
force lets stamp duty be paid onto an electronic instrument. Before a court or the Arbitrator it is inadmissible until the
duty and ten times it are paid (Stamp Act s.35 — Tk 3,300 at Tk 300 duty), and executing it unstamped is an offense
fined up to Tk 500 (s.62). The contract itself is valid (Contract Act ss.3–4, 10; ICT Act s.6) and, in Shariah, valid in
principle (Islamic Fiqh Academy Res. 52 and 230; AAOIFI SS 38). The advisers approved stamped paper in September and were
not asked about this.

**Consequences:** the Portal Consent and the Agreement Offer change, a মনোনয়নপত্র becomes offerable, Investors gain an
optional confirmed email, and the farm gains an email sender beside its SMS gateway. The existing in-app switch is the
Owner's to turn on.

**Revisit** when stamp duty can be paid by A-challan (reported for July 2027): pay it at agreement time, or if a dispute
ever turns on an Agreement agreed in the app.
