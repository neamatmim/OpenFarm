# Some money moves the cash and is never income or cost

Accepted, 2026-10-13. The Owner chose option A of `.scratch/openfarm-flow-survey-2026-10-13/e3-e6-decisions.md`.

Every hand-entered Money Event used to be income or a cost, and money going out by hand that is not a **Herd Cost** is
an **Overhead**. That is right for rent and wages and wrong for five kinds of money a working farm moves: the Owner
putting her own money in or taking some home, a loan arriving and its principal being paid back, and a machine or a
building that lasts for years. Entered as costs, a ৳3 lakh chopper made one month look ৳3 lakh worse and the next
eleven better; a bank EMI counted the principal as an expense, which no accountant would; a loan received looked like
income; and the Owner's own money either went unentered or drove a hand's cash below nothing.

Five standard Categories are now **Outside the Results**: _Owner's money in_, _Owner's drawings_, _Loan received_,
_Loan principal repaid_ and _Machine or building bought_. They are entered by hand like any other, in or out, and move a
hand's cash or a Farm Account as any Money Event does, so the **Cash Position**, the month's cash flow, the cash
figures ("cash in less out" / «নগদ আসা-যাওয়ার ফারাক») and the **Bank Check** all count them. No result does: they are
no Overhead, reach no Side Result, are charged to no animal (the Owner cannot mark them so), and stay out of Costs by
Side, margins, Return on Cost and the accountant's income and expense. The accountant's summary and the Monthly Report
list them apart, by Category, with the whole of the cash beneath, so the books can still be tied to the hands and the
statements.

A loan's interest is a cost of running the place, as rent is: a sixth standard Category, _Loan interest_, is an
ordinary Overhead.

The app does not depreciate. A machine's cost reaches no result in the app; the accountant spreads it over its life in
the books if they wish. That can be added later (option A+) if the accountant asks for it in the app.

The five are a fixed list of standard Categories, keyed, rather than a flag the Owner sets on any Category: the
accountant's headings should not be chosen month by month, and a mark set wrongly would quietly move money in or out of
every result. A farm's own Category is always inside the results. Old money entered under a farm's own Category for a
loan or a machine stays where it was entered; it can be put right as any entry can.
