# A Venture's terms may change until somebody is bound by them

A Venture could not be edited. A wrong figure, or a decide-by day gone past on a Venture nobody had joined, could only be put right by calling it off and opening another. On 2026-11-08 the Owner asked for a way to change them, and chose the narrow one.

- **While it is Open and nobody is bound.** Nobody has signed for a Unit, the Farm's own Units included, and no **Agreement Offer** is out. An offer counts because its paper was laid out from these terms and an Investor may be reading it; the Owner withdraws it first.
- **Every term it opened on**, worked and refused as opening it is, so the Floor, Units, Cattle Part and Monthly Sums cannot come to something a new Venture would not be allowed.
- **Written over the row, with no reason asked.** Nobody has agreed to anything yet, so there is nothing to answer for; the Audit Event keeps what it said before.
- **After the first signature, nothing changes here.** The terms are what somebody signed, and an **Amendment** moves only the split and the **Target Window**.

**Consequences:**

- `ventures.changeTerms` beside `ventures.open`, sharing `settledTerms`. Asked behind the Farm lock that signing and offering take, so a signature cannot land between the check and the write.
- A **Request to Join** does not stop a change: it binds nobody, and the Investor still reads the terms again before signing.
- A Venture shown in the **Investor Portal** may still change, and whoever can see it reads the new terms. The Owner decides whether to tell anybody.
- A **Venture Plan** is left as it is. A plan over its budget is said and never refused, which is also how a changed budget shows.
