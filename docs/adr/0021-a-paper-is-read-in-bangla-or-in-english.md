# A paper is read in Bangla or in English

Until now every Investor paper was Bangla first. The Agreement, the Amendment and the মনোনয়নপত্র printed the English smaller beneath each line. The Portal Consent and «আপনার তথ্য» printed only their title's English, because "the Investor is handed the Bangla". The joining letter, the progress statement and the settlement statement were text with labels in both languages and everything else in Bangla. Values, units and dates came out in Bangla, or in whichever language the Owner happened to use. On 2026-10-08 the Owner found papers with only one language on them and asked for every paper to be properly in both, with a switch.

**Every Investor paper is read in one language at a time, Bangla or English, chosen by whoever reads or prints it.** The Owner decided three things:

- **Two choices, never both on one paper.** A switch on every paper says বাংলা or English. What is on the screen is what prints.
- **Any paper may be printed in English only, signed papers included.** The Agreement, the Amendment, the মনোনয়নপত্র and the Portal Consent may be signed in English. No line saying which language governs is added.
- **The Investor papers are in scope:** the Agreement, the Amendment, the মনোনয়নপত্র, the Portal Consent, «আপনার তথ্য», the Data Copy, the joining letter, the progress and settlement statements, and the Welcome Letter. Papers for the farm and for the authorities are not: the transport card and the DLS letter are an authority's forms and stay in Bangla.

**Consequences:**

- A paper carries both languages to the screen, and the screen draws one. Every value a paper writes — money, days, dates, shares, the letterhead, the date it was made — is said in each language with its own numerals. A paper's English left empty by the Owner is read in Bangla, so nothing goes blank.
- The rule that a paper an Investor is handed in Bangla prints only its title's English (`englishPrinted`) goes. Its English was always written; it is now printed when English is chosen.
- A paper already kept as offered in the app keeps its words. Where it holds a value in one language only, both readings show that value.
- The joining letter and the two statements are drawn like the Agreement — a letterhead, headed parts, tables — rather than as lines of text.

**Known risk, accepted by the Owner:** the lawyer and the Shariah scholar approved the Bangla wording (2026-09-26, 2026-10-02). The English is OpenFarm's translation, and nobody has said it may be signed on its own. If the two ever disagree, nothing on the paper says which one governs.

**Revisit** if the lawyer asks for a governing-language line, or if a paper must be signed in both languages side by side.

**2026-10-09:** the farm's own papers and the authorities' are read the same way. The Owner asked for every printable paper to look and work alike, and chose one language at a time, with the switch, over the Release 1 rule that compliance papers carry English labels alongside the Bangla. This covers the Inspector View's six registers, the milk dispatch record, the accountant's summary, the sale receipt, the transport card, the animal passport, the withdrawal summary and the DLS letter. Each is laid out on the letterhead like the Investor papers, and an inspector or processor who reads English is handed the English. The transport card and the DLS letter no longer stay in Bangla only.
