# Monthly Sums may be paid early, with allocation per Agreement

Date: 2026-10-11
Status: Accepted (Owner's instruction)

An Investor may send more than their Cattle Part before buying starts. Refusing the extra prevents the farm from recording the actual bank receipt. Counting it as cattle money instead could hide another Investor's missing Cattle Part and spend money meant to keep the animals.

Accept confirmed bank receipts up to the Agreement's whole signed price while the Venture is Open, Buying or Fattening. Keep the existing cutoff when selling starts, signed Units, profit split, due dates and whole-taka schedule. Allocate each Agreement's received capital, less capital returned, to its own Cattle Part first and then Monthly Sums oldest first. Part of a sum may be paid. A future sum already paid is not asked for again.

For example, a 58,000-taka Agreement has a 40,000-taka Cattle Part and nine Monthly Sums of 2,000. Receiving 45,000 pays the Cattle Part, clears two sums and pays 1,000 toward the third. The balance is 13,000. The third sum still asks for 1,000 on its original date. The 5,000 of Monthly Sums enters the Running Budget, including before buying starts. It cannot cover anybody else's unpaid Cattle Part, satisfy the cattle readiness check, or increase a Buying Float's limit.

Use the existing Venture Movement ledger rather than a second wallet or independently editable allocation. Derive current balances, historical budgets and corrections from its scoped receipts and refunds. Record the actual bank date and reference. Farm locking serializes financial changes; re-read the Venture and Agreement under that lock, enforce the signed cap, and reject repeated references. The audit event for a confirmed receipt keeps its before/after allocation as observed when recorded.

An Investor's Pay-in Note remains an unverified claim. Pending notes reserve room for additional notes but never change received capital, budgets or the schedule. The Owner checks the bank statement, records the actual receipt and answers the note. A note is not a receipt. Money beyond the whole signed price is refused; resolve an accidental excess with a separate bank refund rather than inventing Units or silently increasing the contract.

Show confirmed allocation and remaining sums in the portal even after full payment. Show the Owner how a proposed receipt divides before recording it. The printable joining acknowledgment shows bank receipts, confirmed allocation and paid/remaining sums. Pending claims are excluded.

This changes operational receipt handling. It does not rewrite frozen agreements or claim a new legal or Shariah approval for their wording. The previously approved monthly terms and the farm's existing approval switches remain in force.
