# Invited Investors see the Ventures still raising capital and how to pay; the portal still takes no money

ADR 0007 made the portal read-only: "nothing is taken, paid, signed or offered through it". On 2026-09-25 the Owner asked for more: show upcoming Ventures with a way to join, and take payments in the portal. Three questions went to the lawyer, on the portal map's ticket [Bring the portal as built to the lawyer](../../.scratch/openfarm-investor-portal/issues/07-bring-the-portal-to-the-lawyer.md) and on ticket 11.

The lawyer answered the same day, **verbally, with a written opinion to follow**. They said three things are acceptable, **because the portal stays by invitation only**:

- the portal as built;
- showing a Venture still gathering capital to the farm's invited Investors, and letting them say they want to join;
- showing the Venture Account's bank details and the reference to use, for capital an Investor has signed for.

**Taking payment inside the portal was not approved.** That covers card, mobile financial services, a gateway, and the Bangladesh Bank / PSS Act question. It stays out.

So the portal may now **show** an upcoming Venture to invited Investors and **take their word** that they want to join. It may **tell** them where to pay. It still **takes no money and signs nothing**:

- Joining ends in the Owner signing a stamped Investment Agreement with them, as now.
- Capital still arrives by bank into the Venture Account, and the Owner records it.
- No capital is recorded without a stamped Agreement. That guard is unchanged.
- Everything that kept the circle closed still holds: only invited Investors see an upcoming Venture, nothing is public, and there are no referrals, no advertising and no projections of return.

**Consequences:**
- ADR 0007's "nothing offered" gives way to this for invited Investors. Its "nothing taken, paid or signed" stands.
- CONTEXT.md's **Investor Portal** entry changes to match.
- The shape of joining is to be designed, not assumed. What an Investor sees, what saying "I want to join" records, and what the Owner is told are all open questions.

**Revisit** when the written opinion arrives. If it differs from what was said, this ADR follows the writing. Until it arrives, the portal stays switched off for real Investors, as the portal map already has it.
