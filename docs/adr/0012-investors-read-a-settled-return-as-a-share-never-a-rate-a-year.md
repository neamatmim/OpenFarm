# Investors read a settled return as a share over the run, never as a rate a year

The Owner reads each Venture's **Return on Capital** as a share, its days and a rate a year, with a **Bank Rate** beside it. On 2026-09-27 the Owner decided what an Investor reads of the same figure. The research is in [`docs/research/stating-a-mudarabah-return.md`](../research/stating-a-mudarabah-return.md). A settled return put as a share of capital is a fact, not a term, and AAOIFI and Bank Negara Malaysia both allow it. A rate a year on a run under a year is a simulated figure (GIPS 2.A.12). It sets a Venture beside a bank deposit in the reader's eye, and every scheme shut down in Bangladesh led with a rate.

So an Investor reads:

- **A settled Venture's share, and nothing more.** Once the Settlement is approved, the payout in taka comes first, then «প্রতি ১০০ টাকা মূলধনে … টাকা লাভ» over the Venture's own days. A loss is shown the same way. This appears on the portal's Venture page and on the **হিসাব নিকাশ**, under the standing footer.
- **Never a rate a year**, even labelled and with its working. The Owner's rate stays the Owner's.
- **Each Venture on its own.** No blended figure across Ventures: that is a track record in all but name, and BSEC's advertising rules call a past return shown as likely to recur misleading.
- **The Projection stays a taka range** (ADR 0010), never a share and never a rate. Nothing else running is shown.
- **No Bank Rate, no past result on an offer, and never a top-up** to any stated figure. A gift from the Farm's share is shown as a gift, not as part of their return.
- **Behind the Owner's switch**, off until the lawyer and the Shariah scholar have seen the wording. The Owner reads it in the Portal Preview meanwhile.

**Consequences:**

- The **Investor Statement** rule widens: a settled share is a fact and may be printed, but a rate a year may not.
- The advisers' approval of 2026-09-26 covered the portal without any percentage. The research's questions for them go with the spec.

**Revisit** if either adviser objects to the share, or if Ventures ever run longer than a year. A rate a year for a run of a year or more is not a simulated figure.
