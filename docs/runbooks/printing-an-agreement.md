# Printing an agreement for signatures

As the owner, open **Ventures** and choose **Print the agreement to sign** on an open venture. The farm's DLS registration number must be recorded under Farm settings before a paper can be prepared.

Choose a recorded investor, whole units within the venture's available units, the profit share, an arbitrator, and any nominees. Select **Preview printable agreement**, choose বাংলা or English, then **Print**. The browser can print the A4 document or save it as a PDF. The document uses the farm's current agreement wording and includes both parties, the investment terms, stamp or e-challan blanks, signature spaces, and two witnesses. The wording's review status is shown above the preview.

Preparing or printing creates no signed agreement and commits no units or capital. After the parties sign, use **Sign an agreement** to record its terms, signing date, and stamp details and keep the signed paper's photograph. The print option inside that signing form remains available.
