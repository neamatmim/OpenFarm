# Agreed in the app

Asked by the Owner 2026-10-03: an Investment Agreement — and an Amendment — agreed within the app instead of on stamped
paper or e-challan. The Investor agrees in the portal, the Owner approves, and it counts as signed. Any Agreement, no
amount limit; Amendments too. **Built behind a farm switch, off**, until the lawyer and the Shariah scholar confirm the
in-app route — their 2026-09-26 approval covered stamped Agreements, and whether an unstamped Agreement can be relied on
(the Stamp Act's admissibility) is theirs to say. Stamping stays possible afterwards.

**Settled in drafting:**

- **What the Investor agrees to is a snapshot**: the paper as laid out the moment the Owner offered it, kept with the
  offer; its hash is recorded with the agreement. A wording edited afterwards changes nothing they agreed to.
- **An offer is not an Agreement**: the Agreement row is written only on the Owner's approval, after the Investor has
  agreed — every count of Units, Investors and capital reads Agreements, and an offer must not move any of them. The
  same rules as signing are asked again at approval (Units left, the Investor cap, the Venture still open).
- **Its stamp line** says it was agreed in the app: the day it was approved and the offer's number; no taka.
- **The switch is the Owner's** (Investors settings, beside the portal's), off by default; offers cannot be made while
  it is off, and an offer made while on can still be approved after it is turned off — it was agreed.
- **The Investor agrees from their own portal sign-in**; the Owner can withdraw an offer until it is approved.
