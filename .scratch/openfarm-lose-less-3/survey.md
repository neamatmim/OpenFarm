## Smaller or waiting

- A bull bought dear is said only on the Manager's own sheet (`intake-sections.tsx:231`); the Owner is not told, as a
  dear feed purchase is (`feed_price_jump`). The comparison is with the same Manager's own past buys.
- Bought too close to the window: no warning when the Target Window opens before the 30-day quarantine ends.
- Hasil typed with no slip photo, compared with nothing; the Intake photo is optional and is the animal's one
  replaceable photo, though CONTEXT.md lists it as part of the Intake.
- `arrivedAt` and `soldAt` have no lower bound: a back-dated Intake can push an early death out of the seller's 30 days.
- A Selling Trip takes the tags of animals already sold or dead; nothing ties a Sale to its trip, so lorry cost can be
  steered between the Farm and a Venture.
- The broker fee is paid to nobody named and needs no slip.
- A Baki promised day moved forward by Correction is not shown as a habit of the buyer's.
- A Sale requires a vehicle and a driver even for a calf walked to a neighbour — may push small sales off the books.
- No Category is a Herd Cost by default (`chargedToAnimals` false): a visiting Vet's fee entered by hand is an Overhead
  Ventures never share, until the Owner marks it. Not checked against the live farm's setup.
- An approved Settlement not fully paid out is shown only on its sheet; a payout of ৳0 can never be marked paid, so
  such a Venture never reaches Settled (read, not tried). `reimburse` does not check the account holds the sum.
- Bank Check months never read are left off the home troubles on purpose; only the Venture's card says so.
