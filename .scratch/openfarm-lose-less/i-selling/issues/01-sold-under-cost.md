# 01 — Sold under cost, told

**What to build:** A Sale priced under her cost, or under the market low × the sale weight, is told to the Owner in the
evening post; the sale goes through.

**Blocked by:** —

- [ ] **Rule:** domain comparison from `priceOfAnimal` / `priceRangeFor` (her Venture's prices for a Venture animal).
- [ ] **Told:** a digest notice to the Owner only, inside `sale.record` and the sale correction (price changed).
- [ ] **Tests:** under cost told; above not; no market price → cost only; the Manager is never told her cost.
