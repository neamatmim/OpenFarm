# 04 — A Wage Draw, taken off at payday

**What to build:** Money a staff member takes ahead of payday is a **Wage Draw** with a balance; the month's wage entry
takes the balance off, so nobody is paid twice.

**Blocked by:** —

- [ ] **Glossary:** new **Wage Draw** ("Advance" is the Venture's word).
- [ ] **Record:** a draw against a person (Counterparty), amount, day; its own Money Event out.
- [ ] **Payday:** the wage entry shows the person's open draws and pays the wage less them; the draws close.
- [ ] **Screen:** each person's open draws on the money page.
- [ ] **Tests:** a draw then a wage nets; two draws; a draw bigger than the wage carries over.
