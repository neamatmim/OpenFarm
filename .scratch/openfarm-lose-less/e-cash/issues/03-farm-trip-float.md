# 03 — A Float for the Farm's own Buying Trip

**What to build:** Cash handed to the Manager for one of the Farm's own Buying Trips, reconciled when they come back
against the animals bought, the trip's costs and the cash brought back — as a Venture's Buying Float is.

**Blocked by:** 01

- [ ] **Glossary:** widen **Buying Float** (the Farm's own, from the Owner's Cash in Hand).
- [ ] **Draw:** the Owner hands cash for a trip (a Handover tied to the trip).
- [ ] **Reconcile:** out = animals + Hasil + the trip's costs + cash back (into the Manager's or the Owner's hand);
      refused over or short, as the Venture's (`float_over`/`float_short`).
- [ ] **Tests:** balances; short refused; the cash back lands in the hand named.
