# 02 — The weekly Cash Count

**What to build:** The Manager counts the cash in their hand weekly, blind, against what the farm says they hold; the
Owner signs it off; a difference over the Owner's line is told to the Owner.

**Blocked by:** 01

- [ ] **Glossary:** new **Cash Count** (as **Stock Count**, for notes).
- [ ] **Standard Playbook:** a weekly whole-farm count, Manager, checked by the Owner (as the store count).
- [ ] **Effect / record:** counted vs expected at that moment; the difference with a reason.
- [ ] **Farm Parameter:** the line over which the Owner is told (Owner-only).
- [ ] **Tests:** blind; a shortfall over the line told once; a Handover just before the count read in.
