# Baki — money a buyer still owes

On 2026-09-29 the Owner asked whether the app has every way for the farm and its Ventures to lose less. One gap
found: a Sale or a Dispatch can only be booked as paid, in cash, bKash or bank (`PAYMENT_METHODS`). A trader who
takes bulls at Eid and pays later, or a sweet shop that pays for its milk at the month's end, is written up as if the
money came that day, and nothing says who still owes the farm what, or for how long. On a Bangladeshi farm that is
often the biggest loss nobody sees.

**Settled with the Owner, 2026-09-29, and not to be re-asked:**

- **Money counts on the day it is paid.** The Sale keeps its price and the Dispatch its litres × price, but the
  Money Event each makes is only what was paid when the animal or the milk left. Each later payment is its own Money
  Event on its own day. The Money page, Month by month and the accountant's export stay a true picture of cash in.
- **A Venture's animal leaves paid in full.** A Sale of a Venture's animal with anything left owing is refused.
  Investors' money is never lent to a trader, and nothing the lawyer and the Shariah scholar approved changes
  (Settlement, the Venture Account, Bank Checks, statements).
- **The farm is reminded, never the buyer.** Overdue Baki is named on the Owner's and the Manager's homes and in the
  Digest, with the buyer's phone. Nothing is sent to the buyer.

**Settled in drafting** (the Owner may still change these):

- **Only money owed _to_ the farm.** Feed bought on credit (money the farm owes) is not a loss and stays out.
- **Oldest first.** A buyer's payment clears his oldest Baki first, as a trader's khata does. One payment is one
  Money Event, however many Sales or Dispatches it clears, so the register shows the handover as it happened.
- **Milk and cattle apart.** A payment is for milk or for cattle, and books under that Category (milk sales, cattle
  sales), so the reports' totals by Category stay what they are. A buyer paying for both writes two payments.
- **A promised day is asked for a Sale, optional for a Dispatch.** A trader promises a day. A milk buyer usually pays
  on a round, so a Dispatch's Baki without a promise is overdue after a Farm Parameter's days (30 by default, the
  Owner's to set).
- **Warn, never block.** Selling on Baki to a buyer who is overdue, or whose Baki was written off, is warned about on
  the Sale and Dispatch sheets, and the Owner is told. It is never refused: the Manager at the haat cannot wait for a
  phone call. The Owner may later want a limit per buyer. That is a separate question.
- **A write-off is the Owner's, and it is a loss on the animal.** What an animal fetched becomes her price less what
  was written off, in Margin, Return on Cost, Costs and the Farm's Returns. The buyer carries the mark. A buyer who
  pays after all puts the write-off back by what he paid.

## Words for the glossary

To go into `CONTEXT.md` under **Money** with ticket 01 (checked 2026-09-29: none of these is used yet; "waived" is
the Settlement Adjustment's word, "owed" the Investor's capital's, so both are avoided here):

- **Baki**: What a buyer still owes the Farm for one Sale or one Dispatch: its price, less what he paid when it left,
  less every **Baki Payment** since and anything written off. Written with the day he promised to pay by, when he
  promised one. Only the Farm's own: a Venture's animal leaves paid in full. Closed by being paid or written off,
  never by being forgotten. _Avoid_: Credit, due, owed (an Investor's), receivable, dues
- **Baki Payment**: One handover of money from a buyer towards his Baki, for milk or for cattle: its own Money Event
  on the day it came, clearing his oldest Baki first. _Avoid_: Collection, instalment, repayment
- **Write-off**: The Owner closing a Baki that will not be paid, with a reason. What the animal fetched is her price
  less it; the buyer carries it, and the next Sale or Dispatch to him says so. Not a **Settlement Adjustment**'s
  waiver. _Avoid_: Bad debt, waiver, loss (one kind)
