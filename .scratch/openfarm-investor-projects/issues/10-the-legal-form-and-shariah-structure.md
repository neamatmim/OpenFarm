# The legal form and Shariah structure of a project

Status: resolved

Type: grilling

Assignee: Neamat (with Claude)

Blocked by: —

Map: [OpenFarm investor-funded fattening projects](../map.md)

## Question

**Grilling.** Both research notes found forks we must settle before a lawyer can be asked anything useful. This ticket decides the structure the farm _proposes_; [Take the structure to a lawyer and a Shariah scholar](./11-take-the-structure-to-a-lawyer-and-a-shariah-scholar.md) tests it.

- **Legal form:**
  - the Owner signs a separate agreement with each investor;
  - one partnership deed per project;
  - a private company (per project, or one for all).
- **Investor count.** The Companies Act s.4 ceiling is 20 people in one business for gain, and a private company takes at most 50 members. Does "many investors" mean a cap of 20 per project, or a company?
- **Shariah structure.** _Mudarabah_ means investors supply the capital and bear its loss; the farm loses its effort and takes no fee. _Musharakah_ means the Owner's cash sits in the pool and loss follows capital. Or no Shariah label at all. Which fits a pool where the Owner may also invest?
- **Who investors are.** A closed, known group only (friends, family, people the Owner knows); no public advertising, no referrals. Is that the rule?
- **Money channels.** Bank channels only, or bKash/Nagad too?

Inputs: `docs/research/bangladesh-pooled-investment.md` and `docs/research/cattle-investment-schemes.md`.

## Answer

Decided with the Owner on 2026-09-17. This is the structure the farm **proposes**; [the lawyer task](./11-take-the-structure-to-a-lawyer-and-a-shariah-scholar.md) tests it before any money is taken.

- **Mudarabah.** Investors' capital buys the cattle, feed and medicine and bears any loss. The farm brings the work, and the sheds, staff and utilities are its own contribution and its own risk, repaid through its profit share — which is exactly the line [What a project is charged for](./05-what-a-project-is-charged-for.md) already drew. The farm takes **no fee on top of its profit share** (AAOIFI SS 13 8/2). The farm pays a loss only for negligence, misconduct or breach (SS 13 4/4).
- **Legal form: the Owner as mudarib**, signing a separate stamped **Investment Agreement** with each investor. No firm, no company.
- **Cap: 20 investors across all running projects together, the Owner included.** Conservative, because "one business for gain" (Companies Act s.4(2)) may well mean the farm rather than one project. The lawyer may lift it to per project, or to 50 under a company.
- **One Investment Agreement per project.** Each investor signs afresh for each project, with that project's capital, percentages and window.
- **Who may invest:** people the Owner knows personally or who are personally introduced, resident in Bangladesh, paying from a Bangladeshi bank account. No advertising, no public invitation, no referral rewards. Expatriate investors are out of scope for now: money from abroad raises a foreign-exchange question nobody has answered.
- **Money: bank channels only** — transfer, cheque or deposit slip, in and out, with the reference recorded. No cash, no bKash or Nagad.
- **A dedicated Venture Account**, one bank account in the Owner's name used only for investor projects. The farm's own operating money never passes through it; OpenFarm keeps each project's share of it apart.
- **The Owner's own capital is treated exactly like an investor's**: it earns the investors' share pro rata and bears loss like theirs. The farm's management share is a separate line on every statement, and the Owner counts as one of the 20.
- **The software is an internal ledger and a statement printer.** No sign-up, no "invest now", no online payment collection, no referral scheme, and no promised return anywhere, projections included.

Glossary: added **Investor**, **Investment Agreement**, **Venture Account**. The word for the project itself is still open, and is the next ticket's.
