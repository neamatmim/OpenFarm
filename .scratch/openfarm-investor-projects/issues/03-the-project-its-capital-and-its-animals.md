# The Project: its capital, its animals and its life

Status: resolved

Type: grilling

Blocked by: 10

Map: [OpenFarm investor-funded fattening projects](../map.md)

## Question

**Grilling.** What is one investor-funded project, as the Farm records it? (Name it; "Batch" and "Load" are taken.)

- **Lifecycle.** For example: open for money, then funded, then buying, then feeding, then selling, then settled. Which states are real? What moves a project between them, and who does it?
- **Target amount.** Is there a target capital? What happens if it is under-funded? Can money arrive after buying starts?
- **Animals.** How does an Animal come to belong to a project: chosen at Intake, or assigned afterwards? Can one move between projects or to the Farm's own herd? Can one of the Farm's own cattle be sold into a project? Is the answer always exactly one owner per Animal?
- **Capital left over.** What happens to capital not spent on cattle? Does it wait, buy feed, or get returned?
- **End.** Does a project have a Target Window of its own, or do its Animals' Windows decide? When is it over: last Sale, last payout?
- **Side.** How does it show on the register and the Pen board? Do Staff even see which project an Animal belongs to?

## Answer

Decided with the Owner on 2026-09-18. The word is **Venture**; **Venture Account** is renamed **Venture Account**.

**Its life.** Open (Investment Agreements signed, capital arriving, no animals yet) → Buying (capital in hand, Intakes happening) → Fattening (all bought, feeding and weighing) → Selling (first Sale recorded) → Settled (payouts done, closed). A sixth state, **Cancelled**, is where an under-funded Venture ends. The Owner moves it from Open to Buying and from Buying to Fattening; the first Sale and the last payout move the last two by themselves.

**Its plan.** A target capital, a **Floor** below which buying isn't worth starting, and a date to decide by. Capital is planned in two parts: a **Cattle Budget** and a **Running Budget** held back in the Venture Account for feed, medicine and haat costs. The Venture names its own **Target Window** (e.g. Eid 2027), and every Animal bought into it takes that as her Target Window unless the Manager sets hers differently.

**Under-funding.** Reach the Floor by the decision date and the Venture buys what the money allows. Miss it and every taka is refunded through the Venture Account, and the Venture ends **Cancelled**.

**Capital closes at Buying.** Once the first Animal is bought, no new Investor and no top-up. Every Investor's share is fixed for the whole Venture.

**Leftover.** Cattle Budget unspent when buying closes rolls into the Running Budget. Whatever is left at the end goes back to Investors as part of settlement.

**Short of running money.** The Owner may make an **Advance**: interest-free money into the Venture, recorded against it, repaid at cost from sale proceeds before any profit is split. Never a charge, never a return.

**Its animals.** Every Intake says whose money bought her — a Venture, or the Farm — and exactly one owns her at a time. A wrong choice is put right inside the normal Correction Window; she never drifts between owners.

**Internal Sale.** An Animal may nevertheless be sold between the Farm's herd and a Venture, and it is a sale, not a reassignment: priced at her latest Weigh-in × a live-weight rate the Owner enters that day with a note of where the rate came from, money actually moving through the Venture Account. Owner-only and audited. **Barred once the Venture is Selling or the Animal is Ready for Sale**, so a finished bull cannot be lifted out of the pool.

**Who sees it.** Owner and Manager only. Barn Staff see animals, Pens and work, never whose money bought them, so nobody handles an Investor's bull differently. Whether the Manager sees Investors and amounts is [the roles ticket](./09-who-may-do-what-with-project-money.md)'s.

Glossary: added **Venture**, **Cattle Budget**, **Running Budget**, **Floor**, **Internal Sale**, **Advance**; renamed **Venture Account** to **Venture Account**.
