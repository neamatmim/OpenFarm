# What a project is charged for

Status: resolved

Type: grilling

Assignee: Neamat (with Claude)

Blocked by: —

Map: [OpenFarm investor-funded fattening projects](../map.md)

## Question

**Grilling.** Project cattle stand in mixed Pens, and the Farm already charges costs to each Animal: purchase price, feed by weighted-average price and animal-days, doses, and named Vet Fees (see **Margin** and **Cost of Gain** in `CONTEXT.md`). What else is a project's cost, and what is the Farm's side?

- **Buying costs.** Are these charged to the project: transport to the farm, haat and market fees, broker commission, quarantine?
- **Selling costs.** Are these charged to the project: transport to the haat, haat lease or stall, broker, Eid-time guards?
- **Herd-wide costs.** Campaign vaccines and deworming, a vet visit that named no Animal, mineral mix, bedding: charged per animal-day, per head, or not at all?
- **The Farm's side.** Is it all of wages, electricity, water, shed repairs and equipment, and never charged? Is anything in between, such as a new trough bought for the project?
- **Unattributed spend.** Which Category, and which side of the line, does a hand-entered Money Event that no record caused fall on? Who decides, and when?
- **Timing.** Are costs charged as they happen, so a running project shows its spend, or only at settlement?

## Answer

Decided with the Owner on 2026-09-17.

**The principle.** A project pays for the animal and what goes into her. The Farm pays for the place and the people: sheds, staff wages, electricity, water, repairs, equipment and shed hygiene. Every question below was judged against that line.

**One way of costing.** An Animal costs the same whoever owns her. Every charge below goes to every Animal, whether she is the Farm's own or a project's, so the Farm's own cattle's Margin and Cost of Gain widen too. A project's cost is the sum of its Animals' costs. Nothing can be moved between the Farm and a project by choosing how to count.

What an Animal is charged, on top of Release 1's purchase price, feed, doses and named Vet Fees:

| Cost | Charged how |
| --- | --- |
| Home-grown fodder | A **Fodder Price** per kg set by the Owner on each home-grown Feed Item; a Harvest enters the store at it and joins the weighted average. It is no longer free. |
| **Hasil** (haat toll) | Typed per Animal from the haat's slip; charged to her alone. |
| **Buying Trip** (broker, lorry home, men's food and lodging) | Recorded once per trip; split evenly across the Animals brought in on it. |
| **Selling Trip** (lorry both ways, stall, men's food and lodging) | Recorded once per trip; split evenly across every Animal _taken_, sold or brought home. A broker's per-sale fee is typed on that Sale. |
| **Herd Cost** (Vet visit naming no animal, lab tests, fly spray, dewormer not given as a dose) | A hand-entered Money Event whose Category is marked as charged. Split across the Animals of its Side by animal-days in its month. |

**Never charged to an Animal (the Farm's):**

- wages, utilities, repairs, shed hygiene (disinfectant, lime, bedding)
- equipment, even when a project prompted the purchase (a trough, shade net, scale)
- feed lost from the store, as found by a Stock Count

**Who decides.** The Owner marks each **Category** once as charged or not. The Manager only picks the Category and Side, as today. Changing a mark is the Owner's, and audited.

**Timing.** A project's spend is worked out live from the records whenever it's read, so Corrections flow through. A Herd Cost appears once its month closes. Settlement freezes the numbers; how is the Settlement ticket's to decide.

**Holes in prices.** Uncosted doses and unpriced kg on a project Animal are shown on the project as holes to fill. They are never counted as free, and **the project cannot be settled until they are filled** by recording the missing purchase.

Not decided here: what a dead or culled Animal's costs mean for investors (belongs to **The contract**).

Glossary: added **Fodder Price**, **Buying Trip**, **Selling Trip**, **Hasil**, **Herd Cost**. Widened **Harvest**, **Margin**, **Category** and **Stock Count**.
