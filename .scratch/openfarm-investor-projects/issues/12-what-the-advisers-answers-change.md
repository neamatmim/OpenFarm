# What the advisers' answers change

Status: open

Type: grilling

Blocked by: 11

Map: [OpenFarm investor-funded fattening projects](../map.md)

## Question

**Grilling.** The lawyer and the Shariah scholar have answered ([the task](./11-take-the-structure-to-a-lawyer-and-a-shariah-scholar.md)). What changes?

- **The structure.** Does the 20-Investor cap hold, move to per Venture, or give way to a company? Does anything about the Owner-as-mudarib form have to change?
- **The Investment Agreement.** The stamp article and duty; whether the wording risks reading as a deposit; what the agreement must say that the decided terms do not.
- **The software.** Whether an Investor-facing screen would count as a platform; whether the statements need anything added; what must be refused outright.
- **Tax.** Whether any withholding applies to a payout, what certificate the Investor is owed, and what the accountant export must carry.
- **Shariah.** Whether Buying and Selling Trip costs and Herd Costs may be charged to the pool ([What a project is charged for](./05-what-a-project-is-charged-for.md) assumes they may), and whether reversible interim payouts are acceptable.
- **Decided tickets.** Which earlier answers must be revisited, and which stand.
