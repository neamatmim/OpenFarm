# Who may do what with project money

Status: resolved

Type: grilling

Assignee: Neamat (with Claude)

Blocked by: 06, 07

Map: [OpenFarm investor-funded fattening projects](../map.md)

## Question

**Grilling.** Where do projects sit in the Release 1 roles matrix (`../openfarm-release-1/assets/roles-matrix.md`)?

- **Who acts.** Who opens a Venture, records capital in, records payouts, prepares and approves settlement, and generates a statement? (An Internal Sale and an Advance are already Owner-only.)
- **Who sees what.** The Manager sees which Venture owns an Animal and Barn Staff see nothing of it. Does the Manager also see Investors' names and amounts, the Venture's budgets, or its result? Does the Vet see anything?
- **Approvals.** Does the Approval Threshold apply to payouts, or does every payout wait for the Owner?
- **The money chores.** Who records capital received, draws and reconciles a Buying Float, runs the monthly Reimbursement, and checks the Venture Account against the bank?
- **Corrections.** What are the Correction Windows for capital, payout and Settlement records? Which of them are special actions? A Settlement is frozen by the Owner's approval, so who may raise a Settlement Adjustment, and who decides a supplementary payout or accepts a waiver?

## Answer

Decided with the Owner on 2026-09-18. The matrix is `../assets/venture-roles-matrix.md`.

- **No new Role.** Owner and Manager as they stand; Barn Staff and Vets see nothing of Ventures at all.
- **The Manager sees the work, not the people.** Which Venture owns an Animal, its budgets, its spend and its warnings — and nothing of Investors, Units, the split, payouts or the result. That is between the Owner and the people whose money it is.
- **Owner banks, Manager spends.** Owner only: open a Venture and move it on, the Investor record, capital and refunds, drawing and depositing the **Buying Float**, the monthly **Reimbursement**, checking the account against the bank, the **Advance**, the **Internal Sale**, approving the **Settlement**, recording payouts, issuing statements and raising a **Settlement Adjustment**. The Manager records the Intakes, Hasil, trip costs and Sales — the work of spending, not the banking.
- **Approvals.** Nothing new waits: every Venture money act is already the Owner's own. What the Manager records follows the existing Approval Threshold, as for the Farm's own animals.
- **Corrections.** Release 1's windows carry over — Manager 30 days on their own entries, Owner always — except that approving a Settlement freezes that Venture. Afterwards only a Settlement Adjustment, the Owner's alone.
