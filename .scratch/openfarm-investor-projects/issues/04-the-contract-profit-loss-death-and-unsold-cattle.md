## Question

**Grilling.** The terms the Owner decides as a matter of business, and which of them OpenFarm holds as numbers or rules it computes with. The legal and Shariah wording is [What the advisers' answers change](./12-what-the-advisers-answers-change.md), so this ticket does not wait on the lawyer.

- **Split.** Is the profit split one percentage per project (Investors : Farm), set when the project opens and frozen? Mudarabah bars a fee on top of the profit share, so there is nothing to take before the split — but a **ceiling** is allowed (profit above a stated rate all goes to the Farm, AAOIFI SS 13 8/5). Do we want one? Known practice gives investors 30–50%.
- **Loss.** Mudarabah settles the principle: loss comes out of the Investors' capital, and the Farm loses its work and its sheds, staff and utilities. What remains is the negligence rule — what counts as the Farm's negligence, misconduct or breach, who decides it, and what it costs the Farm.
- **Death and culling.** Is a dead Animal a project loss, replaced by the Farm, or covered by insurance?
- **Unsold at the end.** Carry on feeding (at whose cost, given the Running Budget is finite), a buy-out by the Farm at a valuation — which is an Internal Sale, already priced by weight — or a forced sale?
- **Early exit.** Can an Investor leave early, and at what value? Capital closes when a Venture starts Buying and no new Investor may join, so somebody must take the share over — the Owner, or another existing Investor. Known practice (WeGro): a full refund in the first 20% of the term, then a charge and no profit.
- **Changing terms.** Can terms change mid-Venture, and what does the record of the Investment Agreement (versions, signed copy photo) need to hold?

Inputs from research (see the two resolved research tickets):

- Profit is a percentage of _actual_ profit. There is no guaranteed capital or return, and the percentages are fixed at signing and changed only by dated amendment.
- Under Mudarabah the farm may take no fee on top of its share.
- Known practice: investors get 30–50% of profit.
  - _Unsold animals:_ the farm buys them at the Eid price (nusaQu).
  - _Early exit:_ full refund in the first 20% of the term, then 2% or Tk 2,500 and no profit (WeGro).
  - _Death:_ Green Delta sells 4–6-month fattening cover.
- No scheme publishes an underfunding rule.

## Answer

Decided with the Owner on 2026-09-18. These are the commercial terms; the legal and Shariah wording is [What the advisers' answers change](./12-what-the-advisers-answers-change.md).

- **Split.** One percentage per Venture, Investors : Farm, taken from a farm-wide default of **60 : 40** and changeable by the Owner before the Venture opens. Frozen in every Investment Agreement of that Venture once it is Open. Never per Investor: the same percentages for everybody in a Venture. **No ceiling clause** — one split all the way up.
- **Loss.** Mudarabah: a loss comes out of Investors' capital. The Farm loses its work and its sheds, staff and utilities, and pays only where it was negligent.
- **Death.** A dead Animal is the Venture's loss: everything spent on her stays in its costs. No insurance and no replacement by the Farm, which would guarantee capital and turn the agreement towards a deposit. The mortality, treatment and SOP records are the evidence the Farm was not negligent.
- **Negligence.** The Investment Agreement names what counts: an Animal dying where the SOP record shows feeding or treatment was not done, a Sale below an agreed floor without approval, records not kept. An **Arbitrator**, named by both sides at signing, decides a claim. Found negligent, the Farm makes good **that specific loss**, not the whole Venture.
- **Unsold at the window.** A **Wind-up Period** (default 30 days) after the Target Window to keep selling, fed from the Running Budget or an Advance. Whatever is still unsold at its end the Farm buys as an **Internal Sale** at the latest Weigh-in × the day's live-weight rate, so the Venture settles on time.
- **Early exit.** None: the capital is in cattle and there is nothing to refund. A share may be taken over by the Owner or by another Investor in that Venture at capital value, by signed amendment. Otherwise the Investor waits for settlement.
- **Amendments.** Percentages and the window are frozen at signing. A change needs a dated amendment signed by **every** Investor in that Venture, stored as a photo like the original. The system keeps both and shows which was in force when.
- **Farm-wide settings** (Owner may change; a Venture takes them as its starting point): default split 60/40, Wind-up Period 30 days, Floor as a percentage of target capital, and the Running Budget's share of capital.

Glossary: added **Wind-up Period** and **Arbitrator**.
