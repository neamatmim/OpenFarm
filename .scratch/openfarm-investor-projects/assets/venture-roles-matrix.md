**Approvals.** Every Venture money act is the Owner's own, so nothing waits for approval. What the Manager records against a Venture — Intakes, Hasil, trip costs, Sales — follows the existing Approval Threshold exactly as for the Farm's own animals.

**Corrections.** The Release 1 windows carry over: Manager 30 days on what they recorded, Owner always. Approving a Settlement freezes everything in that Venture; after that only a **Settlement Adjustment**, raised by the Owner alone, who also decides a supplementary payout or accepts a waiver.
