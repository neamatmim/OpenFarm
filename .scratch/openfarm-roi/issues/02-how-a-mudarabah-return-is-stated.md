## Question

A **Venture** is a mudarabah, with the Owner as mudarib. Before an Investor is shown a return, as a share of their capital or as a rate a year, on a settled Venture or on the **Projection**, find out how such a return is stated elsewhere, and what makes a stated rate read as a promise.

- **Islamic banks in Bangladesh.** How do they publish profit rates on mudaraba deposits: per year, "provisional" then "final", weightages? What words do they use in Bangla and English, and what does Bangladesh Bank require of them?
- **AAOIFI and the Shariah boards.** What do the standards say about stating a past or expected return to a mudarabah investor, and about putting it as an annual rate that could look like interest?
- **Cattle and agri platforms.** [`cattle-investment-schemes.md`](../../../docs/research/cattle-investment-schemes.md) quotes "projected ROI 8% (Annualized ROI 24%)" and "Variable Return". Collect how the live Bangladeshi platforms show a settled or past return, in what words, and with what disclaimers.
- **Regulators.** Anything from BSEC, Bangladesh Bank or consumer law on advertising or stating a return to investors outside a licensed scheme, beyond what [`bangladesh-pooled-investment.md`](../../../docs/research/bangladesh-pooled-investment.md) already found.
- **The line.** From all of the above: which ways of stating a past return, a range, or an annual rate have read as a promised or fixed return in the collapsed schemes and the enforcement cases, and which have not?

Primary sources first: Bangladesh Bank circulars, bank profit-rate notices, AAOIFI Shariah standards, platform pages (archived), BSEC notices.
