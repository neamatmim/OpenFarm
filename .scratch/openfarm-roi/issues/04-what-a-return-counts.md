# What a return counts

Status: done

Assignee: Neamat Khan Mim

Type: grilling

Blocked by: 01, 03

Map: [OpenFarm: what the money in cattle returns](../map.md)

## Question

Decide the sum: what money went in and what came back, for a **Season** of the Farm's own cattle ([What a run of the Farm's own cattle is](./03-what-a-run-of-the-farms-own-cattle-is.md)) and for a **Venture**. It should be one sum wherever it can be, so the two read side by side.

- **The money in.** Purchase prices (or the Internal Sale price, for one bought from a Venture) plus everything charged, as a **Margin** counts it, or the capital raised? For a Venture, is it the capital the Investors put in, or what was actually spent from it? Money that sat unspent in the **Venture Account** (the **Running Budget** left over, a short **Cattle Budget**) earned nothing while it was tied up.
- **The money back.** A Season's sales, and the Internal Sales that took its animals to a Venture. A Venture's proceeds, before or after the Farm's share of the profit? An Investor's return comes after it. The Farm's share is earned for work with no capital in, so it has no return in this sense. Say what the Owner sees of it instead.
- **Deaths.** An animal that died has no Margin, so a sum of Margins leaves her out. Her cost must come off the Season's return.
- **The Advance.** The Owner's own money in a Venture, repaid at cost. Is it part of the money in?
- **What is never charged.** Wages, sheds, utilities, repairs and equipment are the Farm's and no animal's. A Venture pays for them through the Farm's share; a Season carries none of them. Is that a fair comparison, or does a Season read against a charge for them?
- **The word.** CONTEXT.md lists _return_ as a word to avoid for Margin. Choose the word for this sum, and say how it sits beside Margin and Settlement.

[Ticket 01](./01-how-a-cattle-return-is-measured.md)'s research says which of these the published measures include, and how.

From [the research](../../../docs/research/measuring-a-cattle-return.md) (2026-09-27): the comparable figure is **net return ÷ total cost**, not a BCR. A dead animal's cost stays in the run. A home-bred calf enters at market value when she leaves the dairy herd, as an **Internal Sale** is priced, not at 0. Bangladeshi studies also count dung and sacks as income, and charge labour and interest, so decide whether to show a study-like figure beside ours. For a Venture, a money-weighted return on the Investors' dated money catches capital idle in the Venture Account; a return on the animals' cost does not. See its "Choices for the grilling".

From [the second research](../../../docs/research/stating-a-mudarabah-return.md) (2026-09-27): the word must not read as a deposit rate. Avoid মুনাফার হার and "বার্ষিক রিটার্ন" standing alone, and never সুদ, নিশ্চিত, নির্ধারিত, ফিক্সড or secure. If the Owner gives up part of the Farm's share after a Settlement, it is a gift from that share, not the Venture's return (SS 40 5/6).

Since [What a run of the Farm's own cattle is](./03-what-a-run-of-the-farms-own-cattle-is.md) (2026-09-27): a calf bred here or a cow crossed from Dairy joins a Season on the day she crosses, so her money in is what she is worth that day. Settle the price: market value as the research says, weight × the farm's market price a kilo, or her costs as a calf. Her costs before the crossing are the Dairy side's, but a Margin counts her whole life.

## Resolution

Grilled with the Owner, 2026-09-27. CONTEXT.md gains **Return on Cost** and **Return on Capital**. **Margin** keeps its avoid list and is unchanged. No ADR: the sum is the Settlement's, which already exists.

- **A Season's result is worked as a Settlement is.** It is what its animals fetched, Internal Sales out to a Venture among it, less what they cost to take on and everything charged to them while they were the Season's. An animal who died is in it. A Season and a Venture are therefore one sum. Chosen over a sum of Margins, with or without the dead: a Margin counts a whole life at the intake price, so a crossed calf would carry her dairy days. No new word for the taka: it is what the Season made or lost.
- **What an animal is taken on at.** One bought at a haat enters at her price. One bought from a Venture enters at the Internal Sale's price. **One bred here or crossed from Dairy enters at a price the Owner enters**: her weight at the crossing × a rate a kilo, with where the rate came from, as an Internal Sale is priced. It is the Owner's, since the Manager may make the crossing but never sees money. Until she is priced, the Season is not yet a result and says why. **The same taka is the dairy herd's return for her** ([What the dairy herd returns](./08-what-the-dairy-herd-returns.md)). Chosen over the farm's market price (a finished bull's), her rearing cost, and nothing.
- **Return on Cost** (খরচে লাভ) is the result ÷ total cost, for a Season and for a Venture's cattle, so the two read side by side. It is said as a fact: «প্রতি ১০০ টাকা খরচে ২৮ টাকা লাভ». The **Advance** is no part of it: a charge is a charge, whoever's money paid it.
- **Return on Capital** (মূলধনে লাভ) is the Investors' profit share ÷ the capital they put in, after the Farm's share. All the capital counts, the part that sat unspent in the Venture Account too. The Advance is not capital. The Owner reads **both** for a Venture, named apart. Return on Capital is the one an Investor may later be shown ([What an Investor reads of a return](./09-what-an-investor-reads-of-a-return.md)).
- **The Farm's share has no ratio.** The Farm puts in work and no money, so it is read in taka. A part of it the Owner gives up to Investors is a gift from that share, never their return (AAOIFI SS 40 5/6, from the research).
- **A loss** is the same sum below nothing.
- **One sentence beside every return:** wages, sheds, equipment, dung and what the money would have earned elsewhere are not in it, so it reads higher than a study's on the same animals. Chosen over a second, study-like figure that charges wages by animal-days, and over always charging them.
- **Words:** both terms avoid ROI, yield (the milk's), profit rate, মুনাফার হার and interest, per the research.
- **For the spec:** pricing a crossing needs her weight on the day. The Wind-up buy-back already names and refuses an unweighed animal; decide the same or a Weigh-in at the crossing. A crossing waits on the Owner's price, and that wait is work shown to the Owner.
