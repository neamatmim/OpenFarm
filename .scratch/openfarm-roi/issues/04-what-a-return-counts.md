# What a return counts

Status: open

Type: grilling

Blocked by: 01, 03

Map: [OpenFarm: what the money in cattle returns](../map.md)

## Question

Decide the sum: what money went in and what came back, for a run of the Farm's own cattle ([ticket 03](./03-what-a-run-of-the-farms-own-cattle-is.md)) and for a **Venture**. It should be one sum wherever it can be, so the two read side by side.

- **The money in.** Purchase prices plus everything charged, as a **Margin** counts it, or the capital raised? For a Venture, is it the capital the Investors put in, or what was actually spent from it? Money that sat unspent in the **Venture Account** (the **Running Budget** left over, a short **Cattle Budget**) earned nothing while it was tied up.
- **The money back.** A run's sales. A Venture's proceeds, before or after the Farm's share of the profit? An Investor's return comes after it. The Farm's share is earned for work with no capital in, so it has no return in this sense. Say what the Owner sees of it instead.
- **Deaths.** An animal that died has no Margin, so a sum of Margins leaves her out. Her cost must come off the run's return.
- **The Advance.** The Owner's own money in a Venture, repaid at cost. Is it part of the money in?
- **What is never charged.** Wages, sheds, utilities, repairs and equipment are the Farm's and no animal's. A Venture pays for them through the Farm's share; the Farm's own run carries none of them. Is that a fair comparison, or does the Farm's own run read against a charge for them?
- **The word.** CONTEXT.md lists _return_ as a word to avoid for Margin. Choose the word for this sum, and say how it sits beside Margin and Settlement.

[Ticket 01](./01-how-a-cattle-return-is-measured.md)'s research says which of these the published measures include, and how.
