# The bank rate beside it

Status: open

Type: grilling

Blocked by: 02, 05

Map: [OpenFarm: what the money in cattle returns](../map.md)

## Question

The Owner wants each return set beside a rate the money could have earned in a bank. Decide:

- **Which rate.** An FDR rate, an Islamic bank's mudaraba profit rate, or several? Typed by the Owner, one for the farm?
- **Kept how.** A **Farm Parameter** with one value, or a dated history, so a run from last year reads against last year's rate?
- **Read against what.** Only the rate a year from [ticket 05](./05-how-a-return-is-put-per-year.md), or the share too? Is the bank's rate before or after tax?
- **Who sees it.** The Owner only, or Investors too ([ticket 09](./09-what-an-investor-reads-of-a-return.md))? Setting a mudarabah beside an interest rate may itself be a Shariah question ([ticket 02](./02-how-a-mudarabah-return-is-stated.md)).
