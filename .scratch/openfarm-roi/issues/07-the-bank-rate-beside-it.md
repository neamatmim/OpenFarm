# The bank rate beside it

Status: done

Assignee: Neamat Khan Mim

Type: grilling

Blocked by: 02, 05

Map: [OpenFarm: what the money in cattle returns](../map.md)

## Question

The Owner wants each return set beside a rate the money could have earned in a bank. Decide:

- **Which rate.** An FDR rate, an Islamic bank's mudaraba profit rate, or several? Typed by the Owner, one for the farm?
- **Kept how.** A **Farm Parameter** with one value, or a dated history, so a Season or Venture from last year reads against last year's rate?
- **Read against what.** Only the rate a year from [ticket 05](./05-how-a-return-is-put-per-year.md), or the share too? Is the bank's rate before or after tax?
- **Who sees it.** The Owner only, or Investors too ([ticket 09](./09-what-an-investor-reads-of-a-return.md))? Setting a mudarabah beside an interest rate may itself be a Shariah question ([ticket 02](./02-how-a-mudarabah-return-is-stated.md)).

From [the second research](../../../docs/research/stating-a-mudarabah-return.md) (2026-09-27): candidates are an Islamic bank's final mudaraba rate, a yearly published Shariah-side figure, or Bangladesh Bank's deposit-rate chart. Bank tables show profit before and after the 10% tax. For the Owner's own judgement a bank rate beside the return is ordinary. For Investors it points the other way: BSEC says a past return is no basis for comparing investments, and the collapsed schemes sold themselves as beating bank savings. Whether a Venture may be set beside an interest rate at all is a question for the Shariah scholar.

Since [What a return counts](./04-what-a-return-counts.md) (2026-09-27): there are two ratios, **Return on Cost** and **Return on Capital**. A bank rate is a return on money deposited, so it reads most like Return on Capital. Decide which of the two it sits beside.

Since [How a return is put per year](./05-how-a-return-is-put-per-year.md) (2026-09-27): the rate a year is simple, per year, over money × days, as a bank's is. It is not shown under 60 days (the Owner's Farm Parameter), and then there is nothing to set a bank rate beside.

## Resolution

Grilled with the Owner, 2026-09-27. CONTEXT.md gains **Bank Rate**. No ADR.

- **The Owner's alone.** No Investor and no portal page ever shows a Venture beside a bank rate. BSEC says a past return is no basis for comparing investments, and the collapsed schemes sold themselves on beating bank savings (the second research). The Shariah question about setting a mudarabah beside a deposit rate therefore doesn't arise.
- **One rate a year the Owner types, with a note** of what it is, such as "IBBL 12-month mudaraba, final 2025". Islamic bank or FDR is the Owner's choice. Chosen over two fixed kinds or several named rates.
- **Before tax**, as the bank quotes it, because the return beside it is before tax too. The note says so.
- **Dated.** Each rate starts on the day it is typed and stays until the next, and the old ones are kept. A Season or Venture reads against the rate in force on the day its first taka went in, as a deposit would have locked it. Chosen over one current value, which would judge a 2025 Season by a 2027 rate, and over the rate at the finish.
- **Beside every finished rate a year**: a Season's Return on Cost, and a Venture's Return on Cost and Return on Capital. Never beside a share, a running figure, or anything under the 60-day floor.
- **A plain line with its note**, such as «ব্যাংকের হার, বছরে: ৯.২ — IBBL 12-month mudaraba». Never worded as the cattle beating it. With no rate typed, there is no line.
- **For the spec:** the Owner's to set, audited, dated rows rather than one Farm Parameter; where it is typed goes with where the Owner reads returns (the fog on the map).
