# How a return is put per year

Status: done

Assignee: Neamat Khan Mim

Type: grilling

Blocked by: 04

Map: [OpenFarm: what the money in cattle returns](../map.md)

## Question

A Season or Venture of four months and one of ten don't compare on their share of the money alone. Decide how the return from [ticket 04](./04-what-a-return-counts.md) is put as a rate a year:

- **Over what time.** From the first taka in to the last out, from each animal's purchase to her sale, or by capital × days (each taka weighted by the days it was tied up)?
- **Simple or compound.** Scaling by days, or compounding as a bank would?
- **A short Season.** A winter-market Season of a few weeks scaled to a year can read wildly high. Is there a floor below which the rate is not shown?
- **A loss.** How a loss is put per year, and whether it is.
- **Both or one.** Does the Owner read the share and the rate a year together, or only one?

[Ticket 01](./01-how-a-cattle-return-is-measured.md)'s research names the methods and their known pitfalls.

From [the research](../../../docs/research/measuring-a-cattle-return.md) (2026-09-27): Bangladeshi studies don't annualise, and GIPS 2.A.12 forbids annualising a return for less than a year. Where others scale, it is simple × 365 ÷ days (lenders, bank rates, feedlot closeouts over capital-days), never compounded: 52% in four months reads 155% simple and 248% compounded. The options are no rate a year, simple over total cost, capital-days, or a money-weighted IRR from dated money.

From [the second research](../../../docs/research/stating-a-mudarabah-return.md) (2026-09-27): capital × days is what Shariah practice already uses to weight money by time (SS 40 5/1, Bangladesh Bank's "Total Yearly Product"), and Islamic banks scale by simple days, never compounded. The floors in use for showing no rate a year: under a year (GIPS), under six months (Pakistan's SECP), under 60 days (FINRA). A rate a year never stands alone: it sits after the share for the run, labelled as that share scaled, with its working shown.

Since [What a return counts](./04-what-a-return-counts.md) (2026-09-27): there are two ratios to put per year. **Return on Cost** has a Season's or a Venture's cattle, bought over weeks and sold over weeks. **Return on Capital** has the Investors' capital, which arrives and leaves on dated Venture Movements and includes money that sat unspent. Decide whether both get a rate a year, and whether Return on Capital is weighted by the days each taka was in.

## Resolution

Grilled with the Owner, 2026-09-27. The **Return on Cost** and **Return on Capital** entries in CONTEXT.md each gain how they are put a year. No ADR.

Worked example used in the grilling: a Season buys ৳10 lakh of bulls over six weeks, feeds ৳4 lakh day by day over five months, and sells for ৳17 lakh. That is 21 on every 100, about ৳11 lakh tied up on average for about five months, and about 50 a year.

- **Return on Cost is put a year over money × days.** Each taka counts from the day it was spent, a purchase or a day's feed, to the day its animal was sold: the average money tied up. This is the weighting Shariah practice and Bangladesh Bank's "Total Yearly Product" use. Chosen over each animal's whole cost from purchase to sale, which treats feed as paid on day one and reads low, and over first taka to last, where one kept-over bull collapses the Season's rate.
- **Return on Capital is put a year over arrival to payout.** Each taka of capital counts from the day it reached the Venture Account to the day it was paid back, idle days included. That holds for the Venture's capital together, and for each Investor over their own. Chosen over the Venture's own dates, which flatter whoever paid in early or was paid late.
- **Simple, never compounded:** share × 365 ÷ days. This is how Islamic banks, lenders and feedlot closeouts scale, and a bank rate typed beside it is simple too. A Season turns once a year.
- **No rate a year under 60 days**, counted as the average days the money was tied up. It is a Farm Parameter the Owner may change, like the keep-or-sell numbers. The share over its own days is always shown. Chosen over six months (most Eid Seasons would fall short) and no floor.
- **A loss is scaled the same way**, below nothing, so nothing is flattered.
- **How it shows** (agreed as proposed from the research): the share first, then its days, then the rate a year, labelled as the share scaled to a year, never alone. The working opens under it: the average money tied up and the days. In Bangla «বছরের হিসাবে প্রতি ১০০ টাকায় …», never a bare «বার্ষিক রিটার্ন».
- **Not decided here:** whether an Investor sees a rate a year at all is [What an Investor reads of a return](./09-what-an-investor-reads-of-a-return.md)'s.
- **For the spec:** money × days needs each charge's day, and the costing already dates every feeding, dose, Herd Cost share and Trip. A Season's rate uses what it cost while the Farm owned her, as the Settlement narrows it. The floor's refusal needs a word, as other Farm Parameters' do.
