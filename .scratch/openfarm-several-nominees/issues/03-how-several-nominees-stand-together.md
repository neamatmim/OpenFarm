# How several Nominees stand together

Status: done

Assignee: Neamat Khan Mim

Type: grilling

Blocked by: 02

Map: [OpenFarm: an Investor may name several Nominees](../map.md)

## Question

Once [What a Nominee is in OpenFarm](./02-what-a-nominee-is-in-openfarm.md) is settled, decide how one Investor's several Nominees relate to one another:

- **Shares, an order, or together?** Percentages that add to 100, a first Nominee with substitutes, or all of them collecting jointly. If a Nominee collects for the heirs, shares may mean nothing.
- **How many at most**, and whether none is allowed. Today a nominee is optional.
- **When one Nominee dies first**, what happens to their share or place.
- **A Nominee under eighteen.** Today one guardian line is printed and struck through by hand. Does each minor Nominee need their own guardian, recorded or only printed? The farm doesn't record a Nominee's age.
- **What is recorded of each one**: name, phone, relation, and whether anything more (NID, address) is needed.
- ~~Still per Investor?~~ Settled by 04: one list per Investor, governing every Agreement they hold.
- **Today's single nominee** becomes the first of that Investor's Nominees; if Nominees take shares, it takes the whole until a Nomination says otherwise. Confirm that.

From the research and ticket 02 (2026-09-26): a Nominee only collects, so a share decides **who collects which part, never who inherits**. The market uses concurrent Nominees, each with a percentage (CDBL, banks, insurers) or a taka amount (savings certificates), capped at 2–3 by the forms, with no order or substitute. A Nominee who dies first drops out. Every statute lets the holder name **who receives for a minor Nominee**, a field today's guardian line lacks. See [the research](../../../docs/research/nominees-in-bangladeshi-law-and-shariah.md), sections 5–7 of its answer.

## Resolution

Grilled with the Owner, 2026-09-26. CONTEXT.md's **Nominee** entry is widened, and a **Receiver** entry is added. No ADR: each choice follows the market and the collector reading from ticket 02.

1. **A share each.** Each Nominee collects their own part, in whole percentages that add to 100, and a single Nominee holds 100%.
   - Paying one Nominee their part discharges the Farm for that part, and one absent Nominee holds up no one.
   - The paper says plainly that a share is of the collecting, **not of the inheritance**, or a family reads 60 : 40 as a will.
   - Percentages rather than taka, because a Venture's final value is unknown when the paper is signed.
   - Rejected: all collecting together (one absent or minor Nominee blocks everyone), and any one collecting the whole (the rest chase them).
2. **None, or up to three.** Three matches the most generous market form (MetLife; CDBL and savings certificates allow two). Because Nominees only collect, three trusted people can collect for any number of heirs.
   - None stays allowed, and the money then goes to the heirs.
   - The screen reminds the Owner at signing when an Investor has no Nominee, but never refuses to sign.
3. **A Nominee who dies before the Investor** leaves their share to the survivors in proportion to their own shares (50 : 30 : 20 with the first gone becomes 60 : 40). With none left, the money goes to the heirs as if none had been named, as in Insurance Act s.57.
   - The paper states the rule, so nobody has to tell the farm.
   - OpenFarm records no Nominee's death; applying the rule belongs to the out-of-scope payout.
4. **A Nominee under eighteen collects through a Receiver.**
   - **Every Nominee's date of birth is recorded.** This reverses the earlier "the farm doesn't record a nominee's age": the paper now shows who was a minor on the day it was signed.
   - A minor Nominee has one named **Receiver**, who collects their share until they turn eighteen, with no new paper needed at that point.
   - **The guardian line prints only for a minor Nominee**, filled with the Receiver's name, and the Receiver signs it. One signature covers both consent to holding the minor's data and receiving the money. The hand-struck line goes.
   - The Receiver is not a Nominee: no share, and not counted in the three.
5. **What is recorded:**
   - **Nominee:** name, relation to the Investor, phone, date of birth and share. No NID and no address, because the farm needs only to find and pay them, and identity is checked in person at collection.
   - **Receiver:** name, relation to the Nominee, and phone.
   - Phone stays optional for both.
6. **Per Investor**, settled by 04. **Today's single nominee** becomes the only Nominee at 100%, with the date of birth blank until a Nomination is signed. No real Investor has signed yet, so this touches only the seed.
