# External-party access

Status: resolved

Type: grilling

Blocked by: 14

Map: [OpenFarm Release 1](../map.md)

## Question

**Grilling.** Accountant, buyers, auditors, visiting vet were named as users. Decide for R1:

- Do any of them get a login, or do they receive exports/reports only?
- If logins: read-only scoped views? Time-limited?
- What does each actually need to see (ties to [Compliance reports](./19-compliance-reports-and-exports.md))?

Resolved when each external party has a decided access mode.

## Answer

Decided with the Owner on 2026-09-10. **No external party gets a login in Release 1.** The only users are Owner, Manager, Staff and Vet (full or visiting).

| Party | Access mode | What they receive |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Accountant | exports only | monthly CSV/PDF from Finance, sent by Owner/Manager |
| Milk processor / cattle buyers | documents only | sale receipt, transport-card data, and on request an animal's treatment & withdrawal summary — generated by the Manager |
| Auditors / DLS inspectors | in person | Owner/Manager show records on their own device and hand over printed/PDF compliance exports |
| Visiting vet | **user** — Vet role, visiting scope | already decided in the roles matrix |
| Suppliers, AI technicians, labour contractors, everyone else | Counterparty record | named on records; never log in |

**Consequences**

- Every external need is met by a **generated document** — so the compliance/report set ([Compliance reports and exports](./19-compliance-reports-and-exports.md)) is the whole external surface and must include the buyer-facing _animal treatment & withdrawal summary_ and the _sale receipt / transport card_.
- No public share links, no external accounts: the auth surface stays four roles. Time-limited auditor logins and buyer links are **Release 2 candidates**, recorded under Out of scope.
- AI technicians who are not Vets: the Manager records the Service naming the technician as Counterparty.
