## Question

**Inspector view decided** ([DLS farm registration evidence](./22-dls-farm-registration-evidence.md)): one Manager screen with Registration · herd count by Side/State · vaccination register · 30-day treatment register · 6-month disease history · mortality register, each PDF-exportable. This ticket fixes the exact content and format of each report, plus the buyer/accountant documents.

**External-party access decided**: nobody outside the four roles logs in, so this report set is the _entire_ external surface. It must include, beyond authority-facing reports: the buyer-facing **animal treatment & withdrawal summary**, the **sale receipt**, and the **transport-card data** (Meat Rules r.18), plus the accountant's monthly export (Finance).

**Research says** (see [Bangladesh regulatory requirements](./02-bangladesh-regulatory-requirements.md)): concrete artefacts the system should be able to produce — (a) per-animal treatment history with prescriptions and withdrawal periods, filterable to the last 30 days; (b) farm disease history for the last 6 months; (c) an animal's location for the previous 30 days (Meat Rules 2021 Schedule-7); (d) transport-card data: farm of origin, animal count, destination (r.18); (e) buyer/supplier names, addresses and invoices for milk sold (Safe Food Act s.38); (f) vaccination records in the DLS guideline template; (g) written notifiable-disease report to the ULO. Verify the Rules 2008 notifiable-disease list before finalising (g).

**Grilling.** From the regulatory findings and the health model, decide which reports R1 must produce:

- Treatment/medicine register in the format authorities or buyers expect.
- Movement/sale records.
- Vaccination and mortality records.
- Milk records for processors.
- Export formats (PDF, CSV, official form layout) and who can generate them.

Resolved when the R1 report list, each with its audience and format, is written down.

## Answer

Decided with the Owner on 2026-09-10. The **15-item report & document set** is the asset [`assets/report-set.md`](../assets/report-set.md), confirmed as drafted — each with audience, trigger, content (every field traceable to a decided record) and format.

**Groups**: Inspector view (Registration, herd summary, vaccination register, 30-day treatment register, 6-month disease history, mortality register) · per-animal (Animal Passport; buyer Treatment & Withdrawal Summary) · movement & sale (sale receipt — several animals to one buyer on one receipt; transport card data per Meat Rules r.18; movement log) · milk (dispatch record incl. buyer name/address & challan for Safe Food Act s.38; production) · statutory (**notifiable-disease letter to the ULO**) · finance (accountant export).

**Format**: **Bangla by default with English field labels alongside**, A4; registers follow the **DLS guideline column order** where one exists (treatment register: the 11-field template). Every PDF carries farm name, Registration number, generation timestamp and generating user; **every export is an Audit Event**.

**DLS disease letter**: **generated by the system** as a step of SOP 14 — pre-filled in Bangla from the Vet's diagnosis; the Manager reviews, prints/hand-delivers, and records the delivery date and any ULO reference.

**Buyer summary (R8)**: tag, photo, 30-day treatments (drug, dates), withdrawal end dates, and a **clear / not clear** statement — **no prices, no diagnosis text**.

### Consequences

- Notifications: none directly; the letter step is inside an already-immediate SOP.
- Sequencing: the Inspector view is a natural early increment — it makes the system _the_ record-keeping method from day one.
