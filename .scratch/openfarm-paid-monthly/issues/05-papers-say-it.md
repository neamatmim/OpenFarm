**What to build:** The Investment Agreement, the joining letter, the progress paper and the closing statement say the
monthly terms in the wording the lawyer and the Shariah scholar approve — and, at the end, what each paid and the Units
he held for it.

**Blocked by:** 01, the advisers' approved wording

**Status:** done (2026-10-02)

Investment Agreement carries the two rows and M1–M7 before the sale window, M8 after the heirs clause; new fields

differs). A farm whose wording in force is still exactly the previous standard, given by nobody, is caught up to the

editor names a marked line "printed only on a Venture paid by the month". Joining letter: the schedule and the

with the signed in brackets, and Monthly Sums not paid. The seed farm has "মাসে মাসে ২০২৭ ভেঞ্চার": two new Investors,
one paying each 10th, one behind on the latest month.
