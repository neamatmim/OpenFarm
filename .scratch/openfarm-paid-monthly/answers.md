## The Owner's decisions

- **Due day: the 10th** of each month; missed if unpaid by the 17th (Q5).
- **How many months: worked from the Venture's dates** — one sum for each 10th after the month of its decision date and
  before the start of its Target Window. Both dates are set when it opens, so the sum per Unit is known before anyone
  signs (the day buying actually starts is not).
- **Selling:** no monthly sums once selling starts (Q5: a missed month may be paid until then).
- **Stops for good:** his Units stay empty (Q6).

## What it means for the tickets

- 01: due day fixed at the 10th, not stored per Venture; months worked from `decideBy` and `targetWindowStart`.
- 03: sums taken while buying or fattening, refused once selling starts; a missed month may be paid late.
- 04: "late" from the 11th, "missed" from the 18th; no fee anywhere; the Owner told, the Investor reminded by the farm.
- 05: the stamp on the whole capital; the schedule in the Agreement itself. The new clauses go to the advisers as
  drafted wording before they are published as a Version (Q1 approved adding clauses, not yet their words).
- Nominees (Q8): a Nominee of a deceased Investor may pay the remaining sums — whoever pays, it is by bank against his
  Agreement, as any sum.
