# Who may do what, the phone's work, and the herd's numbers — survey of 2026-10-04

The round after the research's accessibility, speed and forms list (`docs/research/next-improvements.md`, all merged).
Three lenses no earlier survey took:

- **S:** security and Roles across the staff app (the portal had its own review, `docs/research/portal-exposure-review.md`).
- **W:** what everyday work costs on a phone, in taps and typed fields.
- **H:** the herd's productivity numbers, against the standard dairy and fattening measures.

Three read-only reviewers read the code. Claims marked **checked twice** were read again by hand afterwards. None has been
proven by a failing test yet. That is the first step of any ticket built from this
([[verify-a-bug-before-carrying-it]]).

## Recommended order

1. **S1 at once.** It is a live way into the Owner's Role, and the fix is a Role check plus a test.
2. **W3 and H1.** A wrong cow dosed, and wrong figures already on the Owner's and Investors' screens.
3. **S2, then S3 and S4** once the Owner has decided them (questions below).
4. **W1, W2, W4.** These carry the most taps a day.
5. **H2 and H3.** The money a dairy loses quietly, worked out from data the farm already records.
6. The rest as the 30 days on the farm show which matter: W5–W7, H4–H6, S5.

## Questions only the Owner can answer

- **S3.** May a Shed Phone switch to anybody but Staff? Or does the Manager (or Owner) sometimes really work from the barn phone?
- **S4.** Which parameters stay the Manager's to set? Should the Owner be told when one moves?
- **H1(5) and the Venture herd's ADG.** The herd's ADG counts only animals still standing (`venture-herd-store.ts:225`), so it drifts as the fast gainers are sold. Is that what the Investors should read?

## Looked for and already handled

- **Guards.** 357 of the 375 procedures carry a Role check. The 18 without one are the Shed Phone token, counted and locked-out code entry, and name-only reads.
- **Who reaches what.** An Investor's session reaches no staff procedure. Disabling a person expires their sessions and drops them on every request.
- **Scope and corrections.**
  - Scope is applied in animals, work, milk, feed, papers and prescriptions.
  - Staff look up any tag read-only.
  - A visiting Vet is held to their Cases.
  - All 19 corrections go through `correct()` and `mayCorrect`.
- **Requests and uploads.**
  - CSRF: requests are checked by `Sec-Fetch-Site` and Origin, and the API refuses GET.
  - Uploads: the type is limited, and they are served as `data:` with `nosniff`.
  - Rate limits apply to sign-in, PIN and codes.
- **Phone work.**
  - Feeding amounts come pre-filled from the Ration.
  - Milk goes to the tank by default and is locked under Withdrawal.
  - Skip reasons are picked, never typed.
  - The head count is blind.
  - All Entries queue offline.
- **Herd numbers.**
  - Cost per Litre is a 12-month trend.
  - ADG uses two rates, with doubted readings left out on the main paths.
  - Expected Gain is judged by Ration, breed and sex.
  - Keep-or-sell, Margin and Return on Cost per Season and Venture.
  - The cull list, heat watch and heifer watch.
  - Death rate on head-years; calf losses with stillbirths apart; early losses by seller.
