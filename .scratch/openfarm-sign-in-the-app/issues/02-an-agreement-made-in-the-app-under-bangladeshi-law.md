# An agreement made in the app, under Bangladeshi law

Status: closed

Type: research (AFK)

Blocked by: —

Map: [OpenFarm: signing in the app instead of on paper](../map.md)

## Question

Is an Investment Agreement, an Amendment or a মনোনয়নপত্র **agreed in an app** — the signed-in Investor reads the paper and
enters a one-time code sent by SMS to their phone — valid and provable under Bangladeshi law, and how is its **stamp
duty** met?

- Contract Act 1872: offer and acceptance made electronically.
- ICT Act 2006 (and any later amendment or the Digital/Cyber Security Acts): "electronic record", "electronic signature"
  versus "digital signature" (certifying authority), the legal recognition sections, and any **exclusions** (instruments
  that may not be made electronically).
- Evidence Act 1872 as amended in 2022: digital evidence, and what proves an electronic agreement in court.
- Stamp Act 1899: is an electronic instrument chargeable, can its duty be paid by **e-challan** (and how is it then
  "stamped"), is there e-stamping in Bangladesh, and what does s.35 mean for an instrument with no stamp.
- Any Bangladesh Bank, BSEC or BFIU rule that treats an SMS OTP as a signature (e-KYC, digital accounts) — as practice
  to point the lawyer at, not as law that binds the farm.

Cite sections and dates, and say what is uncertain. Findings at `docs/research/agreeing-in-the-app-bangladeshi-law.md`.

## Resolution

Findings: [Agreeing in the app, under Bangladeshi law](../../../docs/research/agreeing-in-the-app-bangladeshi-law.md)
(2026-10-08).

- **Valid and provable.** Acceptance may be "by any act" (Contract Act ss.3–4, 10). The ICT Act 2006 recognizes
  electronic records (s.6) and excludes no kind of document; s.13(3)(ক) lets the farm attribute a record to the Investor
  where it used a procedure the Investor agreed to beforehand — so that procedure belongs in the paper Portal Consent.
  The Evidence Act as amended in 2022 admits a digital record with a certificate (s.65B) but presumes nothing about who
  sent a message (s.88A): the farm must prove who entered the code.
- **Stamp duty cannot be met electronically today.** No rule in force lets duty be paid onto an electronic instrument
  (Stamp Act ss.2(11), 10); the challan routes cover documents being registered and RJSC filings. Stamp duty by
  A-challan is decided but not gazetted (reported for 1 July 2027). Unstamped, an Agreement cannot be used in court or
  before the Arbitrator until duty plus ten times it is paid (s.35: Tk 3,300 at Tk 300 duty); executing it unstamped is
  an offense fined up to Tk 500 (s.62).
- **Regulators use an SMS code to check the phone, never as the signature** (Bangladesh Bank BRPD-1 Circulars 11/2026
  and 08/2026; BFIU Circular 29/2026).
- **A Nomination is probably not chargeable** — the easiest paper to move.
- **Side-finding, beyond this map:** paper Agreements whose duty was paid by e-challan may not be "duly stamped" unless a
  registering officer or the Collector endorsed them within a month (s.32).
