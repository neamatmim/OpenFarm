# Agreeing in the app, in Shariah

Status: closed

Type: research (AFK)

Blocked by: —

Map: [OpenFarm: signing in the app instead of on paper](../map.md)

## Question

Does a **mudarabah** contract — and a nomination of who collects on death — made **electronically** (the offer read in an
app, accepted by entering a code) meet Shariah's requirements?

- Offer and acceptance (ijab and qabul) across distance and in writing; the "same session" (majlis) condition for
  contracts made online.
- Writing and witnesses (Al-Baqarah 2:282): obligatory or recommended for a mudarabah, and whether an electronic record
  serves as the writing.
- AAOIFI standards on online financial dealings (Shari'ah Standard No. 38) and on mudarabah (No. 13), and any Islamic
  Fiqh Academy resolution on contracting by modern means.
- What Islamic banks in Bangladesh do for mudarabah accounts opened digitally (e.g. e-KYC accounts).

Cite the standards and resolutions, and say what is uncertain. Findings at `docs/research/agreeing-in-the-app-in-shariah.md`.

## Resolution

Findings: [Agreeing in the app, in Shariah](../../../docs/research/agreeing-in-the-app-in-shariah.md) (2026-10-08).

- **A mudarabah agreed in the app is valid in principle, and so is a Nomination.** Islamic Fiqh Academy Res. 52 (3/6),
  1990, confirmed for electronic contracts by Res. 230 (1/24), 2019: a contract made in writing, the screen included, is
  complete once offer and acceptance reach the parties; only marriage, sarf and salam are excluded. Wills and agency need
  no same session, so a Nomination doesn't either.
- **Writing and witnesses are recommended, not required**, for a mudarabah (AAOIFI SS 13 sets no writing rule; the capital
  is a trust, so 2:282 applies as prudence). The record kept in the app counts as the writing.
- **AAOIFI SS 38:** any form of consent counts (5/1); a confirmation step is advised (5/4/1) — the one-time code is one;
  e-signatures are acceptable where local law allows (8/2/2).
- **Practice:** Islami Bank's CellFin opens Mudaraba accounts with no branch visit, a PIN serving as the signature
  (BFIU e-KYC), the nominee taken in the same form.

**For the route (ticket 07):** under SS 38 5/3 the Owner's approval coming last makes the Investor's agreement the
*offer*, which they may withdraw until the Owner approves — the portal has no way to withdraw today. Also carried: a
Nomination read as appointing a trustee to collect after death; a Receiver who is not the minor's guardian; approving an
Amendment one Investor at a time.
