# How others take a nomination without paper

Status: closed

Type: research (AFK)

Blocked by: —

Map: [OpenFarm: signing in the app instead of on paper](../map.md)

## Question

How do Bangladeshi institutions take a **nominee designation** — and a minor nominee's guardian — **without a wet
signature**, and what do they treat as the holder's consent?

- Banks under the BFIU e-KYC guideline: nominee details and photo in a digital account opening.
- CDBL / BO accounts opened online; National Savings Certificates (Sanchayapatra) bought online; life insurers' digital
  policies; mobile financial services (bKash, Nagad) if they take nominees.
- How a **minor's guardian** is named and consents in each.

Builds on [Nominees in Bangladeshi law and Shariah](../../../docs/research/nominees-in-bangladeshi-law-and-shariah.md);
don't repeat it. Findings at `docs/research/nomination-without-paper.md`.

## Resolution

Findings: [Nomination without paper](../../../docs/research/nomination-without-paper.md) (2026-10-08).

- Every digital channel takes a nomination as **the holder's act alone**: the holder (or the institution's agent) types
  the Nominee's details; the Nominee never signs and is never contacted.
- **Bangladesh Bank's e-KYC guideline (March 2026)** accepts a PIN as the customer's signature for low-risk accounts,
  with two-factor sign-in or a one-time code to the registered phone, and the logs kept.
- **bKash** takes up to two nominees in the app with shares; each needs an NID, which shuts out minors.
- **A minor's guardian** is where every channel stops or goes back to paper: National Savings appoints one by a signed
  letter; no institution was found taking a guardian's consent by SMS.

For this map: a code to the Investor's registered phone inside their signed-in portal matches what regulated
institutions accept for low-risk products; keep the trail (who, when, which phone/email, the paper's fingerprint, the
address) and send a confirmation; never involve the Nominee; keep a minor's Nomination on paper, as the market does.
