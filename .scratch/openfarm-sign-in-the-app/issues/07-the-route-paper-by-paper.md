# The route, paper by paper

Status: closed

Assignee: Claude (with the Owner)

Type: grilling (HITL)

Blocked by: 02, 03, 04

Map: [OpenFarm: signing in the app instead of on paper](../map.md)

## Question

With the research in and the advisers not asked, what is OpenFarm's recommendation for each paper in scope — the
Investment Agreement and the Amendment, the মনোনয়নপত্র, a minor's Receiver — agreed in the app or kept on paper, how the
one-time code (SMS and email) seals it, what the agreed paper and the trail record of it, what the Agreement says about how it was agreed, and
what (if anything) about stamp duty changes? The Owner confirms or corrects; the answer is what the build is planned
from.

## Resolution

Decided with the Owner, 2026-10-08, on OpenFarm's recommendation (the advisers not asked); the why is
[ADR 0022](../../../docs/adr/0022-papers-are-agreed-in-the-app-with-a-one-time-code.md).

1. **The Investment Agreement and the Amendment are agreed in the app now**, the stamp risk accepted: valid and provable,
   but not stamped, so before a court or the Arbitrator the farm pays the duty and ten times it then (Stamp Act s.35),
   and signing unstamped is finable up to Tk 500 (s.62). Revisited once stamp duty can be paid electronically (A-challan,
   reported for July 2027). Stamped paper stays available.
2. **The মনোনয়নপত্র is agreed in the app** — written by the Owner and offered, as an Agreement is — except one naming a
   minor, which stays on paper (no institution takes a guardian's consent by SMS).
3. **Sealed by a one-time code** sent by SMS and by email; entering either seals it.
4. **The Portal Consent gains a clause**: a code the farm sends to the Investor's phone or email, entered by them in the
   portal, is their signature on the paper they agree to there (ICT Act s.13(3)(ক)). An Investor who signed the earlier
   consent signs the new clause at their next visit and stays on paper until then.
5. **The Investor may withdraw their agreement** in the portal until the Owner approves (AAOIFI SS 38 5/3).
6. **The farm keeps the proof** with every agreement: who, when, which channel and destination (partly hidden), the
   device's address, the agreed paper's fingerprint; and sends a confirmation by SMS and email, with the paper's number,
   once the Owner approves.
7. **An Investor's email** is written on their record by the Owner, optionally, at the first meeting, and confirmed once
   by a code before any agreement code goes to it.
