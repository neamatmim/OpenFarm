# Flow and productivity survey — 2026-10-13

The Owner asked: "analyze the full app functionality, flow for any confusion, also check is there anything we could
improve, extend or add so that the system would be more productive."

Six read-only reviews, one per area (daily work and roles; herd, health and trade; milk and feed; the Farm's money and
reports; Ventures, Investors and the portal; setup, people and compliance). Earlier surveys hunted defects; this one
looked for confusion, repeated typing, and work the farm does with no place in the app. Every finding cites the code it
was read from; the ones marked **✔** were checked a second time by hand. Nothing here repeats what was decided against
(portal payments, a rate a year, WhatsApp, a second farm) — Release 2 candidates are marked _(R2 listed)_ where a
finding sharpens them.
