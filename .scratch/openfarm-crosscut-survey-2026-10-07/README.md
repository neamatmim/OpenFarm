## Decisions

Asked 2026-10-07; the Owner chose the recommended answer to each:

- **Voids (C1, C7):** whoever wrote it, within their Correction Window, and the Owner at any time, as a Receivable Payment works. An Investor's capital payment (C2): the Owner alone.
- **After a Settlement (C12):** a Sale on a settled Venture is corrected; the difference becomes a Settlement Adjustment from or to the Farm's books, and the closed Venture Account is never touched.
- **Fixing on the phone (C17, U4, U16):** while an entry is still on the phone it is replaced there; once sent, its Correction waits in the Outbox behind it. The Manager's own forms (a death, a Sale, a dose not prescribed) stay online-only and say plainly "no signal, not saved", keeping the form open.
- **Safety pushes:** a notifiable disease pushes and wakes the farm at any hour; withdrawal-ending pushes to the Manager and her Pen's milkers but waits for morning in quiet hours. SMS stays as now.

Defaults taken without asking, as the obvious reading:

- A Lost write-off against the wrong tag is voided as a death is, the made-good money back to the Farm.
- A count is not told to the person who made it (news of one's own act).
- Agreement typos (Units, stamp, Arbitrator) are correctable by the Owner until the first capital is taken on that Agreement.
- The Staff Correction Window stays two hours from receipt.
- Native date and time boxes stay.
