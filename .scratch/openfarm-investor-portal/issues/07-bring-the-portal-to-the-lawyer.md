- add the consent sheet, if ticket 08 settles on paper.

**Joining and paying through the portal** (asked by the Owner, 2026-09-25, and held back until these are answered). The same questions go on ticket 11's checklist:
- [ ] **Upcoming Ventures:** may the portal show a Venture still gathering capital to the farm's existing Investors only, and let them say they are interested? Or is that an offer of investment made online, which undoes the closed-circle, no-advertising footing and bears on the BSEC question?
- [ ] **How to pay:** may the portal show the Venture Account's bank details and the reference to use, for capital an Investor has already signed a stamped Agreement for? They would pay from their own bank, and the Owner would record it as now. Is that information about their own contract, or a payment channel?
- [ ] **Paying inside the portal:** would taking an Investor's payment in the portal (card, mobile financial services, a payment gateway) make OpenFarm a payment system needing Bangladesh Bank approval (PSS Act 2024 s.15(2))? Can it be squared with bank channels only, through the Venture Account, and with "no capital without a stamped Agreement"?

The Owner's part is the meeting. Resolved when the lawyer's written answer on the portal is in hand. The answer records:
- what the lawyer said
- where the written opinion is kept
- what it changes: the portal stays shut, opens as built, or opens with changes
- whether joining or paying may ever go through the portal, and on what terms.
