## Question

**Task (HITL).** Ticket 11 of the investor-projects map, [Take the structure to a lawyer and a Shariah scholar](../../openfarm-investor-projects/issues/11-take-the-structure-to-a-lawyer-and-a-shariah-scholar.md), asks only "would a future investor login?" be a platform. The portal now exists. So the Owner brings the lawyer the portal as it actually is:
- the preview (ticket 03), or a test Investor, walked through with the lawyer
- ADR 0007's list of what it does and never does
- the notice and privacy wording (ticket 06)
- the data-protection findings, [ticket 01](./01-what-bangladesh-data-protection-law-asks-of-the-portal.md), and how consent is taken (ticket 08).

The agent's part, done AFK before the meeting:
- extend ticket 11's lawyer checklist with the portal's specific questions
- print a one-page summary of the portal for the lawyer, into `assets/`.
- carry over the data-protection research's seven questions (section 14), including the two it flagged: whether the National Data Management Act's s.42 fine applies now, and whether a Singapore server is a transfer abroad under s.29
- add the consent sheet, if ticket 08 settles on paper.

**Joining and paying through the portal** (asked by the Owner, 2026-09-25, and held back until these are answered). The same questions go on ticket 11's checklist:
- [ ] **Upcoming Ventures:** may the portal show a Venture still gathering capital to the farm's existing Investors only, and let them say they are interested? Or is that an offer of investment made online, which undoes the closed-circle, no-advertising footing and bears on the BSEC question?
- [ ] **How to pay:** may the portal show the Venture Account's bank details and the reference to use, for capital an Investor has already signed a stamped Agreement for? They would pay from their own bank, and the Owner would record it as now. Is that information about their own contract, or a payment channel?
- [ ] **Paying inside the portal:** would taking an Investor's payment in the portal (card, mobile financial services, a payment gateway) make OpenFarm a payment system needing Bangladesh Bank approval (PSS Act 2024 s.15(2))? Can it be squared with bank channels only, through the Venture Account, and with "no capital without a stamped Agreement"?

The Owner's part is the meeting. Resolved when the lawyer's written answer on the portal is in hand. The answer records:
- what the lawyer said
- where the written opinion is kept
- what it changes: the portal stays shut, opens as built, or opens with changes
- whether joining or paying may ever go through the portal, and on what terms.

## Answer so far (2026-09-25, verbal; written opinion to follow)

The Owner reports the lawyer's answer. **Because the portal stays by invitation only:**
- **acceptable:** the portal as built;
- **acceptable:** showing a Venture still gathering capital to invited Investors, and letting them say they want to join;
- **acceptable:** showing the Venture Account's bank details and reference for capital they have signed for;
- **not approved:** taking payment inside the portal. It stays out.

Recorded as [ADR 0008](../../../docs/adr/0008-invited-investors-see-ventures-raising-capital-and-how-to-pay.md).

**This ticket stays open until the written opinion is in hand**, and is then resolved with where that opinion is kept. The data-protection questions carried over from ticket 01 were not reported as answered, and still need answers. Until the written opinion arrives, the portal stays switched off for real Investors.
