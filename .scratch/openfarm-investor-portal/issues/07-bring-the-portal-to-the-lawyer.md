## Answer so far (2026-09-25, verbal; written opinion to follow)

The Owner reports the lawyer's answer. **Because the portal stays by invitation only:**

- **acceptable:** the portal as built;
- **acceptable:** showing a Venture still gathering capital to invited Investors, and letting them say they want to join;
- **acceptable:** showing the Venture Account's bank details and reference for capital they have signed for;
- **not approved:** taking payment inside the portal. It stays out.

Recorded as [ADR 0008](../../../docs/adr/0008-invited-investors-see-ventures-raising-capital-and-how-to-pay.md).

**The shape of joining, designed 2026-09-25, for the written opinion to cover.** The verbal answer was to general questions; these are the screens it now has to cover. The terms are in CONTEXT.md's **Request to Join**, **Pay-in Code**, **Investor Portal** and **Venture Account** entries.

- [ ] **What is shown of an open Venture:** only one the Owner chose to show, to every invited Investor. It shows the terms, including the split the farm signs on today, the refund and loss rules, and a few words of the Owner's. It shows no projection, no past result, no "Units left" and nobody else's business. Is the Owner's own description a risk, even with the warning where they write it?
- [ ] **The notice on every page** changes from "not an offer to invest" to roughly: "This is not a public offer. Joining is only by an Agreement signed in person with the farm, and no money moves through this portal." What exact words does the lawyer want?
- [ ] **A Request to Join** records whole Units and a note, and binds nobody. The Owner answers "come and sign" or "not this time". Does a recorded "come and sign" risk reading as an acceptance, forming a contract before the stamped paper?
- [ ] **The bank details** show only on the Investor's own signed Agreement, never beside an open Venture. They come with the amount still owed, a Pay-in Code, the decide-by day, and "the farm will never ask you to pay anywhere else". Is that the line the lawyer meant?

**This ticket stays open until the written opinion is in hand**, and is then resolved with where that opinion is kept. The data-protection questions carried over from ticket 01 were not reported as answered, and still need answers. Until the written opinion arrives, the portal stays switched off for real Investors.
