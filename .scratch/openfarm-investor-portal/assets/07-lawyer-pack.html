1 · What the portal is

The OpenFarm Investor Portal

As built and decided on 25 September 2026. Switched off, and open to no real Investor, until your written opinion is in hand.

What it is. A read-only website. An Investor who has signed a stamped mudarabah Investment Agreement with the farm uses it to read their own record:

  • the Ventures they are in
  • how the animals are doing today: head count, weights, and spending against budget
  • their capital and payouts
  • their three papers: the joining letter, the progress statement and the settlement statement

Who gets in: by invitation only, one person at a time

  • The Owner invites an existing Investor.
  • The Investor signs a consent sheet in front of the Owner and receives a printed Welcome Letter, with a one-time code on a tear-off slip.
  • They choose their own password using their phone number and the code.
  • No account exists for anyone the Owner has not invited, and there is no public sign-up.
  • The Owner can take anyone's access away, and switches the whole portal off with one farm setting. It is off today.

What an invited Investor may do: read, and two things more

  1. See a Venture still gathering capital, if the Owner has chosen to show it.
    • They see its terms: the Unit price, target, floor, dates, budgets and split.
    • They see its rules: every taka back if the floor is missed, a loss comes off capital, nothing is guaranteed, and joining is only by signing in person.
    • They never see a projection, a past result, how many Units are left, or anybody else's business.
  2. Make a Request to Join it: whole Units and a note.
    • It binds nobody. Only the Owner is told, and answers “come and sign” or “not this time”.
    • Joining is still only by signing a stamped Agreement in person.

Once an Investor has signed, the portal shows them the Venture Account's bank details, the amount still owed and a reference code for their transfer. It adds the warning that the farm never asks anyone to pay anywhere else.

What it never does

  • It takes no money: no card, mobile wallet or gateway.
  • Capital arrives by bank into the Venture Account.
  • Nothing is signed in it.
  • Nothing is advertised or referred, and no return is promised.
  • It never shows one Investor another's business.

Every page says: “This portal is not a public offer. Joining a Venture is only by an Agreement signed in person with the farm, and no money moves through this portal.”

Their data

  • What they see. The NID and bank account show only their last four digits. Corrections are made by the Owner, never in the portal.
  • The notice. A privacy notice, “আপনার তথ্য” (page 3), is handed over at signing and on the Welcome Letter, and is also a page in the portal.
  • Requests. Investors make them to the Owner in writing, and get an answer within 30 days.
  • Consent. Consent to the portal is signed on paper and filed (page 5). Withdrawing it ends access at once.
  • Signing in. A sign-in lasts 12 hours, and signing out leaves nothing on the phone.
  • The address. The portal has its own address, investors.{farm-domain}, apart from the farm's own app.
  • The record. Every paper an Investor opens is recorded.

You answered verbally on 25 September 2026. The farm now asks for your written opinion on the questions on page 2.

2 এই তথ্য সিঙ্গাপুরে খামারের পক্ষে চালানো একটি সার্ভারে রাখা হয়, আর প্রতি রাতে এর একটি তালাবদ্ধ (এনক্রিপ্ট করা) কপি অন্য জায়গায় রাখা হয়।
This data is kept on a server in Singapore run for the Farm, and an encrypted copy is kept elsewhere each night.
3 খামারে বিনিয়োগকারীর ব্যক্তিগত তথ্য শুধু মালিক দেখেন। টাকা পাঠাতে ব্যাংক এবং আইন চাইলে কর কর্তৃপক্ষ ছাড়া আর কাউকে তা দেওয়া হয় না।
At the Farm, only the Owner sees the Investor's personal data. It is given to nobody but the bank, to make payments, and the tax authority where the law requires.
4 শেষ ভেঞ্চারের হিসাব মেটার পর বারো বছর এই তথ্য রাখা হবে, কারণ আইন খামারকে এতদিন হিসাবের খাতা রাখতে বলে।
It is kept for twelve years after the last Venture settles, because the law asks the Farm to keep its books that long.
5 বিনিয়োগকারী যেকোনো সময় মালিককে লিখে তাঁর তথ্যের কপি চাইতে, ভুল ঠিক করাতে বা মুছে ফেলতে বলতে পারেন; আইন যা রাখতে বলে তা ছাড়া। বিস্তারিত “আপনার তথ্য” কাগজে আছে, যা এই চুক্তির সঙ্গে দেওয়া হলো।
The Investor may at any time write to the Owner to have a copy of their data, have it corrected, or have it erased, except what the law requires be kept. The details are in “আপনার তথ্য”, handed over with this Agreement.
6 খামারের অনলাইন পোর্টালে নিজের হিসাব দেখানো এই চুক্তির অংশ নয়; তা হবে শুধু বিনিয়োগকারী আলাদা সম্মতিপত্রে সই করলে।
Showing the Investor their own record in the Farm's online portal is not part of this Agreement; it happens only if the Investor signs a separate consent.

Beside the nominee row

7 বিনিয়োগকারী নিশ্চিত করছেন যে তাঁর নমিনি জানেন, খামার তাঁর নাম, ফোন ও সম্পর্ক রাখছে, শুধু এই চুক্তির অধীনে বিনিয়োগকারীর উত্তরাধিকারীদের টাকা দেওয়ার জন্য।
The Investor confirms that their nominee knows the Farm holds the nominee's name, phone and relationship, only to pay the Investor's heirs under this Agreement.
8 নমিনির বয়স আঠারো বছরের কম। তাঁর বাবা / মা / আইনগত অভিভাবক হিসেবে আমি এই তথ্য রাখায় সম্মতি দিচ্ছি। নাম: ____________ সই: ____________
The nominee is under eighteen. As their father / mother / legal guardian I consent to this data being kept. Name: ____________ Signature: ____________
Printed every time; struck through when it does not apply.
