# OpenFarm Investor Portal — one-page summary for the lawyer

_{Farm name}, {registration}. As built and decided, 25 September 2026. Not yet open to any real Investor._

**What it is.** A read-only website where an Investor who has signed a stamped mudarabah Investment Agreement with the farm reads their own record: the Ventures they are in, how the animals are doing today (head, weights, spend against budget), their capital and payouts, and their three papers (joining letter, progress statement, settlement statement).

**Who gets in: by invitation only, one person at a time.**

- The Owner invites an existing Investor.
- The Investor signs a consent sheet in front of the Owner and receives a printed Welcome Letter, with a one-time code on a tear-off slip.
- They choose their own password with their phone number and the code.
- No account exists for anyone the Owner has not invited, and there is no public sign-up.
- The Owner can take anyone's access away, and **switches the whole portal off** as one farm setting. It is off today.

**What an invited Investor may do: read only, and two small things more.**

1. **See a Venture still gathering capital**, if the Owner chose to show it. They see its terms (Unit price, target, floor, dates, budgets, split) and its rules (every taka back if the floor is missed, a loss comes off capital, nothing guaranteed, joining only by signing in person). They never see a projection, a past result, "Units left", or anybody else's business.
2. **Make a Request to Join** it: whole Units and a note. It binds nobody. The Owner alone is told, and answers "come and sign" or "not this time". Joining is still only by signing a stamped Agreement in person.

Once they have signed, the portal shows the **Venture Account's bank details**, the amount still owed and a reference code for their transfer, with the warning that the farm never asks anyone to pay anywhere else.

**What it never does.**

- It takes no money: no card, mobile wallet or gateway. Capital arrives by bank into the Venture Account, and the Owner records it.
- Nothing is signed in it.
- Nothing is advertised or referred, and no return is promised.
- It never shows one Investor another's business.
- Every page says: _"This portal is not a public offer. Joining a Venture is only by an Agreement signed in person with the farm, and no money moves through this portal."_

**Their data.**

- **Masking:** the NID and bank account show only their last four digits. Corrections are made by the Owner, never in the portal.
- **Notice:** "আপনার তথ্য" is given on paper at signing and on the Welcome Letter, and is also a page in the portal. It covers what is held, why, Singapore hosting with an encrypted backup, twelve years, and the Investor's rights.
- **Requests:** they are made to the Owner in writing, and answered within 30 days.
- **Consent to the portal:** signed on paper and filed. Withdrawing it ends access at once.
- **Sign-in:** a sign-in lasts 12 hours, and signing out leaves nothing on the phone.
- **Address:** the portal has its own address, `investors.{farm-domain}`, apart from the farm's own app.
- **Trail:** every paper an Investor opens is recorded.

**What the farm asks in writing.** Detailed questions are attached:

1. Is the portal, so limited, clear of being a payment system or platform (PSS Act 2024 s.15(2)) and of being an offer of securities?
2. Do the joining screens stay on the right side of the line? This covers the Owner's own words on a Venture, the notice, and whether "come and sign" could read as acceptance.
3. Does the data wording meet the Personal Data Protection Act 2026?

_Answered verbally on 25 September 2026. The farm now asks for the written opinion._
