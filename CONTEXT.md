# OpenFarm

An SOP-driven operations system for a single cattle farm in Bangladesh that both produces milk and fattens cattle for sale. It holds the farm's records and enforces the farm's standard procedures.

## Language

**Farm**: The single operating unit the system serves today — one owner, one set of records, one set of SOPs. Modelled so a second Farm could exist later, but only one exists now. _Avoid_: Tenant, organisation, account

**SOP**: A standard operating procedure — a written, versioned procedure the farm expects staff to follow, which the system turns into scheduled or triggered work and records who completed it. Retired by the Owner, never removed: a retired SOP raises no more work, its work nobody has started is **Called Off**, and nothing is published or proposed to it; its Versions, card and the work done under it are kept, and the Owner may bring it back. _Avoid_: Playbook, protocol, checklist (a checklist is one _form_ an SOP step can take, not the SOP itself)

**Playbook**: The farm's full set of SOPs taken together. Refers to the collection, never to an individual procedure.

**Standard Playbook**: The SOPs OpenFarm offers a farm to start from. None is in the farm's Playbook until the Owner adopts it — reads it, names the farm's own Pen or product where it asks for one, and publishes it — so none raises work before then. A standard SOP the farm already has by name is not offered again. _Avoid_: Template, default SOPs

**Standard Lists**: The Feed Items, Rations, Drug List products and notifiable diseases the Owner may start the farm with at Setup instead of an empty store. The fattening Rations go by weight, calf to heaviest bull, each with its Weight Band and a note of the published standards its figures were worked out from. Names, kilos and bands only: a price, a stock level, the Pen a Ration feeds and a product's withdrawal days are the farm's own facts, and the days the Vet's alone — so a standard product waits on the Drug List, not prescribable, until the Vet writes them. A name the farm already uses is left as the farm's, and starting twice adds nothing. Like the standard Categories, which every farm is given without asking. _Avoid_: Seed data, defaults, presets

**Dairy**: The side of the farm that keeps cows for milk. _Avoid_: Milk production, milking (milking is an activity within Dairy)

**Fattening**: The side of the farm that buys or raises cattle to gain weight and sells them for beef. _Avoid_: Beef, finishing, feedlot

**Release 1**: The first version of OpenFarm the farm will run. This map ends in its spec. _Avoid_: MVP, v1, phase 1

## People

**Owner**: The person who owns the Farm, sets the Playbook, and can see and approve everything — and may do anything the Farm Manager does, recording and signing off included. A Vet's clinical acts stay the Vet's. _Avoid_: Admin, boss

**Farm Manager**: The person who runs the Farm day to day: assigns SOP work, signs it off, and keeps the records honest. _Avoid_: Supervisor, admin

**Barn Staff**: The people who do SOP work at the animal — milkers, feeders, herdsmen. They record on phones, often without signal. _Avoid_: Worker, labourer, operator

**Vet**: A veterinarian or para-vet, in-house or visiting, who diagnoses, prescribes, and records treatments. _Avoid_: Doctor, animal health worker

## Animals

**Animal**: One individual head of cattle the Farm is responsible for, from arrival or birth until sale or death. Belongs to Dairy or Fattening at any given time, and to one owner at a time — the Farm, or the **Venture** whose money bought her. Her owner is set by her **Intake**, correctable inside the **Correction Window**, and moved afterwards only by an **Internal Sale**; a calf born here is the Farm's, and a Venture's animal may not cross to Dairy. _Avoid_: Cow (a cow is a female that has calved — a specific kind of Animal), head, stock

**Tag Number**: An Animal's permanent identity: a side-of-origin prefix and a running number (`D-0001`, `F-0001`), assigned at birth or intake — or, on the opening register, read off the Ear Tag the herd was tagged with — never reused, unchanged if the Animal changes Side. _Avoid_: ID, animal number, ear-tag number

**Ear Tag**: The physical tag carrying the Tag Number. Can be lost and replaced; a replacement carries the same Tag Number and the Re-tag is recorded. _Avoid_: Tag (ambiguous with Tag Number)

**Official Tag**: Any government or pilot-scheme tag an Animal also carries. Recorded as an attribute; never the identity. _Avoid_: Government ID, national ID

**Re-tag**: The recorded event of replacing a lost or unreadable Ear Tag with one carrying the same Tag Number. _Avoid_: Re-numbering (which never happens)

**Breed**: One of the farm's list of breeds an Animal is written down under — the standard ones every farm is given (local cattle, the local breeds BLRI recognises, the imported breeds and their crosses) and the farm's own. An Animal names the Breed, not its words, so renaming a Breed renames it on every Animal. Retired, never removed; optional on an Animal, since nobody must know it to write her down. The opening register names one in either of its languages, and refuses a name the list does not have. A Breed is **deshi** — the country's own cattle, which put on less than a cross on the same feed, so an Animal of it is judged against the farm's deshi share of her Ration's **Expected Gain** — or it is not: the standard local cattle and local breeds come deshi, the crosses and imported breeds do not, and the farm says for its own and may put a standard one right. _Avoid_: Type, variety, jat (in English text); local (as a flag — Local is one standard Breed)

**Arrival**: How an Animal came to be on the Farm — born here at a Calving, bought in at an Intake, or already standing when the Farm opened its register. Derived from the Move that put her in her first Pen, never entered. _Avoid_: Entry (an Entry is what a phone sends the Farm), admission, onboarding

**Exit**: How an Animal left and when — Sold, Died, Culled or Lost — with what belongs to that way of going: who bought her and where she went, what she died of and what was done with her, or why the Owner wrote her off. Derived from her State and the records of it, never entered; null while she is still here. Every paper and page says it the same way, because it is worked out once. _Avoid_: Departure, removal, disposal (that is what was done with a carcass)

## Work

**Milking Session**: One of the two daily times the Dairy herd is milked — early morning and afternoon. The milking SOP runs once per Milking Session. _Avoid_: Milking time, shift

**Trigger**: What causes an SOP to fall due: a schedule (a time), an event (something happened), or an animal's state (a condition became true). One SOP may have more than one. What happened before a Version was published raises no work under it — except that a procedure published for the first time **catches up** with animals already on their way, raising the work still ahead of the publishing (a bull twenty days into Quarantine is released ten days on) and never work that would already be overdue. _Avoid_: Schedule (that's one kind of Trigger), reminder

## SOPs

**SOP Definition**: The authored procedure: its name, purpose, Triggers, assigned role, checker role, due time and grace, and ordered Steps. What the Owner edits. _Avoid_: Template, workflow

**SOP Version**: An immutable snapshot of an SOP Definition. Every change creates a new one; history shows which Version was followed. _Avoid_: Revision, edit

**SOP Instance**: One occurrence of an SOP falling due — e.g. morning milking on a given day. Assigned to a role, claimable by a person, reviewed by the checker. Most are in a Pen, one for each Pen holding an animal the SOP concerns — so a farm with none raises none. Work about the whole farm, like the Registration's renewal or an SOP marked for the whole farm (the daily biosecurity check), is in none, and raised once. _Avoid_: Task (too generic), job, run

**Step**: One ordered item inside an SOP. May repeat per animal in the Instance's group. Requires Evidence; may write a farm record or change an animal's state. _Avoid_: Checkpoint, action

**Step Completion**: The recorded act of doing one Step (once per animal if the Step repeats): who, when, and the Evidence. _Avoid_: Tick, log, entry (the general word — a Step Completion is one kind of **Entry**)

**Skipped**: A Step passed over with one of the reasons its Version wrote against it, rather than done — a dose the medicine ran out for, a second service a heat did not need. A Step done animal by animal may always skip the animal; a Step done once for the Pen only where its Effect allows it. Said of a **Step**, never of an **Instance**: an Instance nobody did becomes **Missed**. A Step with no reasons written against it cannot be skipped, because the reason is chosen from that list and there is nowhere to type another.

**Evidence**: What a Step requires to count as done: a tick, a number with unit, a choice from a list, a photo, or a note. Required or optional per Step. _Avoid_: Proof, data, field

**Step Shape**: What a Step that feeds a later act has to ask for, and in what order. Most Steps are the Owner's to word as they like; a few are not, because what a Service, a Calving, a Pregnancy Check, a DLS report or a Campaign's Lot Number records is read back by the farm afterwards. A Shape names each answer it asks for — what kind it is, whether the Step must insist on it, may not, or the farm decides, and for a choice the fixed words the record reads back under whatever the farm calls them. Said once: the phone drafts a Step from it, publishing refuses one that does not keep it, and the Effect reads each answer by its name. _Avoid_: Schema, contract, form

**Effect**: What completing a Step writes into the farm's records beyond the Evidence itself — a Milk Record, a Weigh-in, a dose. Runs in the same transaction as the Step Completion and is keyed on it, so a corrected entry replaces what it wrote. When the farm has moved past what the Step says — she has been walked on since, her calves have left — the Effect stands aside and writes nothing: a Step arriving so is a late Entry, kept for a person, and a Correction so is kept and put in front of the Manager as Needs Review. _Avoid_: Side effect, hook, trigger (which is how an SOP falls due)

**Gate**: A rule by which an animal's state blocks a Step or an SOP from completing — e.g. milk withdrawal blocks that cow's milk from bulk; meat withdrawal blocks her sale. A hard block, not a warning. _Avoid_: Validation, warning, lock

**Sign-off**: The checker's review of a completed SOP Instance: approve, or send back with a reason. _Avoid_: Approval (one outcome of Sign-off), verification

**The Day Turning**: Everything the Farm does because time has passed rather than because somebody did something: work falling due, a visiting Vet's days running out, Alerts swept for work gone late and holds ending and feed running low, and the evening's Digest carried. Idempotent, and run both by the server's own timer and by whoever opens the app — so a farm whose server is asleep is a farm that catches up the moment somebody looks at it, and neither run does anything twice. _Avoid_: Schedule (that is one kind of Trigger), cron, job

**Grace**: The minutes after an Instance's due time before it counts as Overdue. Set per SOP. _Avoid_: Buffer, slack, tolerance (which is milk's word)

**Overdue** / **Missed**: An Instance past its due time and Grace is Overdue and the Manager is alerted. It becomes Missed only when the Manager closes it with a reason. Nothing disappears on its own. _Avoid_: Expired, skipped, failed

**Called Off**: An Instance the farm no longer owes, closed by what changed rather than by a person: the animal it was for left the farm, the Heat that raised it was taken back, her calving moved, the report it would deliver was withdrawn, the SOP it follows was retired before anybody started it. Not Missed and not outstanding — nobody fell short. Named in the trail with what called it off, and raised again if its cause comes back; Missed work never is, and neither is work a retired SOP left — bringing the SOP back raises its work afresh from the next time it is due. _Avoid_: Withdrawn (the Treatment Gate's word), cancelled, Missed (the Manager's close)

## Herd structure

**Side**: Which half of the farm an Animal currently belongs to: Dairy or Fattening. Exactly one at a time; changing Side is a recorded move. _Avoid_: Department, unit, type

**State**: Where an Animal is in its lifecycle. Dairy: Calf, Heifer, Pregnant Heifer, Milking, Dry. Fattening: Quarantine, Fattening, Ready for Sale. Exits: Sold, Died, Culled, Lost. Exactly one at a time. _Avoid_: Status, stage, category

**Shed**: A building on the Farm containing Pens. _Avoid_: Barn, house, unit

**Pen**: A physical enclosure inside a Shed. Every Animal is in exactly one Pen; SOP Instances run per Pen or per Shed. A Pen may be marked a quarantine pen by the Owner or the Manager: where a bought animal comes in and is kept until she is released — never unmarked while it holds an animal in Quarantine. _Avoid_: Group (a Pen _is_ the group), lot, batch

**Move**: The recorded event of an Animal changing Pen — including a change of Side. The only way an Animal's location changes. An Animal in Quarantine is walked only into a quarantine **Pen**, however the Move comes — by hand, from a phone's late Batch, or by a Step; one left outside one from before pens were marked is named for the Manager to walk in. _Avoid_: Transfer, relocation

**Pen Spell**: Where an Animal stood and for how long: from the Move that put her there until the Move that took her away, or until her Exit. Derived from her Moves, never entered — her Pen history is her Pen Spells end to end, and her page, her Animal Passport and what her feed cost all read the same ones. _Avoid_: Stay, placement, pen history line

**Weaning**: The point at which a Calf stops being fed milk; on this Farm the trigger for a male Calf's Move to Fattening. Recorded by a Step, once per Animal, with what she weighed: a heifer calf becomes a Heifer on the Dairy side, and a bull calf is walked across to the Fattening Pen the farm named, his Days on Feed counting from it. A Correction cannot undo it — a Heifer made a Calf again, or a bull walked back from Fattening, would unpick a Season — so a Step corrected to a skip stands aside for the Manager. _Avoid_: Separation

## Health

**Observation**: What somebody saw of one Animal on the round — off her feed, lame, bulling — recorded by the Step that saw it, from the words that Version offers. Starts the health chain; not a Diagnosis. An Observation of oestrus is what a Heat is recorded as. A cow a milker skips as unwell is one too, saying only that, since the milker does not know why; her litres written in its place take it back. Anything but a Heat is work for the Manager, raised about her: late in a day, or in an hour for bloat and laboured breathing, which kill within hours. The Vet's Diagnosis answers it and calls the work off; the Manager answers it by saying what was done — the Vet rung, looked at again, better. Several animals in one Pen seen with sores on the mouth or feet within the farm's hours — three in two days unless the farm says otherwise — are told to the Owner and the Manager at once, once for the Pen, naming what was seen and never a disease: that is the Vet's to conclude. Corrections withdraw one and write another beside it, taking back the work it raised; nothing is removed. _Avoid_: Symptom, complaint, report, sighting

**Diagnosis**: The Vet's recorded conclusion about what an Animal has. Vet-only, recorded by the Vet themselves and never on their behalf — it is their act in law. It may answer an Observation, which is how the health chain reads as one story on the Animal's page; it may also stand alone. Corrected only by the Vet who made it, with a reason, and nothing is removed. The Vet says how it ended — recovered, or not — once, and puts it right with a reason like the rest of it; a death is her Mortality, not an outcome. An Animal the Vet diagnoses again and again within the farm's days (three in a year unless the farm says otherwise) is on the Manager's list, for the Owner to weigh; never pushed, and not a Cull Reason. _Avoid_: Finding, condition, case

**Prescription**: The Vet's order for one Animal: drug, dose, route, frequency, duration. Vet-only, from their own account, and only from a product whose Withdrawal days are known. It answers a Diagnosis. The system turns it into one Instance of the Treatment SOP per dose, at the times the Vet set, so a dose nobody gave is Overdue beside a milking nobody did. _Avoid_: Treatment plan, order, script

**Treatment**: One dose given to an Animal, and the Withdrawal it earns: what was given, who gave it and when. It reaches her three ways — a dose of a Prescription, where the farm knows it owes the dose before anybody gives it (which is what makes a missed one visible as work nobody did); a dose of a Campaign, where nothing is owed until the Pen is walked; or a dose not prescribed — the pharmacy's advice, or anybody's, given before the Vet saw her — written afterwards by the Owner or the Manager with who advised it and why, and told to the Vet at once. The last Treatment **given** starts the Withdrawal, whichever way it came. _Avoid_: Medication, administration, dosing

**Drug List**: The farm's list of products that may be prescribed or given in a Campaign, each with milk and meat withdrawal days. Maintained by the Vet; a product with blank days cannot be prescribed, and a dose of it not prescribed takes the **Default Withdrawal Days**. _Avoid_: Formulary, inventory (stock is a different concern), medicine list

**Withdrawal**: The period after the last Treatment given during which an Animal's milk may not go to bulk (milk withdrawal) or the Animal may not be sold for meat (meat withdrawal). A hard Gate. Only the Vet may shorten it, with a reason. _Avoid_: Withholding period, waiting time, hold

**Default Withdrawal Days**: The milk and meat days the Vet writes once for the farm, for a dose not prescribed of a product the Drug List has no days for yet. The dose keeps them, so a new default does not change what it held her for; days the Vet writes for the product itself are the Drug List's word and win. Until the Vet has written them, such a dose is refused. _Avoid_: Default hold, standard withdrawal

**Campaign**: A vaccination or a deworming run over a Pen as one piece of work with a per-Animal Step, so every Animal ends up with the Treatment in her own history. The Version names the product; the Manager decides the day. _Avoid_: Programme, batch treatment, mass medication

**Vaccine**: A product on the Drug List the Vet has marked as one. Its doses go on the vaccination register, and each must be traceable to a Lot Number. _Avoid_: Jab, shot

**Lot Number**: The manufacturer's number on a vaccine vial, a box of medicine or a bag of feed, which traces what was given or fed back to what was in it. A Campaign asks for a vaccine's once for the Pen, and every dose of that Campaign without one of its own came from it; a dose from another vial carries its own. A **Medicine Purchase** and a **Feed Purchase** carry the Lot they came in — its Lot Number and **Expiry** — and the store is read Lot by Lot: first to expire, first used. _Avoid_: Batch (a Batch is an Outbox send), batch number, vial number

**Expiry**: The last day a Lot says it may be used, as the farm's own day. Refused when it has already passed on the day the Lot comes in. The Manager hears of a Lot with some left in it a farm-set number of days before its Expiry (30 by default), and again once it has passed; a dose given from a Lot past its Expiry is recorded, because an animal that needed treating was treated, and the Vet and the Manager hear of it at once. _Avoid_: Best-before, use-by, shelf life

**Missing**: An Animal the round looked for in her Pen and could not find, opened by the round's "Animal not found" — a skip reason the farm acts on whatever the Owner rewords it to — or by the Manager's "Not found" once a **Head Count** has sent them to walk her Pen. She stays in the herd, on her Pen's board and on the round, until the Manager marks her Found: nobody has said she is gone, only that she was not where the farm thought. One open at a time, so a second morning that cannot find her is the same Missing, told once to the Owner and the Manager at once, by push. A Correction that finds her on the round after all takes it back. The Owner may write her off, and is asked to once she has been missing as many days as the Owner set (a week): she leaves as **Lost** from the morning she was last looked for — stolen or strayed in the Owner's words, with the thana's GD number for a theft — and counts as gone with nothing back, as a death does. Found after that is the Owner's, and she comes back in the State she left from. A Venture's animal is not written off until its agreement says what a loss is. _Avoid_: Lost (which is written off, not looked for), stolen, strayed

**Mortality**: The record that an Animal died or was culled: when, the cause as far as the farm knows it, and how the carcass was disposed of. The Owner's or the Manager's to record, and nobody else's — except a stillborn calf's, which her Calving records with the cause stillbirth, leaving the disposal awaiting until the Manager writes it. She leaves the herd — off the pen boards, out of the day's work, out of the headcounts — and everything else recorded about her stays exactly where it is. Its cause is picked from the farm's short list — a calf's, or a grown animal's — or typed; where she died of what the Vet diagnosed, it is linked to that Diagnosis of hers, and the mortality register names the disease and the office's reference. Grown animals' deaths are read as a rate: deaths for every hundred head kept a year, by Side, culls apart. _Avoid_: Death record, loss, wastage

**Disposal**: What was done with a carcass: buried (the rule is six feet) or burned. Evidence, because an inspector may ask. Never a word for a Sale — an Animal sold is Sold, and "disposal" is what happened to a body. _Avoid_: Removal, destruction

**Notifiable Disease**: A disease on the farm's own list of those that must be reported to DLS in writing without delay (Animal Disease Act 2005, s.3). The list is what the Upazila Livestock Officer confirms to this farm, with the confirmation noted beside each entry, because the national schedule could not be sourced. Each one keeps the other names a Vet writes it by — "FMD", "খুরা রোগ" — and a Diagnosis naming it by any of them, in either spelling of Bangla, whatever the capitals, spaces or dashes, names it. A Vet Diagnosis naming one raises the DLS report work immediately and tells the Owner and the Manager; the Vet is offered the list as they write, and told before saving that a report will be raised. _Avoid_: Reportable disease, outbreak

## Milk

**Milk Record**: The litres one cow gave in one Milking Session, with a Destination. Captured as a Step Completion of the milking SOP. _Avoid_: Yield entry, milk log, production record

**Destination**: Where a Milk Record's litres went: Bulk, Calves, or Discard. Withdrawal forces Discard. _Avoid_: Use, allocation

**Bulk**: The saleable milk pooled from a Milking Session. Its recorded total is reconciled against the per-cow Milk Records. _Avoid_: Tank, total milk

**Dispatch**: The recorded hand-over of Bulk milk to a buyer: when, litres, the buyer (a Counterparty, whose name and address make it the farm's milk-buyer record under the Safe Food Act, kept as they stood on the day the milk left), the challan when the collector writes one, the price per litre, and optional fat %, SNF %, note. The Manager's to record. Read beside the litres the day's Milk Records sent to Bulk, so milk into the tank and milk out of the gate are not two stories. _Avoid_: Sale (finance's word for the money side), delivery, supply

**Lactation**: One cow's milking period from a calving to the following Dry-off. Numbered per cow; days-in-milk and totals are derived from Milk Records. A cow in milk giving well under her own week — per recorded milking, the last two farm days against the seven before, 20% less unless the farm says otherwise, whatever became of the milk — is named to the Manager as giving less: a convention that catches sudden illness, not a measured line, and a heat drops milk too. _Avoid_: Milking cycle, production period

**Reconciliation**: The comparison of a Milking Session's Bulk total against the sum of its per-cow Milk Records destined for Bulk. A difference beyond the Tolerance is flagged for the Manager. Across days, the week's milk into the tank is set against what Dispatches took out of the gate, allowing for what was in the tank when the week began and is in it now; what is left is milk nobody can account for, told to the Owner and the Manager in the evening's post when it passes the Owner's line (3%). _Avoid_: Balancing, audit, check

**Tolerance**: The Farm Parameter, as a percentage, within which a Reconciliation difference passes unremarked. _Avoid_: Margin, threshold, allowance

**Days in Milk**: How long a cow has been in her current Lactation, counted from the calving that started it. Derived, never entered. _Avoid_: DIM (in prose), lactation age

**Dry-off**: The recorded end of a Lactation before the next calving. Moves the cow from Milking to Dry. _Avoid_: Drying, rest period

## Fattening

**Release**: A bought-in Animal let out of Quarantine by the Playbook's release Step: Fattening from that moment, and walked to the first Pen whose Ration's Weight Band suits what the scale last said of him — where none does, he is released where he stands for the Manager to move. The Standard Playbook hangs it thirty days after he entered Quarantine (DLS GLPP 2023). Never undone by a Correction: a bull put back in Quarantine would look freshly bought. The walk out of the quarantine pen is the Release's, made once he is Fattening. _Avoid_: Discharge, clearance

**Intake**: The recorded arrival of a bought-in Animal on the Fattening side: source, price, intake weight, estimated age, photo, Target Window. Creates the Animal in Quarantine, in a quarantine **Pen** — and only there; a farm with none marked is told to mark one first. As the price and the weight are typed, the sheet sets its taka a kilo beside what the farm's own Intakes of the last two months within a sixth of her weight cost a kilo, weighed by weight, and says how much dearer or cheaper — the price alone, as the sheet's own figure is, Hasil apart. The Intakes of the last year that died, were culled or were diagnosed within their first thirty days — the quarantine — are counted for the Owner by seller and by haat, the most lost first: a pattern to ask a trader about, never written on the animal. Her intake weight is set against her first Weigh-in within her first thirty days: weighed short of it by more than the Owner's line, 5% unless she moves it, she is told in the evening's post, once — a lorry takes weight off a bull and a fortnight puts it back, so the reading is right and it is the purchase she asks about. Those weighed short are counted beside each seller and haat with the year's early losses. _Avoid_: Purchase (finance's word), arrival, admission

**Weigh-in**: A recorded scale reading for one Animal on a date. Fortnightly for Fattening; daily gain and projections are derived from Weigh-ins. Every reading is kept — the difference between two of them is the thing being measured — and one that changed more than an animal could is still kept, flagged with what the farm found, and raised as a **Needs Review** for the Manager. An animal's first one after her Intake is also read against the weight she was bought at. _Avoid_: Weight check, weighing record

**Shrink**: What an Animal loses between her last weighing on the farm — her last Weigh-in, or the weight she came in at — and the scale she is sold on: the lorry, the haat, a night without water. Shown in kilos and as a part of her last weight on the sale sheet as the day's weight is typed, on the day's sales, and for those sold off each Selling Trip together, weighed by weight; a last weighing more than three weeks old is said with its age, and a sale heavier than it asks for the scale to be checked. Never refused: the price is struck on the day's weight. More than the farm's allowance, 8% unless the Owner moves it, on a fattening animal's last trusted Weigh-in within three weeks is told to the Owner in the evening's post, once per Sale. _Avoid_: Weight loss (that is a sick animal's), tare

**Days on Feed**: How long a bought-in Animal has been on the Farm being fed, counted from its Intake. Derived, never entered — like Days in Milk. _Avoid_: Age on farm, feeding days

**Average Daily Gain**: Kilogrammes an Animal puts on in a day, worked out between two Weigh-ins or between its Intake and its latest Weigh-in. The Farm reads both: the gap between them is how it sees a Ration that has stopped working. Read over at least four weeks and set beside her Ration's **Expected Gain**, it says whether she is gaining what that Ration should give her. Derived, never entered. _Avoid_: ADG on its own (say it in full), growth rate

**Eid-ul-Adha**: The market the Fattening side is fed towards. Its three days of Qurbani start on 10 Dhul Hijjah, which Bangladesh's moon sighting committee fixes. The Farm's day for it is one of three:

- **announced**: the committee's day, written in by the Owner or the Manager;
- **expected**: from the app's list, kept a decade ahead;
- **estimated**: past the end of the list, the Umm al-Qura calendar's day plus one.

Every screen says which it is. An announced day can be corrected, or taken back — the Eid is on its expected day again, as if nobody had announced it; both are rows of their own, so what the Farm believed and when stays in the trail. Writing in the announced day moves no Animal by itself. The Farm's own Animals still aimed at the day expected are offered the move, as their own act. A Venture's Animals are not offered it, because a Venture's Target Window moves only by an **Amendment**. _Avoid_: Eid date as a fixed fact, Qurbani (the sacrifice, not the date)

**Target Window**: The period in which the Farm intends to sell an Animal — by default the next Eid-ul-Adha. Set when she comes to the Fattening side: at her **Intake**, when she crosses from Dairy, or when the Farm buys her from a **Venture**. Drives projected weight, the Ready-for-Sale suggestion and which **Season** she is in. Once an Eid's Qurbani is over, the animals aimed at it still on the Farm — its own and a Venture's — are told to the Owner and the Manager in the evening's post, once for that Eid and within a fortnight of it; a Ready animal unsold past her window says so where she is offered for sale. _Avoid_: Sale date, deadline

**Season**: The Farm's own fattening Animals fed towards one **Target Window**, judged together once they have gone: one Eid-ul-Adha, whichever of its days each of them carries, or a window that is no Eid, named by its dates. An Animal joins hers on the day she comes to the Farm's Fattening side — by **Intake**, by crossing from Dairy, whose days before it are the Dairy side's, or bought from a Venture by **Internal Sale** — and stays in it however she goes: sold before the Eid or kept on after it, dead, or sold to a Venture. A Season is finished when the last of its Animals has gone, not when its Eid has passed, so a bull nobody could sell stays on the Season that bought him. A **Venture**'s Animals are in none: the Venture is their own. _Avoid_: Run, batch (which is an offline send), lot, cohort

**Ready for Sale**: The State an Animal enters when the Manager confirms it may be sold. Suggested by the system when target weight is reached or the Target Window opens; impossible under meat Withdrawal, whichever way it is asked for. _Avoid_: Finished, market-ready, matured

**Suggestion**: The farm saying an Animal may be ready to sell, with the grounds it says so on. Never a decision and never a **Gate**: it moves nothing on its own, and the Manager confirms or sets it aside. _Avoid_: Recommendation, alert (an Alert is told to somebody; a Suggestion waits on a screen)

**Set Aside**: The Manager's recorded answer to a Suggestion — this Animal is staying, and why. Not a **Needs Review**, which is the system asking a person to settle something it could not; this is a person settling something the system only offered. The farm stops suggesting her until a ground appears that was not there when the Manager looked. _Avoid_: Dismiss, snooze, ignore

**Sale**: The recorded hand-over of an Animal to a buyer: buyer, price, weight at sale, destination, transport. Hard-gated by meat Withdrawal; a Venture's animal is paid for by bank or in cash, never by bKash; the Animal exits as Sold, and by no other route — every way out of the herd is the record of how she went. A cull that ends at a butcher is a Sale and not a **Mortality**: one exit, one record, and the reason she was culled in the Sale's own note. A broker at the haat is typed on the Sale: the Farm pays it as money of its own, and it is her cost, dated the day she went — counted with her trips in her Margin, Return on Cost and Settlement, and repaid by a Venture in its Reimbursement as a Selling Trip is. A fattening animal sold for less than she cost the farm, or for less than her weight on the day at the low price a kilo — her Venture's, or the farm's market price — is told to the Owner in the evening's post, never refused; a dairy cow culled to a butcher is not. Her weight there is the heavier of the day's and her last trusted Weigh-in within three weeks less the farm's allowance for **Shrink**, 8% unless the Owner moves it, so a weight typed low cannot lower the floor with it; the notice says which it was. _Avoid_: Dispatch (milk's word), disposal, exit, offtake

## Reproduction

**Heat**: A recorded observation that a cow is in oestrus: cow, time, signs. Raises the AI SOP, due at the start of the farm's **AI Window** and late at its end. Seen twice before she is served, it is still one heat and raises one job. A cow the farm expects in heat and has not seen is on the **heat watch**: open and past the farm's days after calving (60, as DLS re-examines one not in heat by 50–60 days) with no sign of heat in the last 24 days, or 18–24 days after a service with no return and no check yet. A service counts as a sign — a bull serves cows nobody saw — and most cows on it are heats nobody saw, so it asks for eyes and the Vet, never calls her barren. A heifer past the age to be served first and never served is on it too — crossbred at 18 months and deshi at 30 unless the farm says otherwise, by her breed — and one whose age nobody knows is named apart. _Avoid_: Oestrus, standing, bulling

**AI Window**: The hours after a Heat is seen within which a service takes — by default twelve to eighteen. A Farm Parameter, because how soon a technician reaches the farm is this farm's fact and not a fact about cattle. _Avoid_: Service window, breeding window

**Service**: A recorded insemination — AI (semen straw) or natural (bull) — with date, sire, and who did it. Recorded by the Manager alone, as the Step of the AI work its Heat raised, so the service _is_ that work done rather than something closed beside it. A natural service names a bull standing on this farm. The ones that did not take are kept: a run of them is what makes a Repeat Breeder. _Avoid_: Breeding (the whole area), mating, insemination (AI only)

**Attempt**: The services of one heat — one, or two when she is served again a few hours later. The Pregnancy Check is of an Attempt and counts from its first service, and one that did not take is one failure however many times she was served. An Attempt followed by another before anybody found her carrying did not take: she came back into heat. _Avoid_: Cycle, try, breeding

**Pregnancy Check**: The Vet's recorded result, positive or negative, of an Attempt — due a Farm Parameter's days (45 by default) after its first service, once per Attempt, and only for her latest. The Vet's alone. Positive sets the Expected Calving and makes a Heifer a Pregnant Heifer; a negative is kept and takes nothing from her. _Avoid_: PD (fine in speech), scan, confirmation

**Expected Calving**: The first service of the heat a positive Pregnancy Check found her carrying from, plus the gestation length (a Farm Parameter, 283 days by default). Worked out and re-worked whenever what it counts from changes, never typed — except for a cow that arrives already carrying, whose Expected Calving is given at intake or on the opening register because nobody on this farm served her. Drives the Dry-off and Calving-prep SOPs, due a lead of days before it (Farm Parameters, 60 and 7 by default); when it moves, work still open moves with it and work done stays done. It is a forecast of Dairy work, so it goes when she calves, leaves the farm or crosses to Fattening, taking the calving work still owed with it; whether she was carrying stays in her Pregnancy Checks. _Avoid_: Due date, calving date (that's the actual Calving)

**Calving**: The recorded birth event: when, how it went (unassisted, assisted, with the vet), and each calf's sex and whether it was born alive. Recorded by Barn Staff as a Step, or by the Manager. Starts the dam's next Lactation and ends her Expected Calving; every calf becomes an Animal with the next dairy Tag Number in her mother's Pen. Twins are one Calving with two calves, and a stillborn calf is created and leaves as Died in the same act. Recording it is also what the farm hangs the cow's own care on: she is looked at once a day for five days from it — down and unable to rise, the afterbirth held, off her feed, a hard udder — and what is seen is an Observation, a cow down or an afterbirth held the Manager's within the hour. _Avoid_: Birth, parturition, delivery

**Abortion**: The recorded loss of a pregnancy before Calving: date, stage, Vet note. The Vet's alone. Clears the pregnancy and its Expected Calving, closes the calving work still owed, and puts a Pregnant Heifer back on heat watch. Not a failed Attempt: she took, and lost it. _Avoid_: Miscarriage, loss

**Repeat Breeder**: A flag the system raises on a cow after a threshold of failed Services (default 3), counted by Attempt — two services in one heat that did not take are one failure. Waits on the Manager's queue and buzzes nobody's phone, until somebody answers — serve her again, treat her, or cull her — and comes back if she fails again after the answer. A prompt for a human decision, never an automatic State change. _Avoid_: Infertile, cull candidate

**Cull Reason**: Why the farm names a dairy cow to the Owner as one to think about letting go. Three of them, any of which will do: her milk sent to Bulk over the days her keep is read over fetched less — at what a litre got in the farm's own Dispatches over a Farm Parameter's days (60 by default, the Owner's to set) — than her keep over those days cost, the same keep as a fattening Animal's **Cost of Gain** now reads; she is still not in calf a Farm Parameter's days after her last Calving (150 by default, and the Owner's to set, as the list is the Owner's to read), or is Dry and not in calf; or she is a **Repeat Breeder** nobody has answered for yet. A cow in calf is never named for her milk or for being empty, because she will calve and milk again; and her milk is not weighed until she is a Farm Parameter's days into her **Lactation** (35 by default, and never fewer than her calf's days — a Farm Parameter, a week by default — more than the days her keep is read over, since that first milk is her calf's and the days after it are what is read; the Owner's to set, as the other is). Worked out, never entered; the Owner's alone to read, with the money beside it, and nothing to answer — a cow leaves the list when the facts under her change. Never a decision and not a **Suggestion**: culling her is still a **Sale** to a butcher, or a **Mortality**. _Avoid_: Cull candidate, cull suggestion

## Feed

**Feed Item**: Something the Farm feeds, counted in one of three units: kilos (the default), litres (molasses) or bundles (napier cut by the আঁটি). Its Ration lines, purchases and counts all mean that unit. A bundle is counted whole, never weighed out, so a Ration gives it by the head and never by body weight. Feed counted in kilos may also be bought by the **bag**, once the farm says what that feed's bags weigh, or by the **maund** (মণ, 37.324 kg) a trader's slip gives. Either way it comes into the store as kilos, with what was typed kept beside them. Home-grown fodder is a Feed Item too. _Avoid_: Ingredient, feed type, commodity; "piece" or "sack" for a bundle or bag

**Ration**: A named, versioned list of Feed Items, each line so many kg a day either for every animal or for every 100 kg of body weight — grass, straw and concentrate grow with the animals, salt and minerals go by the head. Pens are put on one; several Pens may share it, and changing it is one change. _Avoid_: Diet, feeding plan, formula

**Weight Band**: The weights a Ration is written for — a grower's 150 to 250 kg, a finisher's from 250 — kept on the Ration, not its Version, because it says who should eat it rather than what they are fed. From is where it starts and To where the next one takes over; either end may be open. A fattening animal weighed outside her Pen's Ration's band — grown past it, or not yet up to it — is pointed out on the Fattening page with the Pens whose Ration suits her, weighed as feeding weighs her; nobody is told by Notice. _Avoid_: Weight class, bracket, stage

**Expected Gain**: The kilos a day, low to high, a Ration is written to put on the animals that eat it — a grower's 0.6 to 0.9 — kept on the Ration beside its **Weight Band**, and both ends or neither. Always a range, because the trials a figure comes from never agree on one. A fattening animal is judged against her Pen's Ration's on her **Average Daily Gain** over at least a Farm Parameter's days (four weeks by default, the Manager's to set), read between Weigh-ins the farm did not doubt, and never from before she had settled in three weeks after her Intake or before she began eating that Ration; until then it is too soon to say. One weighed outside the Ration's **Weight Band** is not judged by it at all — what it should put on a bull its size is not what it should put on her, and moving her is the answer. She is pointed out too when she is gaining under a share of her penmates — four fifths by default, a Farm Parameter, the Manager's — each Animal's gain first set against what her own Ration should give her, so a deshi bull is not measured against crosses; a Pen is a group to measure her against once four in it are judged. That catches the slow one in a Pen that is all slow, a hot month or a poor load of feed, which the Ration's figures alone cannot. One gaining under the low end, or losing weight, is pointed out on the Fattening page for somebody to go and look at, and marked on the board; nobody is told by Notice, and it is never a **Suggestion**. The standard fattening Rations come with theirs, from the published trials, and a Ration's figures are a crossbred bull's: an Animal of a **deshi** Breed is judged against a share of them (seven tenths by default), a cow or heifer against another (eight tenths), and one who is both against both — Farm Parameters, the Manager's — while one nobody wrote a Breed down for is judged as a cross, and it is said beside her. The same figures say, at **Intake**, what an Animal should weigh when her **Target Window** opens — her intake weight grown after she has settled in, a day at a time at the Expected Gain of whichever band she has grown into — as a low and a high; the low end is what she is fed towards unless somebody types a target of her own, and the farm's own target weight only where no Ration says what an Animal her weight should gain. A **Venture Plan**'s bands are offered them too, never written in unasked. Beside a Ration's Expected Gain stands what the farm's own Animals put on eating it, by kind — crossbred bulls, deshi bulls, bulls with no Breed written, cows and heifers — once five of a kind have a gain: the middle one and the middle half. Each Animal counts once, by her longest stay on it — the gone as well as the standing — measured as she is judged: settled in, over at least the Farm Parameter's days, and only since her Pen was put on the Ration it is on now, since which Ration a Pen was on before is kept in the trail, not in the Pen. The middle half of the crossbred bulls is offered as the Ration's own, and the deshi and female shares are set beside what the farm's own show. _Avoid_: Target gain (a **Target Window** is a date range), planned gain (a **Venture Plan**'s), growth rate, slow gainer

**Feeding Target**: What one session of feeding calls for in one Pen — the Ration in force, times the animals standing there or what the Pen weighs, divided by how often they are fed. The Pen's weight is each animal's latest Weigh-in the farm did not doubt, or her Intake weight, with anybody never weighed counted at the average of the rest; as weighed, never projected, and a Pen nobody has weighed owes no figure by weight until it is. Worked out, never typed. Not a **Target Window**, which is Fattening's date range. _Avoid_: Quota, allowance, plan

**Feeding**: The recorded act of feeding one Pen in one session: kg of each Feed Item actually given, and the **Leftover** of each. Consumes Stock. Its cost — each Feed Item's price at the time, times what was given — is charged evenly to the Animals standing in the Pen when it was fed; fodder at no price costs nothing. _Avoid_: Feed log, feed entry

**Leftover**: What a Pen did not eat of a Feeding, weighed per Feed Item and written with it; left blank, it is none. Counted against what was eaten when a session's shortfall is judged, and added up by Pen and Feed Item over days to show a Ration that gives more of an item than the Pen eats — more than a tenth of it left, and the Pen is **wasting** it — or a trough never left with a scrap, which is a Pen that may want more or a feeder not writing it down. Priced as the Feeding was charged, so what was left is what the animals were charged for and did not eat. Per Feed Item, never in total, because each is in its own unit. _Avoid_: Waste (not all of it is thrown away), refusals, orts

**Feed Purchase**: Feed bought and brought into the store: the Feed Item, how much, what the lot cost, and the Counterparty who sold it — the **seller**, as on an Intake. What a Feed Item's price is worked out from. Each one's price per unit — a lot bought by the bag or the maund read per kilo — is set beside the last Feed Purchase of the same feed by the day it came, never a Harvest, and read afresh so one put right is compared where it now falls; one dearer than that by more than the Owner's line (10%) is told to the Owner in the evening's post, once for the lot. Weighed on the farm's scale as it comes — a choice, for feed bought by the kilo — the scale is what the store holds and what its price per unit is read on, and the slip's figure is kept beside it: each seller's lots of the last ninety days show how short they run, in kilos, percent of the slips and taka at what each slip kilo was charged. A lot nobody weighed claims no difference. Not the finance side of buying anything else, which is a Money Event. _Avoid_: Order, delivery, procurement, supplier

**Harvest**: Feed cut from the farm's own fields and brought into the store, from nobody and bought from nobody. It adds to Stock on Hand at its Feed Item's **Fodder Price**, so an Animal fed home-grown fodder is charged for it like bought feed. The fields are the Farm's, but what grows on them is not free to whoever eats it. _Avoid_: Home stock, own production

**Fodder Price**: The price per kg the Owner sets on a home-grown Feed Item: roughly what it would cost to buy. What a Harvest brings into the store at. Changing it is the Owner's, and applies to Harvests from then on. _Avoid_: Market price, notional cost

**Stock on Hand**: Current quantity of a Feed Item: Feed Purchases and Harvests in, minus Feeding, corrected by the latest Stock Count — or of a product on the Drug List, in doses: Medicine Purchases in, minus every dose given, never below nothing. Worked out, never typed, and a Feed Item's shown below nothing when the pens were fed from feed nobody wrote down arriving. What is left of each Lot is worked out the same way, taking what was used from the Lot that expires first. Either may be given a level below which the Manager is told it is running low. _Avoid_: Inventory (the whole area), balance

**Head Count**: The evening count of each Pen at lock-up, by the number of animals standing in it — counted blind, without seeing how many the register expects, and set against the animals the register puts in that Pen at the moment it was counted, read from their Moves, so an animal walked in before the count is counted there. Barn Staff count; a Pen that does not count right, short or over, is told to the Manager at once, who walks it and counts again by a Correction, which compares again. A shortfall opens nothing by itself: the Manager marks which animal is not found, and that is her **Missing**. Unlike a **Stock Count**, the register does not give way to it: an animal is only gone once somebody says which. _Avoid_: Stock count (stock is feed here), census, roll call

**Medicine Count**: The monthly count of the medicine, on the first Friday of the month: every product on the Drug List counted in doses, blind, by the Manager who buys it, signed off by the Owner. Set against what the store is thought to hold — doses bought, less doses given, with the earlier counts' differences — and every difference needs a reason. The count wins: Stock on Hand reads a shortfall as doses gone from the Lot that expires first. A shortfall past the Owner's line, at what the doses cost, is told to the Owner in the evening's post: doses gone that no Treatment says went into an animal. _Avoid_: Medicine stock take, drug audit

**Stock Count**: The weekly physical count of each Feed Item — every one the Farm keeps, counted without seeing what the store is thought to hold. The count wins: Stock on Hand reads from it afterwards. Each difference is an adjustment with a reason, read against the store as it now stands, so something written up late but dated before the count shows in it rather than as a loss. A shortfall is the Farm's: keeping the store is the place and the people, so no Animal is charged for feed she was never given. Raised by the clock every Friday, once for the whole farm, made by the Manager and signed off by the Owner; the Owner is told when the store has not been counted for more than a week. Each difference is priced at the store's average price when counted — what the Animals are charged at — shortfall and surplus kept apart, never netted; a count short by more than the Owner's line is told to the Owner and the Manager in the evening's post, and a period's shortfall stands beside the Overheads, in neither them nor any Side's costs. _Avoid_: Stocktake, audit

**Running Low**: A Feed Item holding less than the level the Manager set for it, or fewer **days of feed** than the farm's line (a week) at the rate it has been fed over the last fourteen farm days, today among them — or the days since it was first fed, for a newer one. A feed nobody feeds has no days, and a retired one is never low. It waits on the Manager's queue and the Owner's exception list, and is told once in the Manager's Digest each time it falls below the level, or once for its days until a delivery or a count brings the store back up — never as a push. _Avoid_: Shortage, out of stock (that is nothing left), reorder point

## Money

**Money Event**: One recorded flow of money in or out of the Farm: amount in BDT, date, category, Counterparty, payment method, and a link to the farm record that caused it where one exists. A Dispatch, an Intake, a Sale, a Feed Purchase, a Medicine Purchase and a Vet Fee each make one on their own, and a Correction to the record puts the same one right. A Sale or a Dispatch makes one for what was paid as it left, and no more: what the buyer still owed is its **Baki**, which is money only on the day it is paid. Everything else — wages, electricity, repairs, manure sold — the Manager enters by hand, with a note, a photo of the receipt, and the Side it belongs to when it belongs to one; a wage names the person and the month it pays for, once per person per month. One paid in cash names whose **Cash in Hand** the notes went into or came out of — the person writing it, where they are the Owner or a Manager, unless the Owner names another Owner or Manager — writing up the Manager's haat sale that evening, the notes are in his hand; a Manager names only his own; bKash and the bank name nobody. One by bKash or the bank names the **Farm Account** it went into or came out of, and its transaction ID, once one of that kind is listed. _Avoid_: Transaction, journal entry, payment (one kind)

**Cash in Hand**: What one person holds of the Farm's own cash: every cash Money Event that named their hand, in less out, and every Handover to or from them. The Owner and each Manager have one; the Owner reads every hand, a Manager their own. Money booked before hands were named names nobody, and a Venture's money moves through its account — but for a Venture's animal sold for cash at the haat, whose price is held in the hand that took it, as that Venture's, until it is deposited. _Avoid_: Petty cash, till, float (a Buying Float is one trip's), balance

**Handover**: Cash passed from one hand to another — the Manager's takings to the Owner — or into the bank as the Farm's deposit, or out of it into a hand, or from one **Farm Account** to another, with the slip or cheque where the bank is one end — or a Venture's sale cash deposited from the hand that took it into its **Venture Account**, naming the Sales it carries, each once. Not a Money Event: nothing is earned or spent, only moved. A Manager hands over from their own hand; the Owner may write any. _Avoid_: Transfer (the bank's word), deposit alone, remittance

**Farm Account**: One of the Farm's own bKash numbers or bank accounts, listed by the Owner with a name and its number — the bank's name and branch for a bank account. Once one of a kind is open, a Money Event by that kind names which one, with its transaction ID or slip number, taken once on an account; and a Handover's bank end may name one. Retired, never deleted: it stays on the money that named it and is offered for none after; with every one of a kind retired, money by that kind names none again. Read against its statement each month in a **Bank Check**. The Manager reads them, the number masked to its last four. Not a **Venture Account**, which is a Venture's own and outside the Farm's books. _Avoid_: Wallet, bank (alone), Venture Account

**Cash Count**: The weekly count of the notes in one's own hand — the Manager's, or the Owner's where they count — made blind, without the step saying what the farm thinks the hand holds, and set against that at the moment it was counted. It counts every note in the hand, a Venture's sale cash not yet deposited with the Farm's, and a difference is the Farm's: the Venture is owed what its animal fetched, whole. The count wins: the hand holds what was counted from then on, and the difference is kept with the counter's reason, never quietly absorbed. Raised by the clock every Friday evening, once for the whole farm, made by the Manager and signed off by the Owner; a count short by more than the Owner's line is told to the Owner in the evening's post. _Avoid_: Cash audit, till count, reconciliation (a Buying Float's)

**Wage Draw**: Money a person takes ahead of payday, against their wage. It goes out the day it is drawn as a Money Event of its own under Wages — out of the hand that paid it — so a month's wage cost is its draws and what payday pays, never either alone. Payday takes the person's draws still owed off the month's wage, the oldest first, and books only what is paid then; draws that come to more than the wage stay owed for what was not taken, and come off the next. A wage that took draws keeps its amount, person and month as they were. A draw is put right by a Correction of its own, its Money Event with it: put to nothing, a draw that never happened is taken back; what a payday has taken off it stays taken, so it never goes below that, nor moves to another person. _Avoid_: Advance (a Venture's), loan, salary advance

**Medicine Purchase**: Medicine bought for a product on the Drug List: how much, as the box or the shop says it; roughly how many doses that holds; what it cost; who sold it; and the Lot it came in, with its **Expiry**. What a dose given is costed from, and what the product's Stock on Hand counts in. _Avoid_: Drug order, pharmacy bill

**Vet Fee**: What the Vet charges the Farm for a visit, entered by the Vet: the amount, the day, and the Animals seen when the Vet names them. The only money the Vet enters or sees. _Avoid_: Consultation charge, visit bill

**Receipt**: The paper a buyer leaves with: every Animal they took on one day, with weights, prices and the total, headed by the farm of origin. One per buyer per day, however many beasts — five sheets is how one of them gets lost. A buyer who left owing says so on it: what he paid, his **Baki**, and the day he promised, over his signature. _Avoid_: Invoice (the farm is not billing anybody), bill, challan (that is milk's word, on a Dispatch)

**Baki**: What a buyer still owed the Farm for one Sale or one Dispatch as it left: its price, less what he paid there and then — and, as he pays, less every **Baki Payment** and anything written off. Written with the day he promised to pay by: a Sale asks for one, since a trader promises a day, and a Dispatch takes one when a milk buyer names it. Only the Farm's own: a Venture's animal leaves paid in full, because Investors' money is never lent to a buyer. A Correction to the price keeps what he paid, since cash handed over at the gate is what it was, and a buyer who paid in full stays paid in full. Closed by being paid or written off, never by being forgotten. _Avoid_: Credit, due, owed (an Investor's capital), receivable, dues

**Baki Payment**: One handover of money from a buyer towards his **Baki**, for milk or for cattle: its own Money Event on the day it came, clearing his oldest Baki first. _Avoid_: Collection, instalment, repayment

**Write-off**: The Owner closing a **Baki** that will not be paid, with a reason. What the Animal fetched is then her price less it, and the buyer carries it: the next Sale or Dispatch to him says so. Not a **Settlement Adjustment**'s waiver. _Avoid_: Bad debt, waiver, loss (one kind)

**Transport Card**: The paper the lorry carries: farm of origin with its Registration number, the Animals on that vehicle by tag, the destination, the date and the driver (Meat Rules 2021 r.18). One per **Load**, never one per day. _Avoid_: Movement permit, waybill, pass

**Load**: The Animals that went to one destination, on one vehicle, with one driver, on one day. What a Transport Card describes — a card covering a whole day's sales to one buyer would assert a load that was never on that lorry. _Avoid_: Consignment, shipment, batch

**Venture**: One investor-funded run of fattening cattle, from the first Investment Agreement to the last payout: Open, Buying, Fattening, Selling, Settled — or Cancelled, where an under-funded one ends. It carries a target capital, a **Floor**, a decision date, its two budgets and its own **Target Window**, which its Animals inherit. Ventures run alongside the Farm's own cattle in the same Pens. _Avoid_: Project, batch (which is an offline send), scheme, lot

**Cattle Budget**: The part of a Venture's capital meant for buying animals. What it does not spend rolls into the **Running Budget** when buying closes. The two are a plan for the whole capital, so what arrives is divided between them in the same proportion — half the capital in means half of each, never a full Cattle Budget and nothing to feed the animals with. Paid by the month, the **Cattle Part** comes first and is all cattle money, and the **Monthly Sums** that follow are all the Running Budget's. After that they are spent from separately: a **Buying Float** comes off the Cattle Budget alone, and takes nothing from what keeps the animals. _Avoid_: Purchase fund, capex

**Running Budget**: The part of a Venture's capital held back in the Venture Account for what the animals cost while they stand here: feed, medicine, vet, haat costs. Paid by the month, it fills as the **Monthly Sums** come in. Said to be low when what is left, less what its animals have eaten of the Farm's since the last **Reimbursement**, falls below the Owner's line — the Farm pays all month and is repaid after it, so the account alone always reads a month richer than it is; the figure held, and what an Investor's অগ্রগতি shows, stay what the account holds. What is left at the end goes back to Investors at settlement. _Avoid_: Working capital, float

**Floor**: The least capital a Venture is worth starting on. Reached by the decision date, it buys what the money allows; missed, every taka is refunded and the Venture is Cancelled. Not a **Tolerance**, which is how far a reading may be off. _Avoid_: Minimum, threshold

**Advance**: The Owner's own money put into a Venture whose Running Budget has run out: interest-free, recorded against that Venture, and repaid at cost before any capital returns — even where the Venture lost money, because money put in to feed somebody else's animals comes back before their capital does. Taken only while a Venture is running, since calling one off returns capital and only capital. It earns nothing and is never a charge. _Avoid_: Loan, credit, float

**Internal Sale**: The Owner's recorded sale of an Animal between the Farm's herd and a Venture, or between two Ventures — priced at her latest Weigh-in times a live-weight rate the Owner enters that day, with a note of where the rate came from, and the money moving through the Venture Account by bank. That Weigh-in must be within the Owner's days of the sale, a fortnight unless she moves it: an older one is named and refused, as an unweighed animal is — she has eaten since. Owner-only and audited, and impossible once the Venture is Selling or the Animal is Ready for Sale. She changes owner at the start of the day she is sold on — the day written on the sale, not the moment it was saved — for every sum that asks whose she was. Not a **Move**, which changes her Pen, and not a **Sale**, which is a buyer taking her away. _Avoid_: Transfer, reallocation, book entry

**Holding**: One Animal's time with one owner on one Side — the Farm's Dairy side, the Farm's Fattening side, or a **Venture** — from the day that owner took her on to the day she left them, or today. Taken on by **Intake**, by crossing from Dairy, or by **Internal Sale** in; left by **Sale**, by Internal Sale out, by crossing to Fattening, or by dying. What she cost that owner is her price on the way in and every charge dated inside the Holding; what came back is her Sale's or Internal Sale's price, or the price the Owner put on her crossing, or nothing for a death. The **Settlement**, the **Reimbursement**, **Return on Cost** and the **Investor Statement** all work a Holding this one way, each taking the kinds of charge its own definition names, so none can count a charge another leaves out. An Animal sold between owners has two Holdings, split at the start of the sale's day. **Margin** and **Cost of Gain** ask of her whoever owns her, so they are not Holdings. _Avoid_: Stint, tenure, ownership period, lot

**Settlement**: The close-out of a Venture: what its Animals fetched, less everything it was charged and the Owner's Advance, capital back to Investors, and the profit split by the percentages its Investment Agreements froze. Approved by the Owner as one act, which writes the figures down as they stood — a cost or a **Correction** landing afterwards moves what the costing says and moves nothing here, which is what a **Settlement Adjustment** is for. Before it is approved it is shown all the same, with whatever still makes it a guess said beside it, because an Owner told only "no" has nothing to go and put right. Impossible to approve while an Animal still stands, a price is missing, a Buying Float is unreconciled, a Venture's sale cash is still in a hand, a **Reimbursement** is owed, the Venture Account does not match the bank, or the account would be left a taka or more over — beyond what it still owes the Farm — or short once everybody is paid. Then each Investor is paid what his frozen share says, by bank with the reference it went on, and says so himself — recorded, so that "I never got it" has an answer that is not somebody's memory. The Owner's **Advance** comes back at cost with the rest, and the last of the money going out is what makes the Venture Settled. The Farm's own share of the profit leaves too, for the Farm's books, because the Farm's money never stays in a **Venture Account** whosever name the account is in — so a settled account reads nothing. A loss divides by the same percentages: the Investors' part comes off the capital they get back, and the Farm pays its part into the Venture Account from its own books, as money out, because until it does the payouts add up to more than the account holds. It reads nothing exactly: the paisa by which a monthly **Reimbursement** and a Settlement disagree, one rounding five parts and the other rounding once over the whole run, leaves with the Farm's share on the same line as the taka the per-Unit flooring left behind. A taka or more is not rounding and is not swept — that is the books disagreeing with the bank, and it is the Owner's to go and find. _Avoid_: Payout (one part of it), closing, liquidation

**Acknowledgement**: An Investor saying he had his payout, written down against it with the day and anything he said — so the file shows who has confirmed and who has not, rather than the Owner remembering. Recorded once: a second would write over the day he said it. Never a receipt the Farm issues, and never proof the money moved, which is the payout's own bank reference. _Avoid_: Confirmation, receipt, sign-off

**Settlement Adjustment**: A Correction or a late cost landing after a Settlement was approved. The Settlement's figures stand and money already paid is never chased; the Adjustment shows what each Investor's share would be now, and above the amount the Farm sets it means a supplementary payout or a waiver the Owner writes down and stands behind. Below that amount it is noted and nothing moves, because a hundred taka should not cost a trip to the bank. A share that fell is never collected back — an Investor paid on figures the farm gave him keeps what he was paid — so only news that leaves the Investors better off is ever left **outstanding**; anything else is **noted** and closed where it stands. An outstanding one is closed by being **paid** or **waived**. Each Adjustment says what the figures would be against what was frozen, not against the Adjustment before it, so a payout sends only what earlier ones have not. Not a **Correction**, which puts a record right, and not a Stock Count's adjustment. _Avoid_: Revision, true-up, restatement

**Unit**: One fixed-price share of a Venture. The Venture sets the price and how many there are; an Investor signs for whole Units, and capital, profit and loss all divide by the Units he holds — what he paid in, over the Unit price, a fraction where he paid part of one. Paid for all before buying, or its **Cattle Part** first and the rest in **Monthly Sums**, as the Venture opened. Fixed once the Venture starts Buying. _Avoid_: Share (the general word), stake, slot

**Cattle Part**: What one Unit of a Venture paid by the month pays before the buying starts: the Unit price in the proportion its Cattle Budget is of its capital, to the whole taka. Worked when the Venture opens and frozen on it. _Avoid_: Down payment, deposit, advance (an **Advance** is the Owner's own money)

**Monthly Sum**: One of the fixed sums a Unit of a Venture paid by the month pays after its Cattle Part: due on the 10th of each month after the month of its decision date and before its Target Window opens, the rest of the Unit price divided between them in whole taka, the last taking what the division left. Missed seven days after its 10th, and may still be paid until the selling starts; nothing is ever charged for its being late. Frozen when the Venture opens: an Amendment that moves the window moves no schedule. _Avoid_: Instalment (said of nothing here), EMI, kisti, monthly payment

**Purse**: Whose money a Money Event moved: the Farm's or one named Venture's. Carried on the Money Event itself, unsaid for the Farm's own — so everything recorded before Ventures existed is the Farm's, and every figure the farm already had is unchanged. The Farm's reports, the money list and the accountant export read the Farm's purse alone, so money that was never the Farm's never shows as its income or its cost — and what the Farm's animals are charged reads it alone too, because a Venture's cost belongs to that Venture's own animals. Money waiting for the Owner's approval is not a figure but work, and she is shown all of it, told whose it is. A wage is the Farm's whatever else is true: the Farm provides the labour, and one in any other purse is refused. _Avoid_: Account, fund, source

**Reimbursement**: The monthly transfer from a Venture Account to the Farm's, for what that Venture's animals cost of what the Farm paid for on behalf of the whole herd: feed, medicine, the Vet's fees for visits that named them, their share of the month's **Herd Costs**, and the **Selling Trips** that carried them to the haat. The Farm pays for everybody; the purse is put right once a month. Not a **Buying Trip** and not the Hasil — those came out of the Venture's own **Buying Float**, drawn before the lorry went and counted against it the same evening, so the Farm was never out of pocket for them. A Selling Trip is the other way about: it happens long after that Float is shut, the Farm pays the lorry and the men who went, and until it was paid back a Venture's account kept money the **Settlement** had already charged its Investors for. Charged by who owned each Animal the day she ate it or rode on it, itemised by Feed Item, medicine, Category and outing so the Owner can read it to an Investor, and taken once per Venture per month once that month is over — carrying, as its own lines, what any month already reimbursed has come to since, more or less, so a cost that lands late reaches the Farm with the next transfer; the last one before the **Settlement** catches whatever is left, and until then the Settlement counts it as owed. Not taken while anything its animals ate or were dosed with that month, or in a month it carries, has no price — the Settlement's own rule, a month at a time — because repaid then, it would be repaid at nothing. From the first of each month the Owner is told in the **Digest** which running Ventures owe the month just over, and how much — once for each Venture and month. Gross, not netted: the Farm's expense when it bought the feed stands, and this income stands beside it. _Avoid_: Recharge, settlement (which is a Venture ending), invoice

**Buying Float**: Cash drawn from the Venture Account for one Buying Trip and reconciled when it returns: the float out equals the Animals bought, plus the trip's costs, plus the cash brought back with its deposit slip. One per outing, ever. Every Animal on a funded outing belongs to the Venture that funded it — the lorry went on one purse's money. What the outing cost beyond the Animals is that Venture's money too, in its **Purse** as the Animals' prices are, though the outing is written up before its Float is drawn. **Open** until it is reconciled, and until then the Venture says so and a Settlement may not close over it; once reconciled the outing takes no further Animal and no change to what it cost, because the sum has been signed. The Farm's own outings have one too: cash the Owner hands the Manager out of their **Cash in Hand** for one outing — a **Handover** naming it — counted home by the Owner the same way against the Farm's animals it bought, their Hasil and its costs, the cash brought back going to the Owner's hand. One purse to an outing: a Venture's Float and the Farm's never go on the same one, and a Venture's animal on an outing goes on that Venture's Float, drawn before the lorry went. A Venture's animal with no outing — at the farm gate, from a neighbour — is paid from the Venture Account by bank, with its reference, and is the Owner's to take in; never cash. _Avoid_: Advance (which is the Owner's own money), petty cash, imprest

**Wind-up Period**: The days after a Venture's Target Window in which it keeps selling before the Farm buys whatever is left, so the Venture settles on time. That buy-back is its own act at a fixed moment, not the Owner's discretionary **Internal Sale**, which is refused once a Venture is Selling precisely so a finished bull cannot be lifted out of the pool. The difference is the moment: the buy-back happens because the clock says so and takes every Animal still standing, at one rate on one day, each priced at her own latest **Weigh-in** — and an Animal nobody has weighed is named and refused, because a price nobody can defend is worse than a delay; so is one whose latest Weigh-in is older than the Owner's days, a fortnight unless she moves it, with the day she was weighed. A Farm Parameter, 30 days by default. Not a **Grace**, which is how late an SOP Instance may run. _Avoid_: Extension, overtime, grace

**Arbitrator**: The person an Investor and the Owner name in the Investment Agreement to decide whether the Farm was negligent, and what that costs the Farm. Named at signing, before there is anything to argue about. _Avoid_: Mediator, judge, referee

**Investor**: A person whose money is in a **Venture**: known to the Owner personally or personally introduced, resident in Bangladesh, and one of at most twenty at any time, the Owner included. The Owner is an Investor for money they put in themselves, and the Farm for the work. Written down once — name, phone, address, NID number, the bank account they are paid into, and a nominee — and used for every Venture they join; the same name on the same phone is the same person, but two people may share a name. Put right by the Owner when what was written down changes, the Audit Event keeping what it said before. Retired rather than removed — everything they signed and were paid names them and is kept for the twelve years of **Retention** — and only once their money is in no Venture still running; a retired Investor is not signed for another Venture until the Owner brings them back. Not a **Counterparty**, who is paid for something rather than sharing what the Farm makes. _Avoid_: Shareholder, partner, funder, client

**Investor Cap**: The most Investors the Farm may have at once, counted as distinct people across every Venture that is not Settled or Cancelled, the Owner among them — twenty, because twenty in one business for gain is a company under the Companies Act. A Farm Parameter, with a second one beside it for the number at which the Farm starts warning. Neither may be overridden at the moment of signing. A **Request to Join**'s yes is not a signing: the Owner saying yes to somebody new is shown what signing them would make the count, the other yeses to new people counted in, and may still say it; the Investor is never told the count. _Avoid_: Limit, quota, max investors

**Nominee**: A person an Investor names to collect their capital and share from the Farm if they die before the Venture settles, and to hand it on to their lawful heirs. What a Nominee is paid is not theirs: naming them decides whom the Farm pays, never who inherits, which is the heirs' by law. An Investor names none, one, or up to three, each collecting a share of what is paid in whole percentages that add to a hundred — a share of the collecting, never of the inheritance; with none the money goes to the heirs. One who dies before the Investor leaves their share to the others in proportion to their own, and with none left it goes to the heirs as if none had been named. Written down with their relation to the Investor, a phone and their date of birth, so the paper shows who was a minor; one under eighteen collects through their **Receiver**. Recorded with the Investor, not with any one Agreement, because it is the person the family would come to the Farm about; the Investor's latest **Nomination** names them for every Agreement they hold, whatever the stamped paper of an earlier one says. _Avoid_: Beneficiary, heir, next of kin

**Receiver**: The person who collects a **Nominee**'s share while that Nominee is under eighteen, named with them on the Nomination with their relation to the Nominee and a phone, and signing for the minor's details being held. Usually a parent, never assumed to be. Not a Nominee: they hold no share and do not count among the three, and they stop collecting on the day the Nominee comes of age. _Avoid_: Guardian (a legal status the farm does not decide), trustee

**Nomination**: A short paper, signed and dated by an Investor in front of the Owner, naming every **Nominee** they have in full — never only the change — so any one of them reads alone. The latest one recorded is the list in force for all their Agreements; the record of their Nominees changes only when a new one is recorded, never by the Owner putting the record right, because the farm's answer to a family is that the Investor named these people themselves. Not stamped: it is a letter, not a contract. Printed from the farm's **Template** for it and kept, with its photo, beside their Agreements. An **Investment Agreement** is one too, for the Nominees its parties part names on the day it is signed, so a new Investor signs nothing more — and it prints again with those Nominees, whatever Nomination came after. A list the Investor never signed for, written down before Nominations were kept, is shown as not yet signed for until they sign one. _Avoid_: Nominee change form, nominee update

**Investment Agreement**: The stamped, signed mudarabah contract between the Owner and one Investor for one **Venture**: the capital, the percentages, the Units taken, the named **Arbitrator** and the **Target Window**, all frozen onto the paper at signing and moved afterwards only by an **Amendment**, signed afresh for each Venture. The Farm holds a photo of the stamped instrument with its stamp value, date and serial, and no capital is taken without it. Its stamp duty is paid on stamp paper or by e-challan, and the Agreement keeps which, with the stamp's serial or the challan's number. OpenFarm lays it out from the terms in the farm's **Template** for it before it is signed, to print, and the Agreement records the Version it was signed in; until a lawyer's approval is written on that Version the screen says so, never the paper. _Avoid_: Contract (the general word), deed, terms

**Amendment**: A dated paper moving what an **Investment Agreement** says, signed by every Investor in that **Venture** and photographed like the original. Only the split and the **Target Window** move; Units, the **Arbitrator** and the stamp are what a man signed and never change, because the cap, the capital already taken and every share worked out since all rest on them. One paper, so it is written against every Agreement on the Venture at once and a Venture is never half amended. The Agreement itself is never edited — the terms **in force** on a day are the latest Amendment signed on or before it, falling back to the paper as signed, which is what lets the Farm answer what a man had agreed to on the day a thing happened. Every paper that prints terms prints the ones in force and says which Amendment they came from, and a **Settlement** divides on them too, or the money would quietly disagree with the letter. Refused once a Settlement is approved: those figures are what everybody was already paid on. OpenFarm lays the paper out from the farm's **Template** for it, naming every Investor on the Venture, and each Amendment records the Version it was printed in. Not a **Correction**, which puts a wrong fact right — an Amendment records that the deal itself changed, and both versions are true of their own days.

**Template**: The farm's wording for one kind of paper an Investor signs — the **Investment Agreement**, the **Master Agreement**, the **Venture Schedule** and the **Amendment** — laid out in parts (the parties, with any lines printed under each Investor beside the nominee; the facts, numbered clauses, the stamp, the signatures), each in Bangla with the English beside it, with the paper's own facts asked for by name and filled when it is printed. One per kind, starting as OpenFarm's standard wording for the farm's lawyer to read — except that Agreements signed before the wording could be edited are recorded against the words then printed, as Version 1, with the standard after it. A farm already holding a Version takes a changed standard only when the Owner publishes it. The Owner alone changes it, by publishing its next Version; a Version is never rewritten, because every paper records the Version it was signed in and the farm must print it again as it was signed. A lawyer's approval is written onto one Version, once, with the lawyer's name and the day, and never carried to the next. The letterhead, who the parties are, the stamp's blanks and the closing lines promising no return are the farm's, not the wording's. Not the **SOP Definition**, which words work, not a paper. _Avoid_: Form, boilerplate, contract template

**Master Agreement**: One stamped mudarabah agreement between the Owner and one Investor, setting the terms every **Venture** they join shares; each Venture is then joined by a **Venture Schedule**. The shape the Owner asked the lawyer about so that each Venture needs no stamp paper of its own. Worded and printable as a **Template**; not in use: the lawyer's answer (2026-09-26) left the farm signing every Venture with its own **Investment Agreement**, and the Master Agreement returns only as a fresh effort. _Avoid_: Framework agreement, umbrella agreement

**Venture Schedule**: The short paper joining one Investor to one **Venture** under their **Master Agreement**: the Units, the capital, the split and the **Target Window**. Not in use, with the Master Agreement. _Avoid_: Annex, addendum (an **Amendment** is what changes a paper already signed)

**Venture Movement**: One movement of a Venture's own money through its **Venture Account**: capital arriving against an **Investment Agreement**, and the refund that sends it back when the Venture is called off — and, as their own work arrives, the **Buying Float**, a bull bought with no outing and paid from the account by bank, the **Advance**, the **Reimbursement**, what a buyer paid for one of its Animals, and the payout. Every one carries the day the bank moved it and the reference on the transfer, cheque or deposit slip — except a **Sale**'s paid at the haat, which carries the Animal's tag, because the money came off a buyer there and her tag is what the Owner has to go on; a Sale paid by bank carries the transfer's own reference, which is what the statement reads. A Sale's movement is written from the Sale — paid by bank, on the sale day; paid in cash, on the day it was deposited, carrying the slip — and moves when the Sale's price is put right, so it is never corrected on its own; a bull bought with no outing is written from her **Intake** the same way. Never a **Money Event**, which is the Farm's own income or expense. _Avoid_: Move (which is an Animal changing Pen), transaction, transfer

**Bank Check**: The monthly reading of a **Venture Account**'s or a **Farm Account**'s real balance off its own statement — the bank's, or bKash's — against what the farm believes it held at that month's end. A Farm Account's first reading has nothing to be held against: what it read is what the account held, and every month after is worked out from it — its Money Events, in less out, and the Handovers into and out of it; no month before it is read. One per account per month, put right rather than recorded twice. A month that did not agree stays recorded as not agreeing, and comes right only when the Owner says what she found out about it — a mistake caught in weeks is one somebody can still remember, and the same mistake at **Settlement** is a figure nobody can unpick with Investors waiting. A check also goes **stale** when the farm changes its mind about what that month ended on — a movement written or corrected into it moves that month and every month after it — and a stale month counts as still out until the statement is read again: agreeing with a figure nobody holds any more is not agreeing. A month that went stale and a month that disagreed are different problems, and the Venture says which is which: one needs the statement read again, the other needs explaining. A Settlement will not close while a Venture's month is still out; a Farm Account's month still out is named on the Owner's home until it agrees. _Avoid_: Reconciliation (which is what a **Buying Float** gets), audit, statement (the bank's own paper is what this reads, and an **Investor Statement** is a different thing entirely)

**Investor Statement**: One of the three papers an Investor ever receives, each for one Investor, one **Investment Agreement** and no other person's business: the **যোগদানপত্র** acknowledging his capital and setting out the terms he agreed to; the **অগ্রগতি**, sent while the Venture runs, with what the animals weigh and what the money has gone on; and the **হিসাব নিকাশ** at the end, showing proceeds, every charge, the split and his own payout with the reference it went on. They are the whole of what the Farm tells an Investor who has not been let into the **Investor Portal**, and what one who has reads there. Each is an **Export** with its Audit Event, the Owner's alone to produce, on the **Farm Identity** letterhead, and each carries the footer saying no return is guaranteed and a loss comes off capital. No projection is ever printed on one — weights, gains and days are facts, and a future price on a paper a man keeps reads as a promise. A settled Venture's **Return on Capital** is a fact too, printed on the হিসাব নিকাশ as a share over the Venture's own days beneath the payout it was worked from — but never as a rate a year, which on a run shorter than a year is a figure nobody earned. The Farm's own management share is the only other party's figure that appears, because an Investor shown his payout is owed the line it was worked out beside. Not the bank's statement, which is what a **Bank Check** reads a month against. _Avoid_: Certificate, report, investor update

**Investor Portal**: Where an Investor the Owner has invited reads the Ventures they are in, how each is doing today, and their own three **Investor Statements** — nothing taken, paid or signed through it (ADR 0007). Invited Investors may also see a Venture still gathering capital and make a **Request to Join** it, and are told how to pay for what they signed by bank. Such a Venture shows there only once the Owner has chosen to show it, and then to every invited Investor alike, whether or not their money is in a Venture yet, since the **Investor Cap** is held at signing and not at the door; the Owner may take it out of the portal again. Such Ventures have their own place in the portal's menu while there are any, come first on the home page of an Investor in no Venture yet and are one line at the top of everybody else's, and one the Owner has newly shown is said as new, once, until the Investor has looked — inside the portal, never by message. What it shows is the terms (the Unit price, target, Floor, decide-by day, Target Window, both budgets and the split the farm signs on today, which a mudarabah partner is owed before joining), the rules (every taka back if the Floor is missed, a loss off capital, nothing guaranteed, joining only by signing in person) and the Owner's own few words on it — never a past Venture's result, and never how many Units are left or who else has asked, because "only two left" is selling and the rest is other people's business; a price to come or a gain to expect only as a **Projection**, and only once the Owner shows them (ADR 0010). Either way the Agreement is still signed on stamped paper and the capital still arrives at the Venture Account (ADR 0008). By invitation, one person at a time: the Owner hands over a one-time code, and the Investor chooses their own password with their phone number and the code. A code good for a week that nobody took up has run out, and the Owner gives another; one given to somebody already in — a forgotten password — leaves them in meanwhile. The Owner's list says when each was last in, to the hour, and their page the papers they read. The Investor sees their own record back with the NID and bank account mostly hidden — put right by the Owner, never in the portal — chooses a new password and signs other devices out themselves, and is signed in for a working day at a time. They reach it at its own address, apart from the farm's, and it keeps nothing on their phone once they sign out (ADR 0009); every page says the portal is not a public offer, that joining is only by an Agreement signed in person, and that no money moves through it. Their account holds no Role and is not a **Membership**; the Owner takes it away, and the whole portal is closed until the Owner opens it — which is how the farm answers a lawyer who says it is a platform. The Owner's decision of 2026-09-24, approved as built by the lawyer's written opinion of 2026-09-26. _Avoid_: Investor login, investor app, dashboard

**Venture Plan**: What the Owner means a **Venture** to do, written when it opens: its buying in lines by weight band — so many animals bought between two weights, at a price a kilo, putting on so much a day, of one **Breed** or of any — the low and the high price a kilo it will sell at, and the share of its animals the Owner expects not to live to be sold. Worked out, it says the kilos bought and what they cost against the cattle budget (over it is said, never refused), what each head weighs by the **Target Window** and the herd's weight then. An animal it buys counts towards the line of her Breed that holds her weight, else towards an any-Breed line of her weight, else as bought outside the plan; one weight band may so carry two Breeds. The gain a line is offered is what the farm's Rations say of a crossbred bull that size, cut to the farm's deshi share for a deshi Breed, as she will be judged once bought. Every save is a version of its own, never an edit. The last one saved while the Venture was still Open is its baseline, what it is measured against; one saved after buying began is a revision and says why, and the baseline stays where it was. A plan first made after buying began is its own baseline. The Owner's alone, never a term of any **Investment Agreement**, and not shown in the **Investor Portal** (ADR 0011). _Avoid_: Budget (the Venture's two budgets are sums of money, not this), forecast, target (a **Target Window** is a date range)

**Projection**: What a **Venture**'s **Settlement** might come to at the low and the high of the sale prices a kilo of live weight the Owner expects — a range, said as "an estimate, not a promise", never one figure (ADR 0010). Worked, never typed. A running Venture's comes from:

- what each standing animal should weigh when the **Target Window** in force opens — as the latest **Amendment** set it — at her own rate over her whole stay, or her band's planned gain while nobody has weighed her;
- while it is still buying, what each band of its plan has still to buy, bought at the middle of the band at the band's price and grown at the band's gain — an animal taken across by an Internal Sale is bought too;
- what its sold animals fetched;
- what it has been charged, with the rest of its running budget taken as spent.

One still gathering capital comes from its plan alone: every band bought at its price, and its whole running budget charged. The low end sells fewer of the animals not yet sold, by the share the plan expects to die, while still charging what they cost; the high end is every one of them living. Every animal counted is paid for, past the cattle budget or short of it. Either way its profit divides as the Settlement's does, and a loss at the low price is said to come off capital. The sale prices and the buying are the latest version of the **Venture Plan**, not typed a second time (ADR 0011); Never a term of any **Investment Agreement**. An Investor is shown their own share and payout on a Venture they are in, and a Unit's on one offered to them — only once the Owner shows Projections, which stays off until the lawyer and the Shariah scholar have seen the wording; the Owner reads them in the Portal Preview meanwhile. Never printed on an **Investor Statement**. _Avoid_: Forecast, expected return, target return, ROI

**Portal Preview**: The Owner reading one Investor's **Investor Portal** as they would read it today — every page, their own record masked as they see it — from the Owner's own sign-in, marked as a preview on every page. The Owner's check before inviting somebody, and what the lawyer is shown; so it works for any Investor on file, invited or not, retired or not, with the portal open or shut. The Owner's alone. It never signs in as them or touches their sign-in, never makes, changes or withdraws a **Request to Join** for them — every act shows, and says only they can do it — and leaves nothing on their side: not the hour they were last in, not the papers they read. An **Investor Statement** opened in it is the Owner's own **Export**, noted as made in that Investor's preview. Looking leaves no Audit Event. _Avoid_: Impersonation, log in as, view as investor (the button says "See as they do")

**Welcome Letter**: The one printed page an Investor is handed with their first invitation to the **Investor Portal**: a letter in Bangla on the **Farm Identity** letterhead, addressed to them, saying what the portal shows and that nothing is signed or paid through it, the steps in, the one address they keep using with a QR code to it, the phone number they sign in with, whom to call, that the farm never asks for their password, and the standing notice in Bangla and English. Its **Code Slip** tears off the bottom. Printed only once their **Portal Consent** is signed and while the code is on the Owner's screen, since the farm cannot show it again; never printed a second time. An **Export**, which never holds the code. _Avoid_: Welcome sheet, invitation letter, onboarding pack

**Portal Consent**: An Investor's signed agreement, on paper and in front of the Owner, to the **Investor Portal** showing them their own record, to that record being kept on a server abroad, and to their NID, bank and nominee details being held for it — only what the portal adds, since holding the record to keep their Agreement rests on the Agreement itself. Signed when the **Welcome Letter** is handed over, kept by the farm with their Agreements, and recorded with the day and the wording they signed. No code is given before it; one withdrawn never counts again, so coming back means signing afresh. Withdrawing it takes their portal access away at once and nothing else: their Agreements and money records stay for as long as the law keeps the books. _Avoid_: Terms and conditions, opt-in, acknowledgement (nothing is ticked in the portal)

**Code Slip**: The tear-off strip carrying one Investor's one-time code and its last day, kept apart from the phone number so that either one alone opens nothing. Torn off a **Welcome Letter** the first time, and printed alone for every code after — one that ran out, or a forgotten password — for somebody who already has the letter. An **Export**, which never holds the code. _Avoid_: Code card, voucher, PIN slip

**Data Copy**: Everything the farm holds on one Investor, on one paper, «খামারে আপনার তথ্য»: the points of the privacy notice «আপনার তথ্য» first, then their record unmasked, their Agreements and the money they moved, the papers made for them, their **Requests to Join**, their portal access, consents and sign-ins, and every change to their record, access and consent. Made by the Owner from the Investor's page to answer their written request for a copy, which the farm answers within thirty days; never offered in the **Investor Portal**. An **Export**. _Avoid_: Data export, subject access report, dump

**Request to Join**: An invited Investor saying, through the **Investor Portal**, that they want to join one **Venture** still gathering capital: how many whole **Units**, with a note if they like, and when they said it. It binds nobody — the Investor need not sign and the Owner need not accept — and it holds no Units and moves no money; only an **Investment Agreement** does either. Units, not taka, because Units are what the Agreement freezes. One per Investor per Venture: until the Owner answers it the Investor may change the Units or withdraw it, and every change is kept with when it was made, so "I only asked for four" has an answer; a withdrawn one may be made again. Made, changed or answered yes only while the Venture is Open, shown and short of its decide-by day, and only by an Investor who is not retired — a retired one reads their old papers and is offered nothing. One nobody answered closes by itself, kept, when the Venture stops gathering capital or leaves the portal; a yes survives the Venture leaving the portal, because the Owner promised a signing, and closes only when it stops gathering capital. Retiring an Investor closes theirs, answered or not, since a retired Investor is not signed; taking their portal access away closes nothing, because the Owner may still sign them by phone. Heard by the Owner alone, as one Notice carried by the **Digest** — work waiting for them, like money waiting for approval, and not a **Needs Review**, which is the system unable to settle something and the Manager's — that follows it through its changes and closes itself if it is withdrawn first. The Owner answers **come and sign**, for the Units asked or fewer, or **not this time**, with a line to the Investor if they like. A yes holds no Units, but the Owner may not say yes to more than are left once the signed Agreements and the yeses still waiting to be signed are counted, so three yeses never promise fourteen Units out of ten. Signing is as it always was, in person on stamped paper; the Agreement recorded names the Request it answers, which then reads **signed**, and where the two disagree on Units the paper is right. _Avoid_: Interest, expression of interest (read as _riba_ on a mudarabah farm), subscription, application, commitment, pledge

**Venture Account**: The one bank account, in the Owner's name, that Investors' money moves through: capital in, Venture buying out, what its Animals fetched back in, payouts out. The Farm's own money never passes through it. Every movement is by bank transfer, cheque or deposit slip, never cash: a Venture's animal sold for cash reaches it only when that cash is deposited, with its slip, from the hand that held it. Its bank, branch, name, number and routing number are written on the Venture by the Owner alone, and an Investor is shown them only for capital they have already signed for, with what is still owed, the **Pay-in Code** and the decide-by day, and the warning that the farm will never ask them to pay anywhere else — never beside a Venture still gathering capital, where they would read as "pay here to join". _Avoid_: Escrow (nobody holds it in trust), wallet, fund

**Pay-in Code**: The short code OpenFarm gives one **Investment Agreement** when it is recorded, for the Investor to write on the transfer that sends its capital, so that money arriving in the **Venture Account** says whose it is. Not the reference a **Venture Movement** carries, which is whatever the bank printed and what the Owner still records. _Avoid_: Reference (the bank's), payment ID, invoice number

**Counterparty**: A person or business the Farm buys from, sells to, or pays: name, address, phone. Recorded once per name and shared across Sale, Dispatch, Intake, Purchase and Money Events — the trader who sells the Farm a bull is often the man who buys one back at Eid. Called the **seller** on an Intake and the **buyer** on a Sale, which is the side he stands on rather than a second kind of record. _Avoid_: Customer, vendor, contact, party

**Category**: The farm-defined heading a Money Event falls under (milk sales, feed, medicine, wages, shed rent, utilities…). Used for reports. Every farm starts with the standard ones; the farm adds its own, and retires one rather than removing it. A Category a record books under — milk sales from a Dispatch, say — is not retired, nor entered by hand, which would be the same money twice; the one exception is a vet's fee, which a visiting vet with no login is paid all the same. Wages are not retired either: the one-wage-a-month rule is kept by them. The Owner marks a Category, once, as charged to the animals of its Side or not; that mark, never the entry, is what makes a Money Event a **Herd Cost**, and changing it is the Owner's. The Owner may mark one as paid every month, too, which makes it a **Monthly Cost**. _Avoid_: Account, head, GL code

**Monthly Cost**: A Category of money going out that the Owner has marked as paid every month — shed rent, electricity — so that a month with nothing entered under it is named, rather than read as a cheaper month. From a Farm Parameter's day of the month (the 10th by default, the Owner's to set) the Manager and the Owner are shown each Monthly Cost with nothing entered under it that month in the Farm's purse, waiting for approval or not, and each one still empty for the month before, which stays until something is entered or the month after that is over. Named from the month the mark was put on, never for the months before it. A wage is watched the same way but by the person, since Wages is one Category over many people: anybody paid a wage for one month and none yet for the month after is named once that later month is over and its next month has reached the same day, and is named only the once — someone who has left drops off on their own. Somebody's name on it is a thing to enter, never money owed, and nothing is entered for them: rent written in before anybody paid it would be a lie in the books. _Avoid_: Recurring payment, standing order, bill, due

**Margin**: What a fattening Animal made: her Sale price less her purchase price, the feed charged to her, her doses, her share of the Vet Fees for visits that named her, her Hasil and her shares of the Buying Trip that brought her and the Selling Trips that took her, and her share of the Herd Costs. The same sum for every Animal, whoever owns her. Worked out, never stored, and only once she is sold. Not a **Tolerance**, which is how far a reading may be off. _Avoid_: Profit, return

**Return on Cost**: What a **Season** or a **Venture**'s cattle, or one dairy Animal over her whole stay, made for every hundred taka they cost, said as a fact: «প্রতি ১০০ টাকা খরচে ২৮ টাকা লাভ». Worked as a **Settlement** is — what the Animals fetched, an **Internal Sale** out to a Venture among it, less what they cost to take on and everything charged to them while they were its own — over that cost. An Animal who died is in it, with her cost and nothing back. One bought at a haat is taken on at her price, one bought from a Venture at the Internal Sale's, and one bred here or crossed from Dairy at her weight on the day she crossed times a rate a kilo the Owner enters, with where the rate came from: until the Owner has, the Season is not yet a result. Whose money paid a charge does not move it, so an **Advance** is no part of it. Wages, sheds, equipment, dung and what the money would have earned elsewhere are not in it, and every figure says so, because a published study charges some of them and reads lower on the same Animals. A loss is the same sum below nothing. Put a year, it is scaled simply — never compounded — over the money it tied up, each taka from the day it was spent to the day its Animal was sold, and said after the share and those days as that share scaled to a year, «বছরের হিসাবে»; a loss is scaled the same. Money tied up for fewer than a Farm Parameter's days on average (60 by default, the Owner's to set) is not put a year at all. While a Season or a Venture is still going, an Animal not yet gone counts at what she is worth today — her latest weight times the low and the high price a kilo the Owner has set for the Farm's own, or her **Venture Plan**'s — so the figure is a range, said «আজকের দামে» as an estimate and not a result, its part already sold apart from its part still standing, and never put a year until the last has gone; one who cannot yet be valued is left out whole and named. A dairy Animal is her own: one bred here from the day she is born, at nothing, her calf and heifer years' keep the money in; one bought, or here before the farm kept its books, at a price the Owner enters, with a note. What comes back is her milk sent to Bulk at what each month's **Dispatches** fetched, and what she went for at the end — a Sale, or the price she crossed to Fattening at. Every calf is her own and not her dam's; her dam's calves are shown beside her. Until she has gone she is worth her kind's **Head Price**. Worked out, never stored; the Owner's. _Avoid_: ROI, yield, profit rate, মুনাফার হার, annual return

**Head Price**: The low and the high price a head the Owner sets for each kind of dairy Animal — calf, heifer, pregnant heifer, cow in milk, dry cow — because a cow is sold by the head, not the kilo. What a dairy Animal still here counts at in her **Return on Cost**, as a range said «আজকের দামে». The Owner's alone. _Avoid_: Valuation, market price (which is a fattening Animal's, a kilo)

**Return on Capital**: What the Investors in a **Venture** made for every hundred taka of capital they put in: their share of its profit over their capital — all of it, the part that waited unspent in the **Venture Account** too — after the Farm's share. An **Advance** is not capital. The Farm's share has none, because the Farm puts in work and no money; it is read in taka, and a part of it the Owner gives up to the Investors is a gift from that share and not their return. Put a year as a **Return on Cost** is, each taka of capital from the day it reached the Venture Account to the day it was paid back, the days it waited unspent among them. An Investor reads their own only once the Settlement is approved, for each Venture on its own, as the share over its days and never put a year (ADR 0012). _Avoid_: ROI, yield, interest, profit rate, মুনাফার হার

**Bank Rate**: A rate a year the Owner types, with a note of what it is — an Islamic bank's mudaraba rate, an FDR's — as the bank quotes it, before its tax, to set beside what the Farm's money made in cattle. Kept from the day the Owner says it held from — the day it is typed unless an earlier one is named, never a day still to come — until the next, the old ones kept; a rate put right is typed again from the same day. A **Season** or a **Venture**'s cattle are read against the one in force on the day their first taka went in, and the Investors' **Return on Capital** against the one in force on the day their capital first arrived, as a deposit would have locked it. Shown only beside a finished **Return on Cost** or **Return on Capital** put a year, as a plain line with its note, never as the cattle beating it; and the Owner's alone, never an Investor's, because a past return is no basis for comparing investments and the schemes that collapsed sold themselves on beating the bank. _Avoid_: Benchmark, hurdle rate, interest rate

**Cost per Litre**: What a litre of milk cost the farm: everything charged to a cow in her current Lactation — her feed, her doses, the Vet Fees for visits that named her, and her share of whatever is charged by the head in it — over the litres she sent to Bulk in it — or, for the Dairy side, everything charged to its animals in a period over the litres sent to Bulk in it. Worked out, never stored. _Avoid_: Production cost, unit cost

**Buying Trip**: One outing to buy cattle, with what it cost beyond the animals' prices: broker, lorry to the farm, the men's food and lodging. Split evenly across the Animals brought in on it; the Hasil is not, because a haat takes it per Animal. _Avoid_: Purchase run, procurement

**Selling Trip**: One outing to sell cattle at a haat, with what it cost: lorry both ways, stall or space, the men's food and lodging. Split evenly across every Animal taken, sold or brought home again, because a bull that came back still stood on the lorry. A broker's fee for one sale is typed on that Sale. _Avoid_: Market trip, Load (one vehicle to one destination)

**Hasil**: The toll a haat takes on one Animal bought there, typed per Animal as the haat's slip gives it. Charged to her alone. _Avoid_: Market fee, tax, levy

**Herd Cost**: A hand-entered Money Event that names a Side and whose Category the Owner has marked as charged to the animals of its Side, such as a Vet visit that named no animal, lab tests or fly spray. Split across the Animals of that Side by the days each stood on the farm in its month, each Animal's part dated inside her own days of it — never after she left — and split again between her owners by the days each held her, where an **Internal Sale** fell in the month. One under a marked Category that names no Side reaches no animal, and is an **Overhead**. Shed hygiene, wages, shed rent, utilities, repairs and equipment are never Herd Costs: they are the place and the people, and the Farm's. _Avoid_: Overhead (which is exactly what it is not), shared cost

**Overhead**: What running the place cost in a period: every Money Event going out of the Farm's purse entered by hand that is not a **Herd Cost** — wages, shed rent, utilities, repairs, shed hygiene, equipment, the farm's own Categories left unmarked, and money under a marked one that named no Side and so reached no animal. Added up by Category, and said as what it comes to **per head per day** over every day every Animal stood on the farm in the period, the Farm's and the Ventures' alike, because the same people and sheds keep them all. A figure the Owner reads beside the animals' own costs, never part of them: no **Margin**, **Cost of Gain**, **Cost per Litre**, **Return on Cost**, **Settlement** or **Reimbursement** carries it, and a Venture is never charged it — the Farm's share of a Venture's profit is what pays for it. _Avoid_: Fixed cost, indirect cost, Herd Cost (which the animals carry)

**Cost of Gain**: What each kilogram a fattening Animal put on cost: everything charged to her, over the weight she gained between arriving and her latest Weigh-in or her Sale. Her Cost of Gain **now** asks the same of the kilos she is putting on these days: her keep over a Farm Parameter's days (four weeks by default, a fortnight at the fewest; the Owner's to set) — her feed, her doses, her share of the Vet Fees for visits that named her and her share of the Herd Costs, not the Hasil or a Trip, which moved her rather than kept her — a day, over her **Average Daily Gain** between her last two Weigh-ins — or since she came, where those two are closer together than a Farm Parameter's days (a week by default, the Owner's to set), since a full gut moves a bull more in three days than he grows. Set beside the price a kilo she is priced at, it says whether keeping her the days ahead pays — a Farm Parameter's days (a fortnight by default, the Owner's to set), which size what those days come to and never the answer: at or under the low price it does, over the high price — or while she puts nothing on — she costs more to keep than she gains, and between the two what she fetches decides. What she has cost already is spent whichever way, so it has no say in this; that is her **Margin**'s question. The Owner's alone, as an animal's money is, and a figure for the Owner to weigh, never a **Suggestion**: the Manager's Suggestion says nothing of money. Not said at all, and why, for an animal on the farm fewer than a Farm Parameter's days (a week by default, the Owner's to set, and never more than the days her keep is read over), one with no Feeding charged to her in those days, or one with no rate. _Avoid_: Feed conversion (that is kg of feed, not taka), marginal cost

**Approval Threshold**: The BDT amount above which a Money Event the Owner did not enter waits, unapproved, for the Owner's approval. Only the money waits: the record that made it — the milk gone, the bull bought — is never held back. An approval is of what the Owner read — the amount, who it went to or came from, and its Category — so a Correction that changes any of those asks again. A bill in pieces is one bill: money entered by hand, by anybody but the Owner, that is under the line alone waits all the same when it and what the same person was paid by hand the same way in the week up to it — its farm day and the six before — come to more than the line; the pieces before it stand as they were. _Avoid_: Limit, sign-off amount

**Entered Twice**: Money entered by hand that looks like money already entered — the same person, whatever the capitals, the same taka, the same farm day. It is not kept until whoever entered it has seen the earlier one and said it really is a second; kept so, by anybody but the Owner, it is told to the Owner in the evening's post. A wage is never asked: one wage a person a month is its own rule. _Avoid_: Duplicate (it may be two real bills), double entry

## Access

**Role**: One of Owner, Manager, Staff, Vet. A person may hold several; every recorded action names the Role it was done under. _Avoid_: Permission level, user type, group

**Membership**: What makes somebody one of the Farm's people: the Roles they hold, whether they still work here, the Pens they keep and the PIN they switch in with. It begins with an invitation taken up and ends when the Owner disables them — turned away at sign-in, signed out everywhere, while every record they wrote stays exactly where it is. The Farm is never left without an Owner, and nobody ends their own Membership. Not their **Account**, which is how they sign in at all. _Avoid_: Account (that is the sign-in), user record, permissions (what a Role may do), access

**Account**: How somebody signs in: their email, the password only they know, and wherever they are signed in. The Farm never sets a password for anybody — one somebody else has seen is one that signs work in their name — so somebody who has forgotten theirs is handed a one-time code, read out in person, and chooses their own. Whoever runs the Farm can see where a person is signed in and turn one of those out: a phone left in a yard is the Farm's problem. An Account is not a **Membership**: ending the Membership shuts the Account's door on this Farm, and the Account is what the door is on. Not a **Farm Account**, which is a bKash number or bank account of the Farm's. _Avoid_: Login, credentials, profile

**Pen Assignment**: The Pens a Staff member is responsible for. Defines what they see and may record as Barn Staff — their **Scope** under that Role. _Avoid_: Area, zone, allocation

**Visiting Scope**: A Vet account limited to animals with an open case they are on, granted per visit and time-limited. It is the **Scope** of their work as the Vet. _Avoid_: Guest access, temporary account

**Scope**: What a person may see and record acting under one Role: the whole farm for the Owner, the Manager and a Vet; their Pen Assignment for Barn Staff; their Visiting Scope for a Vet called in for a visit; and both together for Barn Staff who are also visiting. Barn Staff may still look up any animal by her Tag Number, read-only; a visitor may not. Worked out for each piece of work from the Role it is done under, never from the highest Role they hold. _Avoid_: Permissions (what a Role may do), access level, visibility

## Offline

**Entry**: One thing a person recorded that the farm takes the same way however it arrives — at once, or held in an Outbox and sent in a Batch: a claim, a Step Completion, a Step photo, a finish, a Move, an Observation. Recorded under the Role it was done in and dated when it was done, with when the farm received it kept beside; when the world has moved since — the animal has left, someone else took the work — it is kept for a person rather than refused. _Avoid_: Write, mutation, record (a record is what an Entry leaves in the farm's books)

**Batch**: One send from an Outbox: the entries a phone has been holding, with one key for the lot, applied with their Audit Events in a single transaction. Frozen when it is formed — the entries are written down exactly as they will be sent, proof of who recorded them included — so every attempt under its key carries the same thing, whatever the phone learns in between. _Avoid_: Sync, upload, push

**Waiting for a PIN**: A frozen Batch cannot be formed while a tab on the phone still holds a PIN entered offline: the work is that person's, and until the farm has seen the PIN there is nothing to prove it with. The Batch waits and is offered again — waiting is not a failed attempt, and no work is ever handed back for it. A PIN the farm refused, or one no tab holds any more, is not waited for: the entry goes unproved, and the farm keeps it for a person like any Entry it cannot take. _Avoid_: Blocked, stalled, retrying

**Idempotency Key**: The client's own name for a Batch. The same key arriving again is answered from what was stored, never applied twice; the same key carrying different entries is refused. _Avoid_: Request id, transaction id, nonce

**Sequence Number**: The position an entry has in its own phone's Outbox. Gaps in it are entries the farm has never read — worth saying out loud, never a reason to refuse what did arrive. _Avoid_: Index, offset, counter

**Outbox**: The durable on-device queue of entries made without signal, sent in order when signal returns. Never emptied without the server's acknowledgement. _Avoid_: Cache, buffer, pending list (that's what the user _sees_)

**Needs Review**: Something the system accepted but could not settle on its own, waiting for a person: an entry the server took although the world had changed since it was recorded, or a Correction whose effects it cannot walk back. Raised by the system, resolved by the Manager with their judgement recorded; never discarded. _Avoid_: Conflict (a Correction made against a record changed since is refused, not kept), rejected, error

## Audit

**Restore Drill**: The quarterly rehearsal of losing the farm's database: a backup restored into a scratch environment and checked by the Manager in the app. Recorded whether or not it went well. _Avoid_: Test, DR test, failover

**Audit Event**: The append-only record of one state change: who (and in which Role), from which device, when by both clocks, what changed. Written in the same transaction as the change. _Avoid_: Log entry, history row, activity

**Correction**: Putting a wrong fact right, with a reason: the record takes its right value, and an Audit Event written in the same transaction keeps what it said before and supersedes the event that wrote it, so the original stays visible in the trail. An Observation is the exception — withdrawn and a new one written beside it, because the health chain shows both. The only way a fact ever changes. Not an **Amendment**, which is the deal itself changing by agreement rather than a wrong fact being put right. _Avoid_: Edit, update, delete

**Correction Window**: How long after an entry a given Role may still correct it: Staff 2 hours on their own entries, Manager 30 days on any, Owner always, Vet always on their own health entries. Measured by the farm's clock from when the entry reached it. A fact that was never an entry — Expected Calving, a person's name — has no window, only the Roles that may correct it. _Avoid_: Grace period (that's for SOP due times), edit window

## Compliance

**Farm Identity**: What the Farm is, as every document leaving it prints: name, address, phone and Registration number. Written down once by the Owner or the Manager; not a Farm Parameter, which is a number to tune. _Avoid_: Farm details, farm profile, company information

**Registration**: The Farm's DLS registration: number, office, issue and expiry dates, certificate photo. Renewed annually by 31 March; the renewal SOP is raised for the Owner the renewal lead (90 days) before, falls due on the day it runs out, and its closing Step records the new expiry and the renewed certificate. _Avoid_: Licence, permit

**Inspector View**: The single screen the Manager shows a DLS inspector: Registration, herd summary (the animals on the farm by Side and State and by Pen), vaccination register, 30-day treatment register, 6-month disease history, mortality — each printed as a paper and handed over, with the movement log (every Move, Intake, Sale and death in a period) as a CSV; the inspector never touches the phone. _Avoid_: Audit page, compliance dashboard

**Register**: One of the records the Farm keeps for an inspector over a period: the vaccination register (a year), the treatment register (thirty days), the disease history (six months), the mortality register (a year) and the movement log (a year). Each says once how far back it looks unless asked, how it is read, what it is called and what its columns hold — and from that comes the paper an inspector is handed, the CSV they take away, the rows the Inspector View lists and the Export that records each. What a person reads off one on the screen is the screen's own to decide. A Register is not the herd summary or the Registration record, which are what the Farm is today rather than a period of it. _Avoid_: Report (a Report is what a screen shows), log (only the movement log is one), compliance record

**Animal Passport**: The per-animal paper: identity, Pen history, treatments with their withdrawals, weigh-ins, and how she left if she has. Given to a buyer or slaughter vet on request — including after she has gone, which is when they ask. _Avoid_: Animal record (that's the live data), certificate

**Withdrawal Summary**: The one-page answer to the question a buyer actually asks: what an Animal has had in the last thirty days, and whether her meat is clear today. Read from the same Withdrawal record the Sale is gated on, so the paper and the gate cannot disagree. _Avoid_: Health certificate (the farm certifies nothing), clearance

**Retention**: How long the farm keeps what it has written down. **Twelve years** for everything a **Venture** is made of — its **Investment Agreements** and their **Amendments**, every **Venture Movement**, its **Settlement** and every payout and **Settlement Adjustment** under it, and the **Investor Statements** it produced. Twelve because it is the longest period that applies, a company's books under the Companies Act 1994 s.181(5), and it swallows VAT's five years and the six assessment years income tax may reach back through. An Investor disputing a payout years later has to meet the evidence rather than a gap. Nothing is purged and no record of a Venture is deleted; where one is, it is named in `venture-records-are-kept.test.ts` and had to be argued for first. Not the same as an **Audit Event**, which is how a change is remembered — retention is how long the thing itself stays. _Avoid_: Archival, purge, expiry

**Export**: Any report or document the system generates for someone outside it. Always an Audit Event; always stamped with farm, Registration number, time and user. _Avoid_: Download, print-out, report (a Report is what it shows; an Export is the act)

## Notifications

**DLS Report**: The letter to the Upazila Livestock Officer about one notifiable Diagnosis, and the record of it going: when it was delivered and the reference the office filed it under. A report that was sent and cannot be evidenced is a report that was not sent. _Avoid_: Notification (the general word), filing, submission

**Notice**: One thing the Farm tells a person — that work is late, that a hold ends tomorrow, that money is waiting for the Owner. It carries the facts its words need, snapshotted as they were when it was raised, and it sits in that person's list whether or not it ever reached their phone. Every Notice is one of three kinds: an **Alert**, which goes now; one the **Digest** carries; or a **Needs Review**, which is not finished until a person has decided. Each kind says what facts it carries and who on the Farm hears it. _Avoid_: Notification (the general word — this is it, named), message, event

**Alert**: An immediate notification for something that costs money or breaks a legal deadline if missed: overdue work, withdrawal ending, a notifiable diagnosis, an entry the farm would not take. Ignores quiet hours. What makes one an Alert is that it goes _now_ — the farm's in-app list holds these and the quieter notices side by side, and a notice that waits for the Digest is not an Alert however it is stored. Two of them also go by SMS, to the Owner and the Manager: a Withdrawal ending and a notifiable Diagnosis, because those two cost money or break a deadline and a push that does not arrive has cost nobody anything. The farm's delivery table is the one place that decides any of this. _Avoid_: Notification (the general word), warning, reminder

**Digest**: The batched notification carrying everything that is not an Alert, at the times the farm sets (06:00 and 18:00 by default). It names what is in it. Everything it carries is in the app whether or not it is delivered. _Avoid_: Summary, newsletter, report

**Quiet Hours**: The span the farm is asleep (22:00–05:00 by default), when nothing that can wait reaches a phone. A Digest due inside them waits for the farm to wake; an Alert goes anyway. The in-app list is never quietened — quiet hours quieten the phone, not the farm's own record of what is waiting. _Avoid_: Do not disturb, night mode, silence

**Push**: An Alert delivered to an installed app on a device that agreed to be told, so it arrives with the app closed. The in-app Alert is the record; Push is the tap on the shoulder, and may fail without anything being lost. _Avoid_: Notification (the general word), alert (the thing being delivered), message

**Escalation**: The single extra rung: an overdue instance still open after the escalation window (default 2 hours) also notifies the Owner. _Avoid_: Chain, tiering

## Devices & onboarding

**Shed Phone**: A farm-provided Android phone kept in a Shed, holding a device session. Staff record on it after a PIN Switch. _Avoid_: Barn tablet, kiosk, terminal

**PIN Switch**: A Staff member making themselves the active user on a Shed Phone with their 4-digit PIN. Works offline; every entry is attributed to the active user, never to the phone. _Avoid_: Login (that's the device session), shared account

**Switch Token**: What the farm gives a person for one stint on a Shed Phone when it has seen their PIN, and what an Entry recorded during that stint carries to prove whose work it is. Kept only until the Entry is sent; never written into a record anybody can read, because a proof anybody can read is a proof anybody can use. _Avoid_: Session token (that's the device's), password, key

**Coach Overlay**: The one-time in-app hint shown the first time a person meets a screen type. Dismissable; re-openable from help. _Avoid_: Tutorial, tour, onboarding flow

**SOP Card**: The one-page Bangla print/in-app sheet generated from a published SOP Version: name, steps, icons, evidence. The training material. _Avoid_: Manual, guide, cheat-sheet

**Trained On**: The record that one person was taught one SOP Version, on a day, by somebody. Append-only: a new Version does not untrain anybody and training on it does not erase what they were taught before, because "who knew which procedure" is a question about a date that has already passed. _Avoid_: Certified, signed off (that is the Manager checking work), competent
